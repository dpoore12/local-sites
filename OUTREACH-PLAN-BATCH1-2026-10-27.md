## The math

- 128 emails is about 2 days at 50–100 a day.
- Cold email to small trade owners typically gets 1–3% replies, and fewer of those are a yes. So 128 emails probably gets 1–3 interested owners, not 5.
- Getting to 5 likely needs about 500–1,000 owners. They can come from three places:
  1. Phone calls to the 336 that have no email.
  2. Apollo's company search for the same trades within 30 miles of each town.
  3. Pre-selling the plumber and pest towns from `TRADES-AND-TOWNS-2026-10-26.md`. Kyle says to sell before you rank, and each of those towns has 50–100 plumbers.

## Sending setup (costs money, needs Dan's yes)

Sending 50–100 cold emails a day from dan@fixlypro1.com would get that domain flagged as spam. The standard setup:

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| 2–3 look-alike sending domains (e.g. fixlypro-leads.com) | about $10 each, once |
| 2 mailboxes per domain (Google Workspace or Microsoft 365) | about $6–7 per mailbox per month |
| A sending and warm-up tool (Instantly or Smartlead), or Apollo's built-in sequences, which we already pay for | $0–40 a month |
| Warm-up before full volume | 2–3 weeks, about 30 a day per mailbox after |
| **First month** | **about $60–110** |

**Option to start now for $0:** Dan sends 15–25 personal emails a day by hand from his real inbox to the highest-priority owners while the other mailboxes warm up. At low volume and written personally, this is safe.

The law (CAN-SPAM) requires a real postal address and a way to opt out in every email.
