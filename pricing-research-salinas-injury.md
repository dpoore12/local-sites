## Notes and limits

- California Rule of Professional Conduct 1.5 contains **no** percentage ceiling for
  auto injury work. It is an unconscionability standard with 13 listed factors. The page
  says that, and does not invent a cap.
- § 6146's 25%/33% ceilings are **medical malpractice only** and are labeled that way on
  the page and in the table row.
- Monterey County's newest civil fee schedule available on the court's own site is the
  one effective Jan. 1, 2024; the statewide amounts it lists ($435 / $370 / $225 / $150 /
  $60 / $500) are unchanged in the statewide schedule effective Jan. 1, 2026
  (https://courts.ca.gov/system/files/file/statewide-civil-fee-schedule-eff-01012026.pdf,
  retrieved 2026-08-23).
- Could not verify: any Monterey-County-specific local civil surcharge. The statewide
  schedule lists local courthouse-construction surcharges only for Riverside,
  San Bernardino and San Francisco, so Monterey filers pay the uniform amounts.
- leginfo blocks Welf. & Inst. Code § 14124.78 (Medi-Cal lien math) to automated
  retrieval, so no Medi-Cal figure is used on this site.
