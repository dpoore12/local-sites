## Three sourced local facts

1. **SR-1 reporting is separate from a traffic report.** California DMV requires an SR-1 within 10 days when anyone is injured or killed, or property damage exceeds $1,000; DMV explicitly says this is in addition to a report to police, CHP, or an insurer. That makes the separate DMV deadline a practical Modesto crash-file task. Source: [California DMV — Report of Traffic Accident Occurring in California (SR-1)](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/dmv-virtual-office/accident-reporting/).

2. **The former 15/30/5 minimum is no longer the current standard-policy floor.** As California standard policies came up for renewal beginning January 1, 2025, minimum liability limits increased from $15,000/$30,000/$5,000 to $30,000/$60,000/$15,000. The site explains why the policy and renewal date matter instead of presenting the old shorthand as current law. Source: [California Department of Insurance — New Year Means New Changes for Insurance](https://www.insurance.ca.gov/0400-news/0102-alerts/2025/New-Year-Means-New-Changes-for-Insurance.cfm).

3. **Stanislaus County’s 2023 traffic-injury data supports an early-evidence focus.** California OTS recorded 4,679 people killed or injured in county traffic collisions, including 389 fatal-and-injury hit-and-run collisions. The copy uses this as context for preserving reports, cameras, witness details, and policies—not as a prediction about any individual matter. Source: [California Office of Traffic Safety — Stanislaus County 2023](https://www.ots.ca.gov/rankings/stanislaus-county-2023/).

Additional sourced reporting detail used in the copy: CHP says a proper party of interest can request a crash report through the Crash Portal or with CHP 190, and CHP’s Modesto Area states it patrols all freeways, state routes, roadways, and unincorporated areas in Stanislaus County. Sources: [CHP — Request a Crash Report](https://www.chp.ca.gov/traffic/request-a-crash-report/) and [CHP — Modesto Area](https://www.chp.ca.gov/find-an-office/central-division/465-modesto/).
