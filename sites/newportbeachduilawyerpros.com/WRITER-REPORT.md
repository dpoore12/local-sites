# Newport Beach DUI local research — 2026-08-22

1. **Harbor Justice Center venue.** Orange County Superior Court states that the Harbor Justice Center – Newport Beach handles traffic, minor offenses, and criminal matters, with criminal and traffic mailing address at 4601 Jamboree Road, Newport Beach, CA 92660-2595. This makes local court-date and department confirmation a concrete part of preparing a Newport Beach criminal case.
   - https://www.occourts.org/location/harbor-justice-center-newport-beach

2. **Boating under the influence.** California Harbors and Navigation Code section 655 prohibits operating a vessel while under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or both, and separately prohibits operating a recreational vessel at 0.08% or more blood alcohol concentration. This makes a Newport Harbor allegation a vessel-law matter, not merely a roadway DUI on water.
   - https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=HNC&division=3.&title=&part=&chapter=5.&article=1.

3. **Newport Harbor law-enforcement service.** The City of Newport Beach says the Orange County Sheriff’s Department Harbor Patrol-Marine Operations Bureau manages the emergency and law-enforcement services of Newport Harbor. The relevant responding-agency reports and the precise harbor location can therefore be material evidence in a boating-under-the-influence allegation.
   - https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/harbor/contact-us/sheriff-s-harbor-patrol
