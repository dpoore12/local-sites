# Concord car accident law site — research findings

Verified 2026-08-22.

1. **North Carolina contributory negligence.** The North Carolina Supreme Court explained in *Miller v. Miller* that a plaintiff's negligence joining the defendant's negligence can bar recovery even where the plaintiff's contribution was comparatively small. This supports the site's emphasis on preserving evidence and avoiding casual fault admissions.
   - https://law.justia.com/cases/north-carolina/supreme-court/1968/278-0.html

2. **Cabarrus County court structure.** The North Carolina Judicial Branch lists Cabarrus County in Superior Court District 25 and District Court District 25. Its courthouse page gives the address as 61 Union Street South, Concord, NC 28025. This supports the explanation that a civil case, if one becomes necessary, has a local filing context.
   - https://www.nccourts.gov/locations/cabarrus-county
   - https://www.nccourts.gov/locations/cabarrus-county/cabarrus-county-courthouse

3. **I-85 corridor and interchanges.** NCDOT's I-85 Cabarrus project materials describe an approximately 6.8-mile, eight-lane divided facility from south of Bruton Smith Boulevard/Concord Mills Boulevard to north of N.C. 73, with interchange modifications. This supports the instruction to capture exact exit, direction, lane, and evidence sources after a collision.
   - https://connect.ncdot.gov/letting/design%20build%20program/i-3803b/rfp%20final.pdf
