## Local facts used

1. **Sustained deep cold:** NWS Twin Cities reports current winter averages of 19 days with lows at or below 0°F and 55 days whose highs are at or below freezing. This supports the site's seasonal cold angle: repeated cold starts can thicken aged lubricant, contract hardware, and expose marginal spring, roller, bearing, or balance conditions.
   - https://www.weather.gov/media/mpx/Climate/What%20Winter%20MSP.pdf

2. **Garage as a separate thermal zone:** Minnesota's Residential Energy Code calls out garages as unconditioned space in the duct-insulation rules and says a nonconditioned space changed to conditioned space must fully comply with the code. This supports keeping insulation, garage conversion, and normal door repair distinct.
   - https://www.revisor.mn.gov/rules/1322/full

3. **Eden Prairie permit process:** The City accepts building permit applications through ePermits and states that regulated structural alterations or repairs to permanent weather-resistive surfaces require permits; permitted work is subject to inspection. This supports checking altered openings and structural scope before ordering a replacement door.
   - https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/doing-business/building-inspections
   - https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/doing-business/building-inspections/licenses-and-permits
