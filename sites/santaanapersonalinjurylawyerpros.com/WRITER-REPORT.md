# Santa Ana local research — 2026-08-22

1. **Orange County civil venue:** The Orange County Superior Court says the Central Justice Center at 700 Civic Center Drive West, Santa Ana, handles limited civil, unlimited civil, and small-claims matters. Source: https://www.occourts.org/location/central-justice-center
2. **City density:** Santa Ana reports a 2023 population of 310,539 and density of 12,471.5 people per square mile. Source: https://www.santa-ana.org/public-information-and-media-relations/
3. **Industrial employment context:** Santa Ana’s approved South Coast Technology Center allows light industrial, R&D, light manufacturing, and warehousing uses and is anticipated to create 687 permanent and 555 construction jobs. Source: https://www.santa-ana.org/south-coast-technology-center/

The City of Santa Ana’s neighborhood sitemap names Downtown, Floral Park, French Park, Fisher Park, Lacy, and Logan among the city’s neighborhoods. Source: https://www.santa-ana.org/sitemap/
