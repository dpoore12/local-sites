# Virginia Beach personal injury site — research record

Verified 2026-08-22

1. **Local court jurisdiction.** The Virginia Beach General District Court Civil Division states that personal-injury and wrongful-death claims have a $50,000 jurisdictional limit. This supports the site’s explanation that a claim over the limit presents a Circuit Court question in Virginia Beach’s independent-city court system.
   - https://courts.virginiabeach.gov/general-district-court/civil-division
   - https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title16.1/chapter6/section16.1-77/

2. **Military and federal connection.** NAS Oceana is in Virginia Beach; the Navy identifies it as the East Coast Master Jet Base and reports about 10,500 active Navy personnel, 10,000 family members and 4,500 civilian personnel. A federal tort claim based on federal-employee negligence ordinarily requires presentment to the appropriate federal agency before suit.
   - https://cnrma.cnic.navy.mil/installations/NAS-Oceana/
   - https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R45732

3. **Oceanfront resort setting.** A City of Virginia Beach report describes the Oceanfront District as including a three-mile boardwalk, beach-related Atlantic and Pacific Avenue corridors, and over 40 beachfront hotels and resorts. The setting makes precise location and control of a property condition important in a premises-injury review.
   - https://s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/virginia-beach-departments-docs/communications/Hot-Topics/Atlantic-Park/SiteWorks-VB-The-Wave-Final-Report.pdf
   - https://planning.virginiabeach.gov/zoning/form-based-code/oceanfront-resort-district

Neighborhoods used: Kempsville, Great Neck, Hilltop, Bayside, Oceanfront and Sandbridge.
