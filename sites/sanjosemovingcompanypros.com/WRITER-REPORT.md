# San Jose Moving Services — research notes

Verified 2026-08-22 for phase-1 local copy and `site.json`.

1. **California intrastate authority and price protection.** California's Maximum Rate Tariff 4 says an intrastate household mover's advertising and shipping documents must display its Bureau-issued `CAL-T` number. It also describes the required Agreement for Moving Services and its Not to Exceed Price before service begins, subject to bona fide written change orders.
   - https://bhgs.dca.ca.gov/forms_pubs/20240101_maxtariff.pdf

2. **A San Jose departure that crosses a state line uses a different consumer framework.** FMCSA says federal interstate movers must issue a written estimate based on an actual or virtual inspection. A binding estimate establishes the delivery amount for covered work; a non-binding estimate limits the amount a mover may require at delivery to 110 percent of the estimate. The consumer receives the federal Rights and Responsibilities booklet with the written estimate.
   - https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/consumer-protection/protect-your-move/what-binding-move-estimate
   - https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/consumer-protection/what-are-my-rights-and-responsibilities-when-moving

3. **San José curb and loading coordination.** The City lists tow-away and Downtown lane-closure permits. Its permit page strongly recommends applying for tow-away permits at least two weeks ahead and states that transportation-permit processing can take two business days or longer.
   - https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/transportation/permits
