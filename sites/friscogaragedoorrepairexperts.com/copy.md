## symptom_4_title

## symptom_4

Hearing the motor run while the door stays still points to a separation between the opener and the door, or to a failed part inside the drive system. Do not assume the issue is electronic just because the wall button lights up. First make sure the door is fully closed or supported and keep fingers away from the rail and trolley. If the release cord is visibly disconnected, do not reconnect it while the door is crooked, unusually heavy, or caught in the track.
