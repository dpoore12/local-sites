# Victorville research sources — 2026-08-22

1. **I-15 / Cajon Pass traffic and crash context**
   - Source: https://www.gosbcta.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/I-Comprehensive-Corridor-Study.pdf
   - The study covers I-15 from the SR-60 interchange to the Mojave River crossing at Victorville. At SR-138 and Cleghorn Road it reported 110,000–120,000 average daily vehicles, Friday daily traffic over 130,000, and holiday-Friday traffic near 150,000; more than 90% of weekend traffic was autos. It also described sustained Cajon Pass grades near 6% for about five miles, lower truck speeds on the descent, crash rates 58% above comparable facilities, and rear-end collisions as the predominant crash type.

2. **Victorville–Hesperia I-15 pavement-rehabilitation footprint**
   - Source: https://dot.ca.gov/caltrans-near-me/district-8/district-8-current-projects/i-15-pavement-rehab
   - Caltrans' project covered 59 lane miles and ramps from Oak Hill Road in Hesperia to just south of Bear Valley Road in Victorville, with safety and drainage work. During construction, the agency stated lane/ramp and possible US-395 connector closures could reduce the route to one lane in each direction and cause delays. This is background for preserving a precise road location and construction/traffic context; it is not presented as a current closure.

3. **Local civil filing division**
   - Source: https://www.sb-court.org/general-information/where-can-i-file
   - The Civil Division of the Victorville District is at 14455 Civic Drive and accepts limited and unlimited civil filings. Its listed service area includes Victorville and several High Desert communities. The page itself should not be treated as a universal venue rule for every case.
