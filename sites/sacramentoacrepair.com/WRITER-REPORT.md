## The three sourced local facts

1. **delta_breeze_swing** — NOAA 1991–2020 monthly normals for Sacramento Executive Airport
   (USW00023232): July average high 92.6 °F, July average low 59.2 °F, a >30 °F daily swing.
   NWS Sacramento attributes evening cooling to the delta breeze pushing marine air inland
   through the Carquinez Strait, strengthening roughly 2–4 p.m. Downtown Sacramento hit an
   all-time 116 °F on 6 Sep 2022. Peak indoor heat lands inside SMUD's weekday 5–8 p.m. peak,
   priced at $0.3765/kWh summer peak vs $0.1550 off-peak.
   - https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/services/data/v1?dataset=normals-monthly-1991-2020&stations=USW00023232&dataTypes=MLY-TMAX-NORMAL,MLY-TMIN-NORMAL&format=json
   - https://www.weather.gov/media/aviation/CaseStudies/SACDeltaBreezeStratus_CaseStudy.pdf
   - https://www.smud.org/Rate-Information/Residential-rates

2. **housing_vintage** — ACS 2024: Sacramento city median year structure built **1978**;
   ~23,500 of ~212,000 units built 1939 or earlier (≈1 in 9). Elk Grove, same county,
   median year built **2001**. (Modesto's median is 1979, so the differentiator used in copy
   is the pre-1940 share and the county-internal contrast, not the median alone.)
   - https://censusreporter.org/tables/B25035/
   - https://data.census.gov/table/ACSDT5Y2024.B25035
   - https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/latest?table_ids=B25035,B25034&geo_ids=16000US0664000,16000US0622020

3. **permit_rule** — City of Sacramento requires a building permit for repairs/replacements to
   mechanical systems. Its express Planning Exemption Form (CDD-0035) allows an HVAC change-out
   only when the new unit goes back in the same location; a new location must be screened from
   all street views; the form cannot be used on a landmark parcel or in a historic district.
   California energy code adds independent field verification / diagnostic testing, with
   documentation required to close the permit.
   - https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/community-development/building/permit-services/required-permits.html
   - https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/content/dam/portal/cdd/Building/Forms/CDD-0035_Planning-Exemption-Form-Express.pdf
   - https://www.energy.ca.gov/programs-and-topics/programs/home-energy-rating-system-hers-program
