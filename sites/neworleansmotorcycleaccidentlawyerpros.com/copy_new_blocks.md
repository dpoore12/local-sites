Four different jobs arrive under one heading here. A turn across your path is an intersection-geometry question, answered by signal phase, sight lines and where the rail sits. A sideswipe is answered by the long marks down two vehicles and by whatever escape path the pavement left you. An uninsured driver sends you into your own policy and a rejection form your carrier was required to retain. Negotiation is a records-and-privileges problem that outlives all three. Confusing them burns the weeks that decide a file: a Quarter camera overwrites, a Public Works crew patches the defect you needed photographed, and the prescriptive period keeps running underneath.

## qa_4_question

Can a rider sue the other driver's insurance company directly in Louisiana?

## qa_4_answer

Sometimes, and that is unusual. Revised Statute 22:1269, the Direct Action Statute, lets an injured person proceed against a liability insurer rather than only against the person who caused the harm. Acts 2024, No. 275, effective August 1, 2024, narrowed it considerably. An action against the insurer alone is now limited to listed situations. Those include the insured's bankruptcy or insolvency, failed service or a defendant who will not defend within one hundred eighty days, a deceased insured, certain claims between spouses or between parents and children, a coverage denial or a defense under reservation of rights, and the case of an uninsured motorist carrier. The same amendment keeps the insurer out of the case caption and keeps coverage from being mentioned to the jury unless Code of Evidence article 411 requires it.

## svc_left_turn_motorcycle_accident_body

### How you know this is the claim you have

An oncoming vehicle began turning while you were still traveling straight through the crossing. Contact usually lands on the car's front quarter panel or driver's door, and your front wheel, forks or engine case carries the matching mark. If the car was already deep into its arc, the damage sits farther back along its flank. Write down which lane you occupied, whether the turn came out of a dedicated pocket or from a through lane, and what the signal was displaying for you. Do that before anyone invites you to estimate a speed, because the estimate is what gets quoted back later.

### The rule the turning driver was under

Revised Statute 32:122 is one sentence long. A driver within an intersection intending to turn left shall yield the right of way to all vehicles approaching from the opposite direction which are within the intersection or so close as to constitute an immediate hazard. Nothing in that sentence asks whether the approaching vehicle was easy to see. The provision does not end the argument either. Distance, timing, visibility and physical proof still have to be established, and the turning driver will offer a version in which you were neither close nor visible.

### Why these crossings photograph badly

Canal Street and St. Charles Avenue carry streetcar track in the neutral ground, and rails, flangeways and paving joints change what a rider can do in the half second before impact. The city's own carnival guidance tells cyclists to cross tracks at a ninety-degree angle for that exact reason. Shoot the full approach from your position, then from the driver's position, then the resting places, then the surface in between. Wide frames fix the signal head and the sight line. Tight frames preserve the flangeway, the patch and the paint transfer, none of which survive a resurfacing contract.

### Prescription is the word, and the period recently changed

Civil law vocabulary applies here, so the deadline is a liberative prescription rather than a statute of limitations. Civil Code article 3493.1 gives delictual actions two years, commencing the day injury or damage is sustained. That period arrived through Acts 2024, No. 423, effective July 1, 2024, which also repealed article 3492 and the one-year rule the state was known for. The act is prospective only and reaches actions arising after that date, so a cause of action that arose earlier still lives under one year. Filing happens in a parish, not a county: Code of Civil Procedure article 74 permits a damages action where the wrongful conduct occurred or where the damage was sustained, and the general civil trial court for this parish is Civil District Court at 421 Loyola Avenue.

### What the first week should produce

Request the report through the police department's records channel; NOPD states that accident and criminal event reports should be available twenty working days from the date of the incident. Keep the item number. Find out which yard has the bike and whether storage is accruing against it. Photograph the helmet, jacket, gloves and boots before anything gets washed. Begin treatment and keep every discharge sheet, because a gap in care becomes an argument about causation. If a witness gave a name at the scene, call while the light phase is still a memory rather than a reconstruction.

### The mistake that hands over fifty-one percent

Article 2323 changed on January 1, 2026 through Acts 2025, No. 15. A claimant whose share of negligence falls below fifty-one percent recovers a proportionally reduced amount. At fifty-one percent or above, that claimant recovers nothing at all. Fault gets allocated among everyone who caused or contributed to the loss, whether or not they are parties. So a recorded statement in which a rider guesses at speed, or agrees that swerving was possible, is no longer merely unhelpful. It is an argument aimed at the cliff edge, and adjusters working this market know the edge is there.

### How the fee is set under Rule 1.5

The Rules of Professional Conduct permit a fee contingent on the outcome of a tort matter and forbid an unreasonable one, measured against eight listed factors. No percentage appears anywhere in the rule. Paragraph (c) requires the agreement in writing and signed, with a copy handed over at signing, stating separately the shares that apply on settlement, at trial and on appeal. Ask which litigation expenses come out of the recovery. Then ask whether those expenses are subtracted before or after the fee is calculated, because that ordering moves real money.

## svc_lane_change_motorcycle_accident_body

### The marks that separate a sideswipe from a solo fall

Paint down a rear door, a bent lever, a scored bar end, a torn jacket sleeve and a scuffed tank tell one story. A front wheel folded into a stationary bumper tells a different one. Photograph the whole flank of the other vehicle at a shallow angle so the streak reads, then the same flank of the motorcycle. Where there was no contact at all, because the car came over and you braked or steered off, no shared paint exists to find. That sequence has to come from video, from witnesses, and from marks left on the roadway.

### What Revised Statute 32:79 actually requires

Two clauses in the state's lane statute matter to a rider. A vehicle shall be driven as nearly as practicable entirely within a single lane. It shall not be moved from that lane until the driver has first ascertained that the movement can be made with safety. Read plainly, the burden sits on the driver who moved. "I checked my mirror" is an assertion about what was ascertained rather than proof of it, and the carrier will answer by talking about where the bike was positioned instead.

### The escape path this city often does not provide

Whether a rider had anywhere to go is a fact question, and the honest answer here is frequently no. The Office of Strategic Engagement reports the average pavement condition rating for city streets as Poor, a D-minus, with roughly 65 percent of streets rated poor or worse and about $5 billion needed to rebuild them. Subsidence drives much of that. Absolute subsidence across the city averaged 0.2 inches a year, plus or minus 0.1 inches, from 2002 to 2005, with parts of town moving more than 0.8 inches annually and NASA measurements showing spots above an inch a year.

### Rails, plates and the neutral ground

Track sits in the neutral ground along Canal Street and St. Charles Avenue and runs at street level on other downtown alignments. The transit agency's safety guidance notes plainly that streetcars, unlike buses, cannot stop suddenly. Steel plates over utility cuts appear and disappear on Quarter and downtown blocks with no notice to anybody who rides. Two habits help. Photograph the plate or the flangeway with something in frame for scale, and record the date, because the cut may be backfilled and repaved inside a week.

### When the street itself becomes part of the claim

Revised Statute 9:2800 bars liability against a public entity for the condition of a thing in its custody unless the entity had actual or constructive notice of the particular vice or defect, had a reasonable opportunity to remedy it, and failed to do so. Constructive notice means the existence of facts which infer actual knowledge. A 311 record therefore carries weight. Reporting a pothole by phone or at nola.gov/311 generates a service ticket number routed to the Public Works maintenance division, and a ticket predating a crash is documentary. City policy also routes personal injury or property damage claims arising from streets and sidewalks onto a specific form, CAO Form No. 19, received by the City Attorney's small claims division.

### The first week, in order

Get the item number and note which agency worked the scene. Photograph the bike from every side before it is released, repaired or crushed, and leave the gear unwashed. Report the pavement defect and save the ticket number. Pull the declarations page out of the glovebox and read the coverages instead of assuming them. Then write out, dated and in your own words, what you saw and did, because the version you give six months from now will be measured against this one.

### Percentages, prescription and fees

Article 2323 has barred recovery at fifty-one percent fault or more since January 1, 2026, and reduces damages proportionally below that line. Article 3493.1 allows two years from the day the injury is sustained for actions arising after July 1, 2024; before that date the older one-year prescription governs. Rule 1.5 states no percentage for a tort fee and instead tests reasonableness against eight factors. Paragraph (c) of that rule requires a signed writing setting out the settlement, trial and appeal shares and the treatment of expenses.

## svc_uninsured_motorist_motorcycle_claim_lede

The card the other driver handed over is expired, or there was no card, or the tail lights kept going down Elysian Fields. From that moment your policy is the document that matters, and so is a form your insurer was required to keep.

## svc_uninsured_motorist_motorcycle_claim_body

### When your own carrier becomes the opposing party

An uninsured or underinsured claim runs on the same crash facts as any other, with one difference in posture: the company you pay is now the entity valuing your injuries. Everything still has to be proved. Liability, mechanism, treatment, wage loss and permanency all get documented as if a stranger were on the other side, because functionally one is. Send written notice early even while the other driver's identity or coverage is disputed, and keep every claim number, adjuster name and dated letter in one folder.

### The rejection form your insurer had to keep

Revised Statute 22:1295 requires uninsured motorist coverage in an auto liability policy issued here, at limits no lower than the policy's bodily injury liability limits. Coverage is not required only where a named insured rejects it, selects lower limits, or selects economic-only coverage. That election is valid only on a form prescribed by the insurance commissioner and signed by the named insured or a legal representative. A properly completed and signed form creates a rebuttable presumption that the rejection was knowing. Where the form is signed but not properly completed, that presumption does not apply. The insurer must verify completion and retain the form, and it may not delegate the duty to retain it to the producer of record.

### Minimum limits are smaller than a single trauma admission

Revised Statute 32:900 sets the floor for a motor vehicle liability policy at fifteen thousand dollars for bodily injury to one person, thirty thousand where two or more people are hurt in one accident, and twenty-five thousand for property damage. A rider with a shattered tibia and a helicopter transport can exhaust fifteen thousand dollars before being discharged. Underinsured is consequently the more common problem than genuinely uninsured. The work is identical either way: locate every policy that might respond, and read whether the coverage is full or the economic-only variant, which by statute excludes pain, suffering, inconvenience and mental anguish.

### The coverage claim carries its own deadline

Revised Statute 9:5629 prescribes actions brought under uninsured motorist provisions at two years reckoning from the date of the accident. Both dates belong on paper, because the tort claim against the driver and the contract claim against your carrier are separate causes of action. Policy conditions add a third layer. Notice, cooperation, recorded statements and vehicle inspection are contractual duties with their own timing, and a carrier told late will make that point in writing.

### Suing the insurer directly is possible here, within limits

Louisiana looks unlike most of the country on this point. Revised Statute 22:1269 permits an injured person to proceed against a liability insurer, and Acts 2024, No. 275, effective August 1, 2024, cut that right down to a list. An action against the insurer alone now requires one of several circumstances. Among them are the insured's bankruptcy or insolvency, service attempted without success, a served defendant who refuses to answer or defend within one hundred eighty days, and the death of the insured. The list also covers a coverage denial or a defense under reservation of rights, certain claims between spouses or between parents and children, and, most relevant to this page, an insurer acting as an uninsured motorist carrier. Two procedural changes came with the same act. No insurer may appear in the caption, and coverage is not disclosed to the jury unless Code of Evidence article 411 requires it.

### Hit and run, and what actually preserves it

Vehicle color, body style, a partial plate, direction of travel and the minute it happened are the details that keep a search alive. Ask specifically that nearby footage be preserved rather than assuming an officer collected it, and write down which businesses and residences face the block. Dispatch audio and 911 records exist too, and both are requestable through the public records process the department publishes.

### What the agreement has to state about fees

Rule 1.5 governs the arrangement whether the defendant is a stranger or your own insurer. The rule publishes no percentage and instead measures a fee against eight reasonableness factors. Paragraph (c) requires the contingent agreement in a signed writing, with a copy handed to the client at signing, stating the shares for settlement, trial and appeal and identifying which expenses come off the recovery. It also requires a written statement of the outcome when the matter ends.

## svc_motorcycle_injury_claim_negotiation_lede

Nothing about a serious injury file resolves in one phone call. It is months of paper moving between a treating surgeon, a records clerk, an adjuster with a reserve number, and a Civil Code that changed twice inside two years.

## svc_motorcycle_injury_claim_negotiation_body

### What negotiating a rider's claim consists of

The exchange runs on documents rather than argument. Emergency and operative records, imaging, therapy notes, billing ledgers, wage verification, repair or total-loss paperwork, scene photographs and any downloaded event data all go across in an organized package. Each answers a narrow question and should not be stretched past what it shows. An early offer that arrives before treatment has plateaued is usually an offer to buy the uncertainty cheaply, and a release signed at that stage closes every issue in the file, including the ones nobody has measured yet.

### The Direct Action Statute, and what 2024 did to it

Most states make an injured person sue the driver and wait for the carrier to stand behind the judgment. Revised Statute 22:1269 has long allowed a claimant here to reach the liability insurer itself, and that right sits inside the policy's terms and limits rather than outside them. Acts 2024, No. 275, effective August 1, 2024, reshaped it. Proceeding against the insurer alone now requires one of the enumerated circumstances: bankruptcy or insolvency, unsuccessful service, a defendant who refuses to answer or defend within one hundred eighty days, death of the insured, a denial of coverage or a defense under reservation of rights, certain intrafamily claims, or an uninsured motorist carrier. Two mechanics matter in practice. An insurer may not be named in the caption, and a court may not disclose coverage to the jury unless Code of Evidence article 411 requires it. One provision cuts the claimant's way: filing against the insured interrupts prescription as to all insurers whose policies cover the claims asserted.

### The insurer has a clock of its own

Revised Statute 22:1892 imposes timing on the company, not only on the claimant. An insurer must pay the amount of any claim due an insured within thirty days after receiving satisfactory proofs of loss. A bona fide third party's property damage claim and reasonable medical expenses claim are due within thirty days after a written settlement agreement. Loss adjustment on a property damage claim must be initiated within fourteen days of notification, except in catastrophic loss. Dates of every letter, estimate and proof submission therefore belong in a log, because the log is the only way anyone reconstructs whether those windows were met.

### Fifty-one percent is the number they are working toward

Article 2323, as amended effective January 1, 2026, reduces damages in proportion to a claimant's negligence below fifty-one percent and denies recovery entirely at fifty-one percent or above. Fault is assessed against every person who caused or contributed to the loss, party or not. That arithmetic explains the shape of the questions an adjuster asks about lane position, closing speed, gear and reaction. It also explains why a percentage should never be conceded in conversation before the physical record has been assembled and read.

### Helmets: what the statute says and what it omits

Revised Statute 32:190 requires every operator and rider on a motorcycle, motor-driven cycle or motorized bicycle to wear a secured helmet meeting the commissioner's specifications, and subsection F fixes the fine at fifty dollars including all court costs. What the section contains is a traffic requirement. What it does not contain is any evidentiary provision about damages or fault. Compare Revised Statute 32:199 on bicycle helmets, which states outright that a citation under it is not prima facie evidence of negligence. No comparable sentence appears in the motorcycle section, so expect gear to come up and expect it answered with medical proof about the injuries actually sustained. Statewide, the Highway Safety Commission counted 98 rider deaths in the motorcycle category in 2023 alongside 392 suspected serious injuries, with approved helmet use recorded in 59 percent of those fatalities.

### The parish, the court and what builds a number

New Orleans is coterminous with Orleans Parish, which is why nothing here is described by county. The parish's general civil trial court is Civil District Court at 421 Loyola Avenue, one of the state's forty-three judicial districts, with original jurisdiction over civil matters in the parish. Local context has evidentiary value too. The city's Safe Streets for All analysis reports 297 people killed and 1,864 seriously injured on these streets between 2019 and 2023 across nearly 85,000 crashes, and found people walking or riding motorcycles more than 16 times as likely to be killed or seriously hurt as people inside cars.

### Fees, expenses and the closing statement

Rule 1.5 sets no percentage and tests any fee against eight reasonableness factors. Paragraph (c) requires a signed written agreement, a copy delivered at signing, separate shares for settlement, trial and appeal, and a statement of which expenses are deducted and whether the deduction happens before or after the fee is figured. It also requires a written statement of the outcome at the end, showing the remittance and how it was calculated. Two questions are worth asking early. Who advances the cost of records and a reconstruction, and what happens to those advances if nothing is recovered.
