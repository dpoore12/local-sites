# New Orleans Motorcycle Accident Lawyer — research findings

Verified 2026-08-22.

1. **Current Louisiana personal-injury prescription:** Civil Code article 3493.1 says delictual actions have a **two-year liberative prescription**, running from the day injury or damage is sustained. The official page identifies Acts 2024, No. 423, effective July 1, 2024. Source: https://www.legis.la.gov/legis/Law.aspx?d=1386443
2. **Current comparative-fault rule:** The current Civil Code article 2323 applies a 51% bar, not pure comparative fault: less than 51% reduces damages proportionally; 51% or more bars recovery. The official current code identifies the 2025 amendment, effective January 1, 2026. Source: https://www.legis.la.gov/legis/law.aspx?d=109387
3. **New Orleans road ground:** NOLA Ready states that subsidence occurs throughout the New Orleans planning area and causes extensive road and drainage damage; the City pavement assessment reports approximately 65% of city streets in poor or worse condition. Sources: https://ready.nola.gov/hazard-mitigation/hazards/subsidence/ and https://ose.nola.gov/about/
4. **Orleans civil court:** Orleans Parish Civil District Court lists its address as 421 Loyola Avenue, New Orleans. Source: https://www.orleanscivildistrictcourt.org/
5. **Neighborhood source:** Mid-City, Bywater, Marigny, Gentilly, Algiers Point, and Lakeview appear on the City Planning Commission's neighborhood map. Source: https://nolaplans.com/plans/General/Planning_Districts_and_Neighborhoods_Map.pdf
