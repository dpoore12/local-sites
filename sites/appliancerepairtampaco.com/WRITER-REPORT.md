# Tampa Appliance Repair — research record

Verified 2026-08-22.

1. **Humid conditions:** NOAA engineering weather data for Tampa lists 5,093 annual hours meeting its humid-area criterion of wet-bulb temperature above 67°F. This supports copy about humidity making weak refrigerator seals, condensation control, and dryer airflow problems more apparent.  
   URL: https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/pub/data/EngineeringWeatherData_CDROM/engwx/tampa_fl.pdf

2. **Hard water:** The City of Tampa’s 2025 water-quality report states an average finished-water hardness of 211 mg/L (12.3 grains per gallon). This supports inspection of mineral scale in dishwasher components and water-heating areas.  
   URL: https://www.tampa.gov/sites/default/files/document/2026/2025-water-quality-report.pdf

3. **Rental and apartment mix:** Plan Hillsborough’s 2022 Tampa housing report lists 77,745 renter-occupied homes (49.5% of occupied units) and 70,201 apartment units (40.58% of all units). This supports copy about access, authorization, and compact utility spaces.  
   URL: https://planhillsborough.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Tampa-Housing-Section-Data-23.pdf

Neighborhoods used: Hyde Park, Seminole Heights, Ybor City, Tampa Heights, Davis Islands, and Ballast Point. City neighborhood directory: https://www.tampa.gov/service/neighborhood-maps
