# Fresno wrongful-death site research notes

## Sourced facts used in `site.json`

1. California separates a wrongful-death action under CCP §377.60 from a survival action under CCP §377.30. CCP §377.34 contains the general exclusion of the decedent's pain, suffering, and disfigurement in survival damages, subject to stated exceptions.
   - https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CCP&sectionNum=377.60.
   - https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CCP&sectionNum=377.30.
   - https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CCP&sectionNum=377.34.

2. Fresno County Superior Court identifies the B. F. Sisk Courthouse, 1130 O Street, as the location for Unlimited Civil cases.
   - https://www.fresno.courts.ca.gov/divisions/civil/civil-unlimited

3. Fresno County's agricultural-production-and-processing sector directly supported 63,103 jobs in 2023.
   - https://www.fresnocountyca.gov/files/sharedassets/county/v/1/agricultural-commissioner/ag-crop-reports/economic-contributions-of-fresno-county-agriculture.pdf

## Additional worksite record fact used in copy

California Title 8, section 342 requires employers to report a work-connected employee death immediately, generally no later than eight hours after knowledge of the death, subject to the regulation's exigent-circumstances provision.
- https://www.dir.ca.gov/title8/342.html
