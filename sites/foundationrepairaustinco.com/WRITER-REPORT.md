## Local facts used

1. **Balcones geology:** The USGS Austin quadrangle description identifies a higher western Edwards Plateau district and a lower eastern Coastal Plain-margin district, with the Balcones escarpment as a major structural and topographic boundary.  
   https://pubs.usgs.gov/gf/076/text.pdf

2. **Named clay conditions:** A City of Austin appendix says Taylor Group material in the Austin area weathers to montmorillonitic clay generally known as Taylor Clay. The USDA Houston Black series lists a Travis County type location and classifies it as smectitic clay.  
   https://www.austintexas.gov/sites/default/files/files/Appendices%20C.pdf  
   https://soilseries.sc.egov.usda.gov/OSD_Docs/H/HOUSTON_BLACK.html

3. **Dry-to-wet moisture change:** A City of Austin rainfall study records 19.68 inches at Camp Mabry in 2011. The city’s 2022 Watershed Protection report records 2.96 inches in one hour after 51 days with no measurable rainfall.  
   https://services.austintexas.gov/edims/document.cfm?id=297205  
   https://www.austintexas.gov/sites/default/files/files/Watershed/2022WPD-AnnualReport_OFFICIAL_v2_interactive.pdf
