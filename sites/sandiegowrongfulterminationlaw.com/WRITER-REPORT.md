# Research sources — sandiegowrongfulterminationlaw.com

Verified 2026-08-22.

1. California Labor Code section 2922: employment without a specified term may be terminated at the will of either party on notice.
   https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&division=3.&title=&part=&chapter=2.&article=4.

2. California Civil Rights Department employment process: in general, employment-discrimination complaints must be filed within three years of the alleged discriminatory act; a CRD complaint is required even if the person wishes to go directly to court; an immediate right-to-sue notice may be requested when filing.
   https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/employment/

3. San Diego County Superior Court Hall of Justice: 330 West Broadway, San Diego, CA 92101; it houses Civil Independent Calendar courtrooms and the Civil Business Office.
   https://www.sdcourt.ca.gov/sdcourt/generalinformation/courtlocations/hallofjustice%20courthouse

Additional page context:

4. EEOC time limits: the ordinary federal filing deadline is extended to 300 days when a state or local agency enforces a law prohibiting discrimination on the same basis. In California, a CRD complaint may be dual-filed with EEOC when federal law applies.
   https://www.eeoc.gov/time-limits-filing-charge
   https://www.eeoc.gov/filing-charge-discrimination

5. City of San Diego profile: 2024 San Diego County life-science workforce had more than 71,000 direct employees; military/defense accounted for approximately 357,000 regional jobs in the 2025 study period.
   https://www.sandiego.gov/sites/default/files/pb_v1cityprofile.pdf
