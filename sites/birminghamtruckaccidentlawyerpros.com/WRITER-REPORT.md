# Birmingham truck-accident local research

## Local facts used

1. **Alabama’s pure contributory-negligence rule.** In *Bohannon v. Driskell*, the Alabama Supreme Court affirmed a jury instruction that the “slightest bit” of contributory negligence bars recovery on a negligence claim. That is the foundation for the page’s plain-language warning that a claimant’s own negligent contribution can bar recovery, not simply reduce it. Source: https://law.justia.com/cases/alabama/supreme-court/1988/519-so-2d-1314-1.html
2. **Birmingham’s I-20/I-59–I-65 interchange.** The Federal Highway Administration’s final report on ALDOT’s I-59/20 reconstruction project says the affected segment ran from Arkadelphia Road to east of the I-65 interchange, included the interchange modifications, and affected approximately 250,000 vehicles per day. Source: https://highways.dot.gov/media/203331
3. **Jefferson County civil-court structure.** The Tenth Judicial Circuit says Jefferson County has separate civil and criminal divisions. The Birmingham Division Circuit Clerk identifies the Jefferson County Courthouse at 716 Richard Arrington Jr. Blvd. N., Birmingham, AL 35203. Sources: https://jefferson.alacourt.gov/circuit-court/ and https://jeffersoncircuitclerk.alacourt.gov/

## Federal carrier rule used in the copy

FMCSA guidance under 49 CFR 395.8(k)(1) says motor carriers must retain records of duty status and supporting documents for six months from receipt. Source: https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/hours-service/elds/how-long-must-motor-carriers-retain-records-duty-status-rods-and-supporting
