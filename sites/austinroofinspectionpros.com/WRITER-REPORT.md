# Austin roof inspection research — 2026-08-22

## Local facts selected

1. **Texas property-claim timeline.** Texas Department of Insurance states an insurer generally has 15 business days to acknowledge a claim and 15 business days after receiving the information it needs to decide whether to pay; it may extend the latter deadline by 45 days with an explanation. This makes a dated independent roof-condition record useful alongside, but distinct from, an adjuster's inspection.
   - https://www.tdi.texas.gov/blog/insurance-claim-deadlines.html
   - Official statute: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/IN/pdf/IN.542.pdf

2. **Austin residential roofing permit triggers.** City of Austin Development Services lists a Residential Express Building Permit for roof work replacing decking over 128 square feet, and for more than 50% roofing replacement in the Wildland-Urban Interface Code Area. Some asphalt-shingle work without structural impact may be exempt; WUI properties also have material requirements.
   - https://www.austintexas.gov/development-services/express-permits

3. **Tree canopy.** City of Austin reports 41% citywide canopy cover in 2022, from summer satellite imagery. Dense mature canopy can mean branches, leaf buildup, and shaded roof planes deserve explicit documentation in an inspection.
   - https://www.austintexas.gov/climate/austin-nature-city/austin-closer-its-canopy-goal-50-2050
