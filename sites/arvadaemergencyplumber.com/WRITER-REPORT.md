# Arvada emergency plumbing research notes

Verified 2026-08-22.

1. **Arvada water hardness** — The City says Arvada water ranges from about 20 to 100 mg/L, averages 84 mg/L (about 5 grains per gallon), and is soft to moderately hard. It also says moderate hardness can build scale on pipes.
   - https://www.arvadaco.gov/FAQ.aspx?QID=405

2. **Housing-era contrast** — The City says the Stocke Addition was formally platted in 1904 and the Walter subdivision in 1920. Candelas reports home sales opened in 2012.
   - https://www.arvadaco.gov/425/Historic-Neighborhoods
   - https://www.candelaslife.com/news/

3. **Expansive soil and under-slab plumbing** — A soils and foundation summary filed in Arvada's permit system for Arvada Point says expansive soil and bedrock were present. It recommends pressure testing under-slab plumbing, flexible couplings, and isolating plumbing that passes through slabs. Colorado guidance explains that expansive soil expands when wet and shrinks when dry.
   - https://www.arvadapermits.org/etrakit3/viewAttachment.aspx?Group=PERMIT&ActivityNo=RES23-00889&key=AH:2311280950190498
   - https://planningforhazards.colorado.gov/soil-hazards
