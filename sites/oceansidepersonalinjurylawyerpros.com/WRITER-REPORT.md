# Oceanside Personal Injury Site — Research & Build Handoff

## Local facts used

1. **Camp Pendleton federal-claims connection.** Marine Corps Base Camp Pendleton says that claims involving property damage, personal injury, or wrongful death caused by a government employee acting within the scope of employment may be recoverable under the Federal Tort Claims Act or Military Claims Act; its Civil Law page instructs claimants to submit completed packages to the appropriate federal processing agency and says claims must be delivered within two years of accrual.
   - Source: https://www.pendleton.marines.mil/Main-Menu/Staff-Agencies/Legal-Assistance/Civil-Law/
   - Why it matters: Oceanside borders Camp Pendleton, so the location, employer and equipment ownership need to be recorded accurately when an event may have a federal connection.

2. **North County Superior Court in Vista.** The San Diego Superior Court identifies the North County Regional Center at 325 South Melrose Drive, Vista, as a full-service branch that hears civil matters and has a venue encompassing most of Northern San Diego County.
   - Source: https://www.sdcourt.ca.gov/sdcourt/generalinformation/courtlocations/northcountycourthouse
   - Why it matters: court and venue are case-specific, but the incident address and full party names are necessary to assess the local civil-court path.

3. **Coastal rail-corridor boundaries.** North County Transit District documents different fencing conditions along Oceanside segments from Morse Street to Cassidy Street, Cassidy Street to Vista Way, and Vista Way to Eaton Street.
   - Source: https://gonctd.com/about-nctd/safety-security/trespasser-mitigation/eee/
   - Why it matters: a precise rail-corridor location, direction of travel and responder information can identify the relevant records and controlling entity.
