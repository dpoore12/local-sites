## Local facts

1. **Broward injunction filing path:** The Broward County Clerk allows eFiling or in-person domestic-violence injunction petitions, charges no fee, says accepted e-filed petitions receive a same-day decision, and says a hearing follows within 15 days or fewer if an injunction is granted.  
   Source: https://www.browardclerk.org/Divisions/DomesticViolence

2. **Civil injunction versus criminal charge:** Florida Courts says an injunction is a civil-court matter, a related charge is heard in criminal court, and the injunction hearing is separate and recorded.  
   Source: https://www.flcourts.gov/Resources-Services/Office-of-Family-Courts/Family-Court-in-Florida/Domestic-Violence/Overview-for-Respondents

3. **Local court structure:** The Seventeenth Judicial Circuit is the circuit court for Broward County and is headquartered in Fort Lauderdale.  
   Source: https://news.flcourts.gov/All-Court-News/The-17th-Judicial-Circuit-Welcomes-Our-Newest-Judge-Johnathan-D.-Lott
