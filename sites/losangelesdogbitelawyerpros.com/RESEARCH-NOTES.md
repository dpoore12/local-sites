## California statutes
- **Civil Code 3342(a)** — "The owner of any dog is liable for the damages suffered by any person who is bitten by the dog while in a public place or lawfully in a private place, including the property of the owner of the dog, regardless of the former viciousness of the dog or the owner's knowledge of such viciousness." Lawful presence = duty imposed by state law or US postal regulation, or express/implied invitation. (b) withdraws the action against a governmental agency using a dog in military or police work in four listed assists plus self-defense from an annoying/harassing/provoking act; (c) restores it for a person not a party to / participant in / suspected of the conduct prompting the dog's use; (d) applies (b) only where the agency adopted a written policy on necessary and appropriate use. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=3342.&lawCode=CIV
- **CCP 335.1** — two years for injury or death caused by the wrongful act or neglect of another. https://law.justia.com/codes/california/code-ccp/part-2/title-2/chapter-3/section-335-1/
- **CCP 352** — (a) minority/incapacity disability time is not part of the limitation period; (b) does not apply to actions against a public entity or employee requiring a Government Code claim. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=352.&lawCode=CCP
- **Gov. Code 911.2(a)** — personal injury claim presented not later than six months after accrual. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=911.2.&lawCode=GOV
- **Gov. Code 945.6(a)** — suit within six months after written notice under 913 is delivered or mailed; two years if no such notice. https://california.public.law/codes/ca_gov't_code_section_945.6
- **Evidence Code 669** — presumption of failure to exercise due care on ordinance violation (4 elements); (b)(1) ordinary-prudence rebuttal; (b)(2) child measured by maturity, intelligence and capacity, unavailable for adult-only activities. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=669.&lawCode=EVID
- **Civil Code 1714(a)** — responsibility for injury occasioned by want of ordinary care or skill in the management of one's property or person. https://law.justia.com/codes/california/code-civ/division-3/part-3/section-1714/
- **Bus. & Prof. Code 6147** — contingency contract written, signed by both, duplicate at signing; must state rate, cost effect on fee and recovery, compensation for related matters, and that the fee is not set by law but negotiable; (b) voidable at plaintiff's option. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=6147.&lawCode=BPC

## Los Angeles Municipal Code
- **53.06.2(a)** — dog kept exclusively on keeper's premises; off premises only under control of a competent person and restrained by a substantial chain or leash not exceeding six feet, or in a dog exercise/training area established under section 63.44. https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/los_angeles/latest/lamc/0-0-0-136443
- **53.34** — a person who owns, is in charge of, controls or possesses a dog and permits it at large on public property or another's private property is guilty of a misdemeanor if the dog bites, attacks or injures a person or animal; conviction bars owning that species three years and the Department shall not issue or renew a license for it. https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/los_angeles/latest/lamc/0-0-0-137068
- **53.34.4** — Department, after a hearing, may declare an animal dangerous whenever it has bitten, attacked or caused injury to any human being or other animal; eleven evidence categories including prior history, nature/extent of injuries and number of victims, place, presence or absence of provocation, property damage, fight training, temperament, retrainability, manner maintained by owner or custodian, other maintenance evidence, ability to protect public safety. Disposition (d)(3): a declared dangerous animal shall be humanely destroyed; General Manager signs the order within two days after the appeal time passes, or five days after service of a Board determination. https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/los_angeles/latest/lamc/0-0-0-137100
- **53.18.5** — (b) Hearing Examiner appointed by the General Manager; (c) notice served at least 10 days before the hearing stating purpose, reason, remedy or penalty sought, time and place; (h) technical evidence rules relaxed, hearsay may supplement but not alone support a finding, oral evidence on oath; (i) recording or transcript taken; (j) Department's burden by preponderance; (k) Examiner reports within 15 days, report is a public record and is served on the owner; (m) General Manager's written decision within 15 days of receipt, final on service unless appealed within 15 days; (q) appeal to the Board of Commissioners on a Department form; (l) conditions may include locations on the property where the dog may not be kept, enclosure size/construction/design, approved training, removal of dogs from the premises, restraint or muzzling, photo/permanent/electronic identification, sterilization, Restricted Dog Permit, civil penalty; $250 civil penalty on re-issuance. https://www.laanimalservices.com/sites/default/files/documents/PERMITS/LAMC53.18.5.pdf
- **LA Animal Services administrative hearing guide** — ordinances enacted July 1987 (LAMC 53.18.5, 53.34.4, 53.63) authorized the Department to conduct administrative hearings; dangerous animals reported to the nearest shelter; officers investigate menacing behavior and the animal may be impounded for observation. https://www.laanimalservices.com/administrative-hearing-guide
- **53.15.3** — $91.50 license processing fee, $16.50 for a spayed/neutered dog or one certified incapable of breeding; failure to pay the license tax and fee within 45 days of acquiring a dog four months or older, of the dog reaching four months, of license expiration, of mailed notice, or of anti-rabies vaccination expiration is a misdemeanor. https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/los_angeles/latest/lamc/0-0-0-136780

## LA County
- **DPH Veterinary Public Health, bite reporting** — all people with knowledge of a bite may report; a bite report does not mean the animal is deemed dangerous, and a resident who believes it dangerous must file a separate report with the local animal control agency; quarantine minimum 10 days for dogs and cats, 30 days for livestock; the vast majority of quarantines are performed in the biting animal's home; animals impounded by an agency complete quarantine in a shelter; Pasadena, Long Beach and Vernon have their own contacts. http://publichealth.lacounty.gov/vet/biteintro.htm
- **DPH rabies manual, quarantines** — biting animal quarantined, confined and observed at least 14 days (dogs and cats 10) after the day of the bite; dogs or cats in strict confinement under a licensed veterinarian may be released after five days of veterinary observation if the veterinarian examines the animal on the fifth day or later and certifies no clinical signs; owner must report to the director at once. http://publichealth.lacounty.gov/vet/rabiesmanual/quarantines.htm
- **County Code 10.37.110** (unincorporated county / contract cities) — Director may petition the Superior Court or serve a petition for an administrative hearing; hearing held promptly between 5 and 10 working days after service; no jury; preponderance; neutral hearing officer. http://lacounty-ca.elaws.us/code/coor_title10_div1_ch10.37_sec10.37.110
