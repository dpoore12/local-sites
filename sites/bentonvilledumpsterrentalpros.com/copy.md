### Loading a heavy box

Spread the weight evenly across the floor, never piled at one end, so the truck can lift and carry the box safely. Break slabs into pieces you can handle. Stop at the fill line the hauler gives you, even if the box looks half empty. A box too heavy to lift gets partly unloaded on site, by hand, and that labor is yours.
