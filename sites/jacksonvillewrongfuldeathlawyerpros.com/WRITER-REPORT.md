## Sourced local facts

1. **Florida’s personal representative and survivor rule.** Florida Statute § 768.20 requires the decedent’s personal representative to bring the wrongful death action for the benefit of survivors and the estate. Section 768.18 defines survivors, including a spouse, children, parents, and certain dependent blood relatives and adopted siblings.
   - https://www.leg.state.fl.us/Statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0700-0799/0768/Sections/0768.20.html
   - https://www.leg.state.fl.us/Statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0700-0799/0768/Sections/0768.18.html

2. **Florida’s ordinary wrongful death limitations period.** Florida Statute § 95.11(5)(e) places wrongful death actions in the two-year limitations period; the statute also includes a distinct provision addressing certain intentional torts resulting in death.
   - https://www.leg.state.fl.us/Statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0000-0099/0095/Sections/0095.11.html

3. **Duval County court structure.** Duval County is part of Florida’s Fourth Judicial Circuit. The official circuit page lists the Duval County Courthouse at 501 West Adams Street, Jacksonville, and identifies Clay, Duval, and Nassau as the circuit’s counties.
   - https://www.jud4.org/news/2021-03-30-welcome-to-the-official-website-of-the-4th-judicial-circuit-court
