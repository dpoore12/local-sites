## Read before filing

- **What the listings say.** Every description ends with the same line the sites carry on every page: independently operated advertising, not itself a contractor or hauler. The sites make no license, insurance, years or review claims, so the listings don't either. Leave every such field blank.
- **Two of these are for working providers.** HVACServiceFast is a "register as a pro" form, and RentADumpster.co tells homeowners "We are not in the middle" and lists haulers by yard. Filing an advertising site there is a mismatch with what they're for. NearSpotUSA and BizHwy are general directories with no such premise. Whether to file the first two before a renter signs is Dan's call.
- **No photos.** The site photos are AI-made stand-ins. Don't upload them as "photos of your work".
- **Contact details** (not shown publicly; used to verify): use one inbox for all 27, for example a desk address. NearSpotUSA and HVACServiceFast also want a first and last name: the person filing.
- **Phone checks.** RentADumpster.co goes live once the number is confirmed. The numbers ring the call-desk voicemail, so a code read out by an automated call is in the Telnyx recording.
- **Hours:** none of the sites state hours. Leave them as "not specified".

## Directory by directory

**NearSpotUSA** (all 10): Business name, Category, ZIP (one), Phone, Website, Description. Leave Street address and Business email blank. Untick the marketing-email box.

**BizHwy** (all 10): Business name, Category, State, then the next step. Pick the closest subcategory there (for example Waste Management, Duct Cleaning, Window Tinting, Septic). If it requires a street line, stop and note it.

**HVACServiceFast** (3): First and last name, Company name, Website, Email, Phone; tick the services below; Service Area: pick the state, then tick each town; About your business: the description. Leave Address, Google Maps link, Years of experience, License number, "I am insured", logo and photos empty.

**RentADumpster.co** (4): make the account, search for the business name (it won't be there), choose "Add it": company name, phone, yard location (stop if it insists on a street address), then the service ZIPs below.

## Where the ZIPs come from

The towns are the ones each site names in its own "where we serve" copy. ZIPs are from the Census Bureau's 2020 ZIP-to-town relationship file: a ZIP is listed if it covers at least 3% of the town's land or a quarter of the ZIP lies inside the town, which drops P.O.-box-only ZIPs and keeps the edges. Added by mailing name, because Census doesn't treat them as places: 30068 (East Cobb) and 32533 (Cantonment). Perdido Key falls in 32507. Dropped: 34240, the Sarasota County side of Lakewood Ranch, since the Bradenton site serves Manatee County. Data: `kyle-screen/oct-top3/site_zips_census2020.json`.
