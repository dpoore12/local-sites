## Virginia statutes

- **Med mal total damages cap — Va. Code 8.01-581.15.** Caps "the total amount recoverable for any injury to, or death of, a patient" in a verdict/judgment against a health care provider. Schedule: $2.70 million for acts July 1, 2025–June 30, 2026; **$2.75 million for acts July 1, 2026–June 30, 2027** (the current year as of Aug 2026); $2.80M (2027-28), $2.85M, $2.90M, $2.95M (through June 30, 2031); **$3 million for acts on or after July 1, 2031.** Steps are $50,000/year from 2012 onward. Each annual increase applies to acts occurring on or after the effective date of the increase. https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter21.1/section8.01-581.15/
- **Expert certification — Va. Code 8.01-20.1.** Requesting service of process is deemed a certification that plaintiff already holds a signed written expert opinion that the defendant deviated from the standard of care and the deviation proximately caused the injuries claimed. Each certified defendant must be identified. Not needed where the alleged negligence lies within the jury's common knowledge. Within 21 days of an answer, plaintiff must certify compliance. Sanctions under 8.01-271.1 and possible dismissal with prejudice. Amended 2025, c. 359. https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter3/section8.01-20.1/
- **Review panel request — Va. Code 8.01-581.2.** Either side may request a medical malpractice review panel within 30 days of the filing of the responsive pleading. Request goes to the Clerk of the Supreme Court of Virginia with the pleadings; a copy to the circuit court clerk and all counsel. Supreme Court designates a panel within 60 days of receipt. Action is stayed during review. https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter21.1/section8.01-581.2/
- **Panel composition — Va. Code 8.01-581.3.** Two impartial attorneys, two impartial health care providers licensed and actively practicing in the Commonwealth, plus a circuit judge who presides, has no vote and need not attend deliberations. Selected by the Supreme Court from Board of Medicine and Virginia State Bar lists. https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter21.1/section8.01-581.3/
- **Panel opinion — Va. Code 8.01-581.7.** Within 30 days after receiving all evidence the panel renders one or more of four listed opinions; written, signed, filed with the clerk and mailed to the parties within five days. https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter21.1/section8.01-581.7/
- **Panel opinion admissible — Va. Code 8.01-581.8.** Admissible as evidence but not conclusive; either party may call any panelist except the judge as a witness at his own cost; panelists have absolute civil immunity. https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter21.1/section8.01-581.8/
- **Standard of care — Va. Code 8.01-581.20.** Statewide standard (a reasonably prudent practitioner in the field or specialty in the Commonwealth); a locality standard applies only if a party proves by a preponderance that local services and customs make it more appropriate. Expert must have had active clinical practice in the defendant's specialty or a related field within one year of the alleged act. https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter21.1/section8.01-581.20/
- **Limitations — Va. Code 8.01-243.** (A) two years for personal injuries, "whatever the theory of recovery"; (B) **five years for injury to property**; (C) malpractice extensions: one year from discovery of a retained foreign object; one year where fraud, concealment or intentional misrepresentation prevented discovery; one year from communication of a diagnosis of malignant tumor, cancer, or an intracranial/intraspinal/spinal schwannoma; 10-year outer limit. https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter4/section8.01-243/
- **Contract limits — Va. Code 8.01-246.** (A)(2) five years on a contract in writing and signed by the party to be charged; (A)(4) three years on an unwritten or unsigned contract. (C) UCC 8.2-725 controls sale-of-goods contracts, except that products liability actions for injury to person follow 8.01-243. https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter4/section8.01-246/
- **UCC — Va. Code 8.2-725.** Four years to sue for breach of a contract for sale; breach of warranty accrues at tender of delivery unless the warranty explicitly extends to future performance. https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.2/chapter7/section8.2-725/
- **Punitive cap — Va. Code 8.01-38.1.** Total punitive damages against all defendants may not exceed $350,000; the jury is not advised of the limit and the judge reduces any excess. Applies including medical malpractice actions. https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter3/section8.01-38.1/
- **Ad damnum / amount sued for — Va. Code 8.01-379.1.** Any party may tell the jury the amount of damages sought in opening statement or closing argument, or both; the plaintiff may request less than the ad damnum. https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter13/section8.01-379.1/
- **Virginia Tort Claims Act cap — Va. Code 8.01-195.3.** Commonwealth liable for employee negligence within scope of employment; recovery capped at $100,000 for causes accruing on or after July 1, 1993, or the maximum limits of any liability policy in force, whichever is greater; no prejudgment interest and no punitive damages. https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter3/section8.01-195.3/
- **VTCA notice — Va. Code 8.01-195.6.** Claim against the Commonwealth is forever barred without a written statement of the nature of the claim, including time, place and the agency alleged liable, filed within one year of accrual, with the Director of the Division of Risk Management or the Attorney General (Commissioner of Highways where VDOT is the agency). Actual knowledge by those officials can substitute. Claimant bears the burden of proving receipt. https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter3/section8.01-195.6/
- **Locality notice — Va. Code 15.2-209.** Negligence claim against a county, city or town is forever barred without a written statement of the nature of the claim including time and place, filed within **six months** of accrual with the local attorney, chief executive or mayor. Actual knowledge substitute; claimant bears burden of proving receipt. https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title15.2/chapter2/section15.2-209/
- **Railroad carve-out — Va. Code 8.01-58.** In actions by employees against common carriers by railroad, contributory negligence does not bar recovery; damages are diminished in proportion to the employee's negligence. The Commonwealth's one legislated comparative-fault exception. https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title8.01/chapter3/section8.01-58/

## Virginia case law

- **Assumption of risk — Amusement Slides Corp. v. Lehmann, 217 Va. 815 (1977).** The standard is subjective: what the particular plaintiff in fact sees, knows, understands and appreciates. Assumption of risk is "venturousness" with two requirements: the nature and extent of the risk must be fully appreciated and the risk must be voluntarily incurred. Plaintiff assumed the risk of a fast slide ride but not the hazard created by the operator's employee failing to water the waxed slide. https://law.justia.com/cases/virginia/supreme-court/1977/760300-1.html
- **Premises duty / open and obvious — Fobbs v. Webb Building Ltd. Partnership, 349 S.E.2d 355 (Va. 1986).** An owner owes an invitee (1) ordinary care to have the premises in a reasonably safe condition consistent with the invitation and (2) ordinary care to warn of any unsafe condition known or which should have been known, "except that the owner has no duty to warn its invitee of an unsafe condition which is open and obvious to a reasonable person exercising ordinary care for his own safety." https://law.justia.com/cases/virginia/supreme-court/1986/831575-1.html
- **Constructive notice — Memco Stores, Inc. v. Yeatman, 232 Va. 50 (1986).** Store owed ordinary care to an invitee, including removing foreign objects from its floors within a reasonable time; plaintiff need not prove actual notice, constructive notice suffices. https://law.justia.com/cases/virginia/supreme-court/1986/830796-1.html
- **Products proof — Logan v. Montgomery Ward, 216 Va. 425 (1975).** Whatever the theory, plaintiff must show the goods were unreasonably dangerous for ordinary use or for a special use brought to the defendant's attention, and that the condition existed when the goods left the defendant's hands. Res ipsa loquitur does not apply to an unexplained accident attributable to one of several causes for some of which the defendant is not responsible. https://law.justia.com/cases/virginia/supreme-court/1975/741053-1.html
- **Lex loci / Virginia Beach circuit filing — Hazelwood v. Lawyer Garage, LLC, Record No. 1374-23-1 (Va. Ct. App. Aug. 6, 2024), from the Circuit Court of the City of Virginia Beach.** Published opinion; used only as evidence that vehicle-repair and out-of-state-injury choice-of-law fights are litigated in this courthouse. https://law.justia.com/cases/virginia/court-of-appeals-published/2024/1374-23-1.html

## Federal

- **28 U.S.C. 2401(b).** A tort claim against the United States is forever barred unless presented in writing to the appropriate federal agency within two years of accrual, or unless action is begun within six months after the mailing of notice of final denial. https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2401&num=0&edition=prelim
- **28 U.S.C. 2675.** (a) No suit until the claim has been presented to the agency and finally denied in writing by certified or registered mail; agency failure to dispose of the claim within six months may be treated as a denial at the claimant's option. (b) The action may not be instituted for any sum in excess of the amount of the claim presented, except on newly discovered evidence not reasonably discoverable at presentation, or allegation and proof of intervening facts. https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2675&num=0&edition=prelim

## Virginia Beach local

- **City notice of claim procedure.** The City Attorney's Office states that 15.2-209 requires written notice within six months, delivered to the Office of the City Attorney, containing claimant name and address, incident date, individuals involved if known, location, and a description of what happened and why the City is contended to be responsible; mailing address 2401 Courthouse Drive, Virginia Beach, VA 23456. The City Attorney forwards it to Risk Management, which investigates and decides whether payment will be made voluntarily. https://attorney.virginiabeach.gov/file-a-notice-of-claim
- **Circuit Court civil practice.** The court's goal is that all civil cases conclude within 18 months of filing, exceptional cases aside. A pretrial conference under Rules 1:19 and 4:13 is required in all civil jury trial cases. A completed civil cover sheet must be attached to the initial pleading to assist filing-fee assessment and indexing. For actions originating in circuit court, counsel arranges the court reporter and files a praecipe to set a trial date. https://courts.virginiabeach.gov/circuit-court-clerks-office/circuit-court-civil
- **General District Court.** $50,000 jurisdictional limit for personal injury and wrongful death; civil filing costs published by the clerk. https://courts.virginiabeach.gov/general-district-court/civil-division
- **NAS Oceana.** Commissioned August 17, 1943; more than seven miles of runways; manned by over 14,600 military personnel and 2,000 civilians; home to 19 fighter/attack squadrons. https://cnrma.cnic.navy.mil/Installations/NAS-Oceana/About/History/
- **Tourism scale.** 14.3 million visitors in 2024; visitor spending up 2% to $2.6 billion; $3.9 billion total economic impact; 34,076 jobs, 19% of all jobs in the city; day visitors 58.8% of volume; lodging 27% of spending; food and beverage nearly $1.1 billion (42.6%). https://virginiabeach.gov/connect/news/tourism-continues-to-fuel-economic-growth-in-virginia-beach-with-3-9b-total-impact-in-2024
- **Oceanfront district (from site.json, sourced).** Three-mile boardwalk, Atlantic and Pacific Avenue corridors, more than 40 beachfront hotels and resorts. https://planning.virginiabeach.gov/zoning/form-based-code/oceanfront-resort-district
