## Santa Clara County Superior Court

South County Courthouse (301 Diana Ave, Morgan Hill) covers San Martin, Morgan Hill, Gilroy. - Misdemeanor flow and speedy-trial: Arrest → Arraignment → Pretrial → Trial; PC 1382 trial within 30 days of arraignment/plea if in custody, 45 days if out; after a time waiver, within 10 days of the date set. — https://santaclara.courts.ca.gov/divisions/criminal-division/misdemeanors
- Felony flow: Arraignment → Preliminary Hearing → Trial; Information filed within 15 days of holding order; trial within 60 days of arraignment on the Information. — https://santaclara.courts.ca.gov/divisions/criminal-division/felonies
- Criminal division locations (HOJ, Family Justice Center for Drug Court, Palo Alto, South County). — https://santaclara.courts.ca.gov/divisions/criminal-division
- Hearing times memo (effective January 18, 2022): out-of-custody misdemeanor arraignments A–M 9:00 am, N–Z 1:30 pm Mon–Fri; out-of-custody felony arraignments 1:35 pm; in-custody misdemeanor on-views 1:30 pm; felony on-views 1:35 pm. — https://www.santaclaraca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/76133/637776012184830000
- Warrant policies: bench-warrant self-calendaring, effective May 2023, warrant stays active until the new appearance; arrest-warrant out-of-custody arraignment protocol effective November 2023, scheduled through counsel, second or fourth Monday of each month, Felony Arraignment Department, Hall of Justice, 190 W. Hedding Street, at 1:46 p.m.; all charges eligible. — https://santaclara.courts.ca.gov/system/files/news/press-release-new-policies-bench-warrants-and-arrest-warrant-arraignments_0.pdf
- 2026 Criminal Bail Schedule: VC 23152 no priors $5,000 (BAC under 0.15) / $10,000 (0.15+); one prior $15,000/$20,000; two priors $35,000/$40,000; three priors (felony) $100,000; VC 23153 no priors $25,000; 23153 one prior $50,000; 23152 with 23550.5 $100,000. "In any DUI case, including those alleging prior convictions, defendants SHALL BE ELIGIBLE FOR S.O.R.P. RELEASE," with pretrial services advised to consider alcohol monitoring and/or testing. — https://santaclara.courts.ca.gov/system/files/criminal/2026-criminal-bail-schedule.pdf
- Court DUI program brochure: enroll within 30 days of the court appearance or risk a warrant, added penalties, driving-privilege impact; court and DMV notified within 10 days of completion; first-offender three-month (30 hours), six-month (44 hours), nine-month (60 hours) tracks; wet-reckless 12-hour program; first offenders may enroll pre-court using the Administrative Per Se form (pink temporary license); "No one is denied services for documented inability to pay"; programs licensed by CA Dept. of Health Care Services. — https://santaclara.courts.ca.gov/system/files/general/ddp-brochure-english.pdf
- Collaborative Courts: Drug Treatment Court, Mental Health Treatment Court, YADEJ (18–21, Dept 62), mental health diversion (proceedings suspended up to two years), IST Court. — https://santaclara.courts.ca.gov/divisions/collaborative-courts

## DMV
- San Jose Driver Safety Office: 90 Great Oaks Blvd., Suite 104, San Jose 95119; (833) 543-7703; Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri 8:00 am–5:00 pm, Wed 9:00 am–5:00 pm; middle building (Building 90) of a three-building complex, parking entrance on the south side of Great Oaks Boulevard between San Ignacio Avenue and Via Del Oro; no license or registration services; online case management to receive notices, schedule hearings, upload documents. — https://qr.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-education-and-safety/driver-safety-offices/
- DUI page: administrative action against the driving privilege only, independent of court penalties; hearing must be requested within 10 days of receiving the order; a reduction to reckless driving does not affect the suspension; an acquittal can reverse it. — https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-education-and-safety/dmv-safety-guidelines-actions/driving-under-the-influence/
- Statewide IID Pilot Program (VC 23575.3), violations Jan 1, 2019–Dec 31, 2032: 0 priors non-injury none / injury 1 year; 1 prior 1 year / 2 years; 2 priors 2 / 3; 3+ priors 3 / 3; prior felony 3 / 4. First-time offenders: court may order IID up to six months; or apply for an IID restriction up to six months; or a work-and-program restriction for one year. Wet reckless: court may order IID minimum three months. Install to drive immediately instead of serving the suspension. Requirements: clear other suspensions, DL 920, DL 107 or DL 101, SR 22, reissue/restriction and administrative service fees. Exemptions DL 4062 / medical DL 4063. — https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-education-and-safety/dmv-safety-guidelines-actions/driving-under-the-influence/statewide-ignition-interlock-device-pilot-program/

## Statutes and regulations
- VC 23152(a) under the influence; (b) 0.08 percent or more by weight; percent by weight = grams per 100 milliliters of blood or per 210 liters of breath; rebuttable presumption when a chemical test within three hours after driving shows 0.08 or more. — https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=VEH&sectionNum=23152
- VC 23612 implied consent: deemed consent to blood or breath testing on lawful arrest under 23140/23152/23153; testing incidental to a lawful arrest; admonition that refusal brings a fine and mandatory imprisonment on conviction, one-year administrative suspension, two-year revocation with one qualifying prior (including a violation of Section 23103 as specified in Section 23103.5), three-year revocation with two or more. — https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=VEH&sectionNum=23612
- 17 CCR 1221.1: breath sample only after fifteen continuous minutes with no ingestion of alcohol or other fluids, regurgitation, vomiting, eating or smoking. — https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/california/17-CCR-1221.1
- 17 CCR 1221.2: two separate samples differing by no more than 0.02 grams per 210 liters; accuracy checks within plus or minus 0.010 grams percent across 0.08 to 0.30 grams percent; "periodic" means not exceeding 10 days or 150 subjects, whichever comes sooner; per-instrument records; precautionary checklist at each instrument location. — https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/california/17-CCR-1221.2
- 17 CCR 1219.1: blood by venipuncture as soon as feasible, no alcohol or volatile disinfectant on the skin, anticoagulant and preservative, remaining sample retained one year and a portion provided to the defendant on request. — https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/california/17-CCR-1219.1
- 17 CCR 1219.2: urine collected no sooner than twenty minutes after first voiding, preserved, retained one year. — https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/california/17-CCR-1219.2
- PC 1001.95 judicial misdemeanor diversion (excluded offenses do not name DUI). — https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=1001.95
