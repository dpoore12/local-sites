## Arizona MVD (license)

Source: https://azdot.gov/mvd/services/dui-suspension/suspension-revocation and
https://azdot.gov/mvd/services/driver-services/license-reinstatement/fees

- Suspension fee **$10**; revocation reinstatement **$20**; Admin Per Se suspension **additional $50**
- Application fee by age: **$10** (50+), **$15** (45–49), **$20** (40–44), **$25** (39 and younger, or any age with a Travel ID)
- After a DUI, drug-test refusal or insurance violation: SR-22 future financial responsibility proof required

## Arithmetic published on the page

Minimum first offense: $250 fine × 1.68 = $420, plus $13 (12-116.04), plus the two $500
assessments = **≈ $1,433** in court money, before jail costs, screening, treatment, interlock and
MVD charges. Page states that reducing a fine from $400 to $250 moves the total far less than
readers expect, because the largest items are fixed and surcharge-exempt.
