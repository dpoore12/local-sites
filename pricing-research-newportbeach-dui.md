| Contingent fee in a criminal case | "A lawyer shall not make an agreement for, charge, or collect: ... a contingent fee for representing a defendant in a criminal case." | Cal. Rules of Prof. Conduct 1.5(c)(2) — State Bar of California, Chapter 1 rules page | https://www.calbar.ca.gov/legal-professionals/rules/rules-professional-conduct/current-rules-professional-conduct/chapter-1-lawyer-client-relationship |
| "Earned on receipt" / non-refundable | Allowed only if the fee is a true retainer and the client agrees in writing after disclosure | same page (rule 1.5(d)) | same |
| Written fee contract | Required where total expense to the client is reasonably foreseeable to exceed **$1,000**; duplicate signed copy to client; must state basis of compensation, nature of services, respective responsibilities | Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code 6148(a) | https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=6148 |

**Note on the brief's citation:** the brief specified "California RPC 1.5(d)". In the current
California rule the criminal contingent-fee prohibition sits at **1.5(c)(2)** — subdivision (c)
holds the two prohibited contingent fees (family law and criminal), and Comment [1] to the rule
expressly refers to "Paragraph (c)(1)" as the family-law item. Subdivision (d) is the
"earned on receipt" / true-retainer provision. The page therefore cites 1.5(c)(2).

## Orange County court schedule (the local differentiator vs. San Jose)

Source: **Orange County Superior Court 2026 Uniform Bail Schedule** (adopted under Penal Code
1269b(c), effective January 1, 2026) — https://www.occourts.org/system/files?file=criminal/felonybailsched.pdf

- Misdemeanor VC 23152(a)/(b): 1st **$2,500**, 2nd **$10,000**, 3rd **$15,000**, 4th (felony) **$50,000**
- Felony 23152: **$50,000**; VC 23153 (injury): **$100,000**
- Schedule states bail on these counts is **not** doubled under VC 42009

## Arithmetic published on the page

On the statutory minimum $390 base fine: state penalty $390 (1464) + Orange county penalty
39 × $5.29 = $206.31 (76000) + 20% surcharge $78 (1465.7) + court construction 39 × $5 = $195
(70372) + DNA $39 + $156 (76104.6, 76104.7) + $30 (70373) + $40 (1465.8) = **≈ $1,524 total on a
$390 fine**, i.e. roughly four times the base fine. Described on the page as "roughly $1,500",
"about a factor of four", with the county-option $2 per $10 EMS penalty flagged as an addition.
