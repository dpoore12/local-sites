## Notes and limits

- The Los Angeles Superior Court publishes its civil fee schedule as a PDF in the court's
  own publication storage (lascpubstorage.blob.core.windows.net), which is what
  lacourt.ca.gov links to. The court's public fee page
  (https://www.lacourt.ca.gov/pages/cp/filing-fees, retrieved 2026-08-23) renders no
  amounts to a fetcher, and the older copy at
  https://www.lacourt.org/forms/pdf/fees/fee-schedule-2020_rev2.pdf (retrieved 2026-08-23)
  confirms the same $435 / $60 / $1,000 figures.
- The court reporter per diem figures ($30 / $382 / $764) and the $15-plus-mileage daily
  jury deposit are Los Angeles-specific line items and were read off the LA schedule.
- Could not verify a 2026-dated Los Angeles schedule; the newest one the court publishes
  is dated Jan. 1, 2024, and the statewide amounts in it match the statewide schedule
  effective Jan. 1, 2026.
- California Rule 1.5 sets no percentage cap. § 6146's 25%/33% ceilings are medical
  malpractice only and are labeled that way on the page.
