# Eden Prairie phase 2 — verified research notes (fetched Aug 25, 2026)

## Climate (NWS / MN DNR)
- Twin Cities all-time coldest observed low: -41 F on January 21, 1888. Winter average of 19 nights at or below 0 F; record 58 sub-zero lows in winter 1874-75; average winter (Dec-Feb) temperature 20.4 F. — https://www.weather.gov/media/mpx/Climate/What%20Winter%20MSP.pdf
- MSP 1991-2020 January normals: normal daily maximum 23.6 F, normal daily minimum 8.8 F, normal mean 16.2 F, 10.0 nights with minimum at or below 0 F, 22.7 days with maximum at or below 32 F, 11.0 inches snowfall. — https://files.dnr.state.mn.us/natural_resources/climate/twin_cities/msp_normals_means_extremes_page3.pdf
- January normal mean rose from 15.6 F (1981-2010) to 16.2 F (1991-2020). — https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/climate/journal/twin-cities-normals-1991-2020-compared-1981-2010.html
- Frost depth, Maplewood volunteer frost tube (frost under sod): deepest 35.2 in (2007-08); median maximum 20.0 in; median frost in Nov 23, frost out April 10; about four inches of fluffy snow forms effective insulation, deep snowpack can leave little or no frost. — https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/climate/journal/frost-depth-minnesota-winter-2025.html

## Codes and rules
- Minnesota State Building Code "is the minimum construction standard throughout all of Minnesota including all cities, townships and counties." — https://www.dli.mn.gov/business/codes-and-laws/overview-minnesota-state-building-code
- Footing depth due to freezing: minimum 5 feet in Zone I, 3-1/2 feet in Zone II; Hennepin County is listed in Zone II; lesser depths only with supporting evidence from an engineer competent in soil mechanics. — https://www.revisor.mn.gov/rules/1303.1600/
- Residential energy code: garages grouped with attics and ventilated crawl spaces for duct insulation (R-8 and V) in Table R403.2.1; "Any nonconditioned space that is altered to become conditioned space shall be brought into full compliance with this code." No provision requires an attached garage to be insulated or conditioned. — https://www.revisor.mn.gov/rules/1322/full
- Climate zone 6 envelope values in Table R402.1.1 (ceiling R-49, wood frame wall R-20 or 13+5). — https://www.revisor.mn.gov/rules/1322.0402/

## Licensing (MN DLI)
- A residential building contractor or remodeler license is required of anyone contracting directly with an owner of one-to-four-unit residential real estate to provide work in more than one special skill. Carpentry as a special skill covers "doors, windows and skylights." "Specialty contractors who provide only one special skill are not required to have a state license (except residential roofers)." Exemption at under $15,000 gross annual receipts with a Certificate of Exemption. — https://www.dli.mn.gov/business/residential-contractors/residential-contractor-licensing
- "Cities and counties cannot require a contractor who has a state license to obtain a local license for work that is covered under the state licensing requirements." — https://www.dli.mn.gov/business/residential-contractors/residential-contractor-faqs

## Eden Prairie
- "Building permits are required anytime you do work that is regulated by the Minnesota State Building Code. This includes any work to the permanent weather resistive surfaces of the dwelling such as the siding, windows and roof. Any structural alterations or repairs to those items require a permit." All work subject to inspection; permits expire after 180 days without work or inspections; code allows fees to be doubled if work starts before a permit; electrical inspections are conducted by the State of Minnesota; Building Inspections 952-949-8342. — https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/doing-business/building-inspections
- Building permits are paperless through the ePermits portal. — https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/doing-business/building-inspections/licenses-and-permits
- Population: 6,967 (1970), 16,263 (1980), 39,311 (1990), 54,901 (2000). — https://socds.huduser.gov/Census/totalpop.odb?msacitylist=5120.0*2700018116*0.0&metro=msa
- Population 739 in 1880 and 2,000 in 1960; Flying Cloud Airport 1946; I-494 and Eden Prairie Center in the mid-1970s. — https://www.edenprairiemn.gov/community/about-eden-prairie/eden-prairie-history
- The Preserve: first land purchase 1971 (Nesbitt farmland), association incorporated May 1972. — https://preserveassociation.com/history-of-the-preserve/

## Trade / federal
- 16 CFR part 1211: applies to residential operators manufactured on or after January 1, 1993; a residential garage door operator serves a building of one to four single-family units, rated 600 volts or less, per NFPA 70. Except for the first 1 foot of travel from full up, a downward-moving door must begin reversing within 2 seconds of contact and return to and stop at the full upmost position. Test obstruction on the floor is a solid object 1 inch high; operator exerts 25 lbf or its rated pull, whichever is greater; 50 open-and-close cycles. Systems that do not monitor door position must reverse to full up if the lower limit is not reached within 30 seconds of starting the close cycle. Switch/relay in an entrapment circuit must withstand 100,000 cycles. — https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2023-title16-vol2/pdf/CFR-2023-title16-vol2-part1211.pdf
- ANSI/DASMA 102: minimum 10,000-cycle design (6.1); a cycle is closed to fully open and back to closed (2.2); residential door normally expected to operate under 1,500 cycles per year (2.8). — https://law.resource.org/pub/us/cfr/regulations.gov.docket.15/dasma.102.2004.pdf
- DASMA TDS #190, spring cycle life: doors in climates subject to extreme heat or cold, frequent precipitation, or coastal salt should be kept closed where possible; regular greasing/lubrication per manufacturer instructions helps maximize cycle life; rust reduces the effective area of spring wire and creates corrosion pits from which fatigue cracks accelerate. — https://www.dasma.com/wp-content/uploads/pubs/TechDataSheets/CommercialResidential/TDS190.pdf
- DASMA TDS #195, condensation: condensation is water collecting on a cold surface when humid air contacts it; higher interior humidity can come from a wet or snow-covered car; ice formation on glazing in extreme cold is outside the sheet's scope and care should be used if ice is mechanically scraped off. — https://www.dasma.com/wp-content/uploads/pubs/TechDataSheets/CommercialResidential/TDS195.pdf
- DASMA TDS #163: R-value applies to a door section only and is calculated from a k-factor traced to ASTM C177 or C518 testing at an average material temperature of 40 F; air films total R-0.85; worked example yields a section R-value of 16.47; U-factor is measured on the whole installed assembly under ANSI/DASMA 105; U-factor and R-value are not reciprocals and a section R-value should not be used for a door assembly. — https://www.dasma.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/TDS163..pdf
- Cold storage of lithium-ion cathodes (below 0 C) led cells to lose up to 5 percent more capacity after 100 charges than warmer-stored cells, cracking cathode particles. — https://www6.slac.stanford.edu/news/2021-08-26-how-extreme-cold-can-crack-lithium-ion-battery-materials-degrading-performance
