## Context figures used in the body copy (already sourced in site.json local_facts)

- NWS Twin Cities: 19 days per winter at or below 0F lows, 55 days at or below 32F highs
- Minnesota Residential Energy Code treats a garage as unconditioned space and requires full code
  compliance when a nonconditioned space is altered to become conditioned
- Eden Prairie ePermits portal; permits required for work regulated by the Minnesota State
  Building Code, including structural alterations and repairs to permanent weather-resistive surfaces

## Not verified

- Eden Prairie's current-year fee schedule sits in a cookie-gated Laserfiche portal
  (documents.edenprairie.org) that returns "Cookies are not enabled for this website" to a script,
  and the city's Building Inspections pages publish no fee amounts. The anchor therefore uses the
  most recent Chapter 25 fee ordinance retrievable from the city's Municode ordinance library
  (Ordinance 18-2020, effective January 1, 2021). Amounts may have been revised in a later annual
  fee ordinance; the page attributes the figures to the Chapter 25 fee schedule rather than to a
  current year.
- Minn. Stat. 326B.148 also shows a $5 minimum that applied July 1, 2010 through June 30, 2015;
  the page uses the general rule ($1 or 0.0005) that reads as current in the statute text.
