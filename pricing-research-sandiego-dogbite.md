# Pricing research — sandiegodogbitelawyerpros.com (San Diego, CA — dog bite)

Mode: `fees` / `fee_kind: contingency`. All sources retrieved **2026-08-23**.

## Bottom line on caps

**California sets no percentage cap on a contingency fee in a dog-bite case.** The page says this plainly. The only
limits are (1) the unconscionability prohibition in rule 1.5(a)-(b) of the California Rules of Professional Conduct and
(2) the mandatory written-contract disclosures in Business and Professions Code section 6147. The 25% / 33% schedule in
B&P 6146 is **medical negligence only** and is deliberately NOT used on this page.

## San Diego dog licensing (the city/county amounts on the page)

Sources: https://sdhumane.org/services/dog-licensing/ and https://www.sddac.com/content/sdc/das/license-laws/license.html

- Altered dog: $20 (1 yr), $35 (2 yr), $50 (3 yr).
- Unaltered dog: $60 / $100 / $150.
- Late fee $25; change of ownership $15; transfer $15; replacement tag $15.
- Food & Agric. Code 30804.5 requires the altered-dog fee to be no more than half the unaltered fee;
  30804.7 authorizes fines starting at $150/day for failure to license.
- Health & Safety Code 121690 sets the licensing/rabies deadlines; San Diego County Code 62.610 and 62.620 are the
  local licensing provisions.
