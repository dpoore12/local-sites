## Notes and limits

- Virginia is unlike California in structure but the same in result on the central point:
  Rule 1.5 imposes a reasonableness standard and disclosure duties, not a percentage
  ceiling, for injury work. Virginia's rule is stricter than California's in one respect
  worth naming: it bans nonrefundable advanced legal fees outright and requires a written
  closing statement at the end of a contingent matter.
- § 8.01-581.15 caps the **recovery** in a medical malpractice action, not the fee. Labeled
  that way on the page.
- Could not verify a single "personal injury filing fee" figure for Virginia Beach: the
  clerk's total is assembled from the § 17.1-275 clerk fee, the § 58.1-1727 writ tax and
  the local line items above, and the court's published schedule expresses it as lettered
  fee-component rows rather than a named injury-case total. The court also directs filers
  to a civil filing fee calculator (https://selfhelp.vacourts.gov/page/14/filing-fees-waivers,
  retrieved 2026-08-23), which publishes no static amount. The page therefore states the
  statutory components rather than a single total.
