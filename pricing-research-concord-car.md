# Pricing research — concordcaraccidentlawyerpros.com (Concord, Cabarrus County, NC)

Mode: `fees`, fee_kind `contingency`. Retrieval date for every item below: **2026-08-23**.

## Headline finding

North Carolina sets **no numeric percentage ceiling** on a contingent fee in a motor
vehicle injury claim. The governing limit is the "clearly excessive" reasonableness
standard in Rule 1.5(a) of the N.C. Rules of Professional Conduct, applied through eight
enumerated factors, plus the written-agreement requirements of Rule 1.5(c). Numeric
ceilings elsewhere in North Carolina law are procedural (court costs) or apply to other
claimants (medical provider liens) — not to the fee percentage itself.

## Could not verify / deliberately excluded

  dollar jurisdictional threshold is stated on the page.
