## The fee standard: unconscionability, not a cap

Source: **State Bar of California, Rules of Professional Conduct, Rule 1.5** —
https://www.calbar.ca.gov/legal-professionals/rules/rules-professional-conduct/current-rules-professional-conduct/chapter-1-lawyer-client-relationship
(retrieved 2026-08-23).

Rule 1.5(a) bars an agreement for, charge, or collection of an **unconscionable
or illegal** fee. Rule 1.5(b) lists 13 non-exclusive factors, among them the
amount of the fee **in proportion to the value of the services performed**
(b)(3), the relative sophistication of lawyer and client (b)(4), whether the
lawyer failed to disclose material facts (b)(2), and whether the client gave
informed consent to the fee (b)(13). Rule 1.5(c) prohibits a contingent fee in a
family law matter tied to the dissolution or to support, and for a criminal
defendant. No California percentage ceiling applies to an animal-attack claim.

## B&P 6147 — what a contingency contract has to contain

Source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=6147.&lawCode=BPC
(retrieved 2026-08-23). Verified language used on the page:

- 6147(a)(1): "A statement of the contingency fee rate that the client and
  attorney have agreed upon."
- 6147(a)(4): unless the claim falls under Section 6146, "a statement that the
  fee is not set by law but is negotiable between attorney and client."
- 6147(b): "Failure to comply with any provision of this section renders the
  agreement voidable at the option of the plaintiff, and the attorney shall
  thereupon be entitled to collect a reasonable fee."

## B&P 6148 — the $1,000 writing threshold for non-contingency work

Source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=6148.&lawCode=BPC
(retrieved 2026-08-23).

- 6148(a): in any case not within Section 6147 where it is reasonably foreseeable
  that total expense to the client, including attorney fees, will exceed **one
  thousand dollars ($1,000)**, the contract for services **shall be in writing**,
  and a duplicate signed copy must be given to the client. It must state the
  basis of compensation (hourly rates, statutory fees, flat fees and other
  standard rates and charges), the general nature of the services, and the
  respective responsibilities of attorney and client.
- 6148(b): every bill must clearly state its basis; on request the attorney must
  provide a bill within **10 days**, subject to the 31-day provisions, and the
  client may repeat the request at intervals of no less than **30 days**.
- 6148(c): non-compliance makes the agreement **voidable at the client's option**,
  leaving the attorney a reasonable fee.

## California Rule of Court 7.955 — a child's claim

Source: **California Rules of Court, Rule 7.955** —
https://www.courts.ca.gov/cms/rules/index.cfm?title=seven&linkid=rule7_955
(retrieved 2026-08-23).

- 7.955(a)(1): in all cases under Code of Civil Procedure section 372 or Probate
  Code sections 3600-3601, unless the court approved the fee agreement in
  advance, **the court must use a reasonable fee standard** when approving and
  allowing attorney fees payable from money paid for the benefit of a minor or a
  person with a disability.
- 7.955(a)(2): the court must consider the terms of the representation agreement
  and evaluate it on the facts existing when it was made.
- 7.955(b): **14** non-exclusive factors, including the fact that a minor is
  involved, the fee in proportion to the value of services, the amount involved
  and results obtained, the time and labor required, informed consent, whether
  the fee is fixed, hourly or contingent, and — if contingent — the risk of loss
  borne by the attorney, the costs advanced, and the delay in payment.
- 7.955(c): a petition for approval requires an attorney's declaration.

license application** —
https://animalcare.saccounty.gov/Licensing/Documents/AnimalLicenseApp.pdf
(retrieved 2026-08-23). The application cites **Sacramento County Code
8.24.030/8.25.030** as the licensing and rabies requirement.

Unincorporated Sacramento County residents:

| License type | 1 year | 2 years | 3 years |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Intact animal | **$50** | $100 | $150 |
| Altered animal | **$15** | $30 | $40 |
| Owner 62 or older, altered pet, reduced rate | $10 | $20 | $25 |

Late fee **$25.00**. Duplicate tag **$5**. Reduced unaltered license **$45 per
year** for a qualifying competition animal or working ranch dog. Cities of Galt
and Isleton: intact **$30**/year, altered **$10**/year, late fee **$15**.
