## California Rules of Professional Conduct 1.5 — unconscionability, 13 factors

Source: **State Bar of California, Rules of Professional Conduct, Chapter 1,
Rule 1.5 Fees for Legal Services** —
https://www.calbar.ca.gov/legal-professionals/rules/rules-professional-conduct/current-rules-professional-conduct/chapter-1-lawyer-client-relationship
(retrieved 2026-08-23).

- Rule 1.5(a): "A lawyer shall not make an agreement for, charge, or collect an
  unconscionable or illegal fee." California uses **unconscionable**, not the
  "unreasonable" wording used by Georgia, Nevada and the ABA model rule.
- Rule 1.5(b): unconscionability is judged on all the facts existing when the
  agreement is entered into, except where the parties contemplate the fee will be
  affected by later events, and **13** non-exclusive factors are listed: fraud or
  overreaching in setting the fee; failure to disclose material facts; the amount
  of the fee in proportion to the value of the services performed; the relative
  sophistication of lawyer and client; novelty and difficulty; whether the work
  precludes other employment; amount involved and results obtained; time
  limitations; nature and length of the relationship; experience, reputation and
  ability; whether the fee is fixed or contingent; time and labor required; and
  whether the client gave informed consent to the fee.
- Rule 1.5(c): no contingent fee in a family law matter contingent on securing a
  dissolution or nullity or on the amount of spousal or child support or property
  settlement in lieu of it; no contingent fee for representing a criminal
  defendant.
- Rule 1.5(d)-(e): a fee called "earned on receipt" or non-refundable is allowed
  only if it is a true retainer with written client agreement; a flat fee is a
  fixed amount that is complete payment for described services.

No California statute caps a contingent fee in a dog bite claim.

## Business and Professions Code 6147 — the contingency contract

Source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=6147.&lawCode=BPC
(retrieved 2026-08-23).

- 6147(a)(1): the contract must state the **contingency fee rate** the client and
  attorney agreed on.
- 6147(a)(4): unless the claim is subject to Section 6146, the contract must
  state that **the fee is not set by law but is negotiable** between attorney and
  client.
- 6147(a)(5): if the claim is subject to Section 6146, the contract must say the
  6146 rates are maximum limits and a lower rate may be negotiated.
- 6147(b): failure to comply with any provision **renders the agreement voidable
  at the option of the plaintiff**, and the attorney is then entitled only to a
  reasonable fee.

## Business and Professions Code 6146 — the one California percentage cap

Source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=6146.&lawCode=BPC
(retrieved 2026-08-23).

Applies only to a claim against a **health care provider** based on professional
negligence:

| Circumstance | Maximum contingency fee |
| --- | --- |
| Recovery under a settlement and release executed by all parties **before** a civil complaint or arbitration demand is filed | **25%** of the amount recovered |
| Recovery by settlement, arbitration or judgment **after** filing | **33%** of the amount recovered |
| Case tried in civil court or arbitrated | attorney may move for more than 33%, decided in the court's or arbitrator's discretion on evidence of good cause |

"Recovered" is the net sum after disbursements and costs; the plaintiff's medical
care costs and attorney office overhead are **not** deductible. A dog bite claim
against an owner is not covered by 6146.

## Los Angeles Municipal Code — dog license tax and fee

Sources (retrieved 2026-08-23):
- **LAMC 53.15.3 Dog License Fee** —
  https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/los_angeles/latest/lamc/0-0-0-136780
  Processing fee **$91.50**; **$16.50** for a spayed female, neutered male, or a
  dog a licensed veterinarian certifies cannot breed or be bred.
- **LAMC 53.15 Dog License Tax — Penalties** —
  https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/los_angeles/latest/lamc/0-0-0-136628
  Annual license tax **$8.50** per dog over four months; **$3.50** for a spayed,
  neutered or non-breeding dog. Late payment more than 45 days after the renewal
  date carries a late fee of **25% of the cost of the license**. Field collection
  at the property where the dog is kept: **$25.00**. Failure to license within 45
  days of the triggering event is a misdemeanor (LAMC 53.15.3(b)).
