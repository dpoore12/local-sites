## What actually unlocks page 1 (the hard 20%) — and why it's not a tactic

The authority-gate analysis ([AUTHORITY-GATE-2026-09-15.md](AUTHORITY-GATE-2026-09-15.md))
proved the gate is **not backlinks**. DR-0 sites hold page 1 here. The thing
they have that we don't is a **claimed Google Business Profile with reviews.**

A GBP is not a file we can generate. Google requires, and increasingly verifies
by **video**, that a real business exists:

1. **A real address in/near Savannah** — for verification. A PO box, UPS Store, or
   virtual office is explicitly banned and video verification catches it. A
   service-area business can *hide* the address publicly, but must still verify a
   real one. (Google's own guideline, google-search-essentials ch011.)
2. **A real business name** — "Savannah Mobile Mechanic Pros" is borderline
   keyword-stuffing under Google's name rule. A real operator's real business
   name is safer and ranks the same.
3. **A phone that rings a real person who does the work** — we have the number;
   it needs to route to whoever actually shows up and turns a wrench.
4. **Reviews from real customers** — can't be manufactured safely.

Every one of those points at the same missing ingredient: **a real local
operator.** Not a link, not content, not a citation. A person in Savannah who
does (or dispatches) mobile-mechanic work, whose real presence the GBP verifies.
