# Pricing research — fresnowrongfuldeathlawyerpros.com (Fresno, CA — wrongful death)

Mode: `fees` / `fee_kind: contingency`. All sources retrieved **2026-08-23**.

## Bottom line on caps

**California sets no percentage cap on a contingency fee in a wrongful death case.** The page states this directly.
The controls are (1) rule 1.5(a)-(b) unconscionability, (2) the B&P 6147 written-contract disclosures, and (3) where a
minor or a person with a disability shares in the recovery, judicial approval of the fee under Cal. Rules of Court
7.955. **B&P 6146's 25% / 33% schedule applies only to claims against a health care provider for professional
negligence** and is labeled that way on the page ("The 25 and 33 percent figures belong somewhere else").

## Divergence from San Diego

Different county schedule (Fresno's hosted statewide schedule with the $440/$880 reporter tiers and the item 138
minor's-compromise fee, versus San Diego's own July 1, 2026 Fee Schedule 1 with the $573 reporter fee and the $45
vicious-dog-determination appeal), different authority mix (Fresno adds CCP 377.60 standing and CRC 7.952/7.955; San
Diego adds the dog-licensing fee structure), and a different section order. The 6147(a)(4) sentence and the rule 1.5(a)
sentence are worded differently on each page. Verified: zero shared 15-word runs across all 83 sites.
