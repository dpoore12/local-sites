# Long Beach PI — Phase 2 research notes (all values from pages actually fetched)

## Longshore and Harbor Workers' Compensation Act (LHWCA)
- Program overview: https://www.dol.gov/agencies/owcp/dlhwc/lhwca
- Status test, 33 U.S.C. 902(3): "any person engaged in maritime employment, including any longshoreman ... harbor-worker including a ship repairman, shipbuilder, and ship-breaker"; exclusions (A)–(H) cover office clerical/secretarial/security/data processing, marina employees (not construction/expansion), suppliers/vendors temporarily on premises, aquaculture, recreational-vessel work, and "a master or member of a crew of any vessel". (A)–(F) apply only if state comp covers the person. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/33/902
- Situs test, 33 U.S.C. 903(a): injury "upon the navigable waters of the United States (including any adjoining pier, wharf, dry dock, terminal, building way, marine railway, or other adjoining area customarily used by an employer in loading, unloading, repairing, dismantling, or building a vessel)." https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/33/903
- 905(a) employer liability exclusive; 905(b) vessel negligence action against the vessel as third party under §933; no seaworthiness warranty basis; stevedoring-services limit; shipbuilding/repair employer cannot be sued as vessel owner; remedy exclusive against the vessel. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/33/905
- 906(a): no compensation for first three days of disability unless disability exceeds fourteen days. 906(b) max = 200% of NAWW, min for total disability = 50% NAWW. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/33/906
- 907(b) employee chooses an attending physician authorized by the Secretary; 907(c)(2) employer must authorize that choice, changes need consent; 907(d)(1) self-procured care not reimbursed unless employer refused/neglected; 907(d)(2) physician report within 10 days. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/33/907
- 908: total disability = 66 2/3% of AWW; 908(c) schedule — arm 312 weeks, leg 288, hand 244, foot 205, eye 160; hearing 52 (one ear) / 200 (both); disfigurement of face/head/neck up to $7,500. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/33/908
- 912(a): notice of injury within thirty days, given to the deputy commissioner for the compensation district and to the employer. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/33/912
- 913: claim within one year of injury/death. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/33/913
- 914: first installment due on the 14th day after notice; 10% penalty on late installments without award; 20% on award compensation more than 10 days overdue. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/33/914
- 928(a): carrier declining liability within 30 days of notice of claim and later losing pays a reasonable claimant fee in addition to compensation, lump sum to counsel after the order is final; 928(b) informal-conference route limits the fee to the difference between award and tender; otherwise not assessed against employer. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/33/928
- 933: (a) no election needed; (f) employer liable only for the excess over net third-party recovery; (g)(1) settlement for less than the compensation entitlement needs written approval of employer AND carrier before execution, filed within 30 days; (g)(2) without approval/notice "all rights to compensation and medical benefits under this chapter shall be terminated." https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/33/933
- FY2026 rates (10/01/2025–09/30/2026): NAWW $1,041.35; max compensation $2,082.70; min $520.68. https://www.dol.gov/agencies/owcp/dlhwc/NAWWinfo
- Fees: 20 C.F.R. 702.132(a) — itemized application (professional status, normal billing rate, hours), amount reasonably commensurate with necessary work, weighing quality of representation, complexity, and benefits awarded; "No contract pertaining to the amount of a fee shall be recognized." https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2018-title20-vol4/pdf/CFR-2018-title20-vol4-part702-subpartA-subjectgroup-id1244.pdf
- DOL district office list shows a Long Beach Suboffice (18) in the Western District. https://www.dol.gov/agencies/owcp/dlhwc/lscontac

## Jones Act / seaman status
- 46 U.S.C. 30104: seaman injured in the course of employment may elect a civil action at law with jury trial against the employer. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/46/30104
- Chandris, Inc. v. Latsis, 515 U.S. 347 (1995): duties must contribute to the vessel's function or mission; connection to a vessel in navigation substantial in duration and nature; "a worker who spends less than about 30 percent of his time in the service of a vessel in navigation should not qualify as a seaman" — a guideline, not a rule. https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/515/347/

## OSHA jurisdiction on marine terminals
- Cal/OSHA covers nearly all California workplaces; federal OSHA retains maritime employment on navigable waters — longshore operations on all vessels from the shore side of the means of access, ship repair/building/breaking afloat or in dry dock, floating fuel operations, diving from vessels afloat. Cal/OSHA keeps marine construction on shore and bridges. https://www.dir.ca.gov/dosh/calosha-jurisdiction.html
- Federal/California operational status agreement (2022): federal discretionary enforcement withheld for 29 CFR 1910/1915/1917/1926/1928 outside the retained carve-outs. https://www.dir.ca.gov/dosh/documents/FED-OSHA-CA-Operational-Status-Agreement-signed-2022.09.15.pdf
- OSHA CSP 01-03-001: states may assume all onshore longshoring/maritime; offshore stays federal. https://www.osha.gov/enforcement/directives/csp-01-03-001
- Employer must report a serious injury, illness, or death immediately (Labor Code 6409.1(b)). https://www.dir.ca.gov/dosh/EnforcementPage.htm

## California workers' compensation contrast
- 2026 TTD: max $1,764.11/week, min $264.61 (earnings thresholds $2,646.16 / $396.91); 2025 were $1,680.29 / $252.03; SAWW $1,704 → $1,789. https://www.dir.ca.gov/DIRNews/2025/2025-116.html
- TD = two-thirds of gross lost wages; 104 weeks within five years, 240 weeks for listed severe conditions (amputations, severe burns, chemical eye burns, high-velocity eye injuries, chronic lung disease). https://www.dir.ca.gov/injuredworkerguidebook/chapter5.pdf
- Labor Code 4600(a)/(c)/(d): employer provides treatment; own-physician choice only after 30 days from reporting and only absent a medical provider network under 4616; predesignation requires prior written notice plus nonoccupational coverage. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=4600.
- Labor Code 3600(a): comp liability "in lieu of any other liability whatsoever". https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=3600.&lawCode=LAB

## California civil layer
- Deadlines: personal injury 2 years (CCP 335.1); medical malpractice 1 year from discovery or 3 years from injury, whichever first, with 90 days' notice under CCP 364; property damage 3 years. https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/civil-lawsuit/statute-limitations
- MICRA noneconomic caps, Civ. Code 3333.2: $350,000 / $500,000 base for cases filed on or after Jan 1 2023, escalating $40,000 and $50,000 each January 1 toward $750,000 / $1,000,000; amount in effect at judgment governs. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=3333.2.&lawCode=CIV
- Proposition 213, Civ. Code 3333.4: noneconomic loss barred in specified motor vehicle cases. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=3333.4.&lawCode=CIV
- Gov. Code 911.2(a): six months for death/injury/personal property claims against a public entity. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=911.2.&lawCode=GOV
- Pure comparative negligence: Li v. Yellow Cab Co. (1975) 13 Cal.3d 804. https://law.justia.com/cases/california/supreme-court/3d/13/804.html
- Design defect: Barker v. Lull Engineering Co. (1978) 20 Cal.3d 413 — consumer-expectation test, plus risk-benefit test with the burden shifted to the defendant once the plaintiff shows the design proximately caused injury. https://scocal.stanford.edu/opinion/barker-v-lull-engineering-co-30437/
- Comparative fault extended to strict products liability: Daly v. General Motors Corp. (1978) 20 Cal.3d 725. https://law.justia.com/cases/california/supreme-court/3d/20/725.html
- Premises/retained control: CACI 1009B, citing Hooker v. Department of Transportation (2002) 27 Cal.4th 198. https://courts.ca.gov/system/files/itc/caci-1002.pdf

## Long Beach local
- City claim procedure: written claim required before suit; file with the City Clerk, 411 West Ocean Blvd; six months for death/injury/personal property, one year otherwise; suit within six months of a denial notice under Gov. Code 945.6. The City cites only the Government Claims Act (Gov. Code 810–996.6) — no charter or municipal-code claim section. https://www.longbeach.gov/attorney/how-to-file-a-claim/ and https://www.longbeach.gov/cityclerk/services/claim-form/
- Governor George Deukmejian Courthouse: high-volume criminal, traffic, civil and family matters; 31 courtrooms; opened September 9, 2013; six-acre site bounded by West Broadway, Maine Avenue, West 3rd Street, Magnolia Avenue. https://courts.ca.gov/facilities/los-angeles-county-governor-george-deukmejian-courthouse-long-beach
- LASC Long Beach civil trial departments S10, S28, S29 at 275 Magnolia Avenue. https://www.lacourt.org/division/civil/pdf/Trial_Courtrooms.pdf
- Safe Streets Long Beach: plan adopted July 2020, goal of zero traffic deaths by 2026; 45 people died in traffic collisions in 2022; 73% of high-injury intersections and 83% of high-injury corridors in disadvantaged communities. https://www.longbeach.gov/globalassets/city-manager/media-library/documents/memos-to-the-mayor-tabbed-file-list-folders/2023/july-24--2023---2023-update-on-the-safe-streets-long-beach-action-plan
- I-710 carries nearly 40,000 heavy-duty trucks daily between the port complex and downtown intermodal yards. https://www.metro.net/documents/2025/01/lb-ela-executive-summary-for-release-4-2-2024-englishpdf/
- Port volume (federal source; polb.com is robots-blocked): 2023 loaded containers 5,089,636 TEUs (3rd among U.S. ports); total tonnage 85,260,786 short tons (6th); six container terminals, five with on-dock rail as of October 2025. https://www.bts.gov/sites/bts.dot.gov/files/2026-01/BTS_Port-Performance-2026_Annual-Report_Final%2011326.pdf
- Trauma care: the Los Angeles local EMS agency lists a Level II adult trauma center in Long Beach. https://emsa.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/71/2025/08/California-Designated-ACS-Verified-Trauma-Centers-8.22.25.pdf
