## Conservative drafting note

The authored copy presents a neutral legal-information resource. It avoids claims about selecting, screening, evaluating, directing, or transmitting a person calling to a lawyer, and it directs readers to make their own independent choice.

There is a shared-template limitation outside the permitted copy-only scope: the template’s automatic disclosure states that Tampa enquiries are sent to a local attorney, and pre-existing staged output retains older language. That generated shell should not be treated as fully clear of Florida Bar Rule 4-7.22 concerns until an authorized template revision removes that assertion and the staged output is regenerated cleanly. Florida Bar information on qualifying providers: https://www.floridabar.org/ethics/etad/qualifying-providers-lawyer-referral-services/
