## Anti-duplication note vs. the Tampa reference site

The Tampa criminal page already uses Fla. Stat. 27.52(2)(a) (200% federal poverty line),
775.083 fine ceilings, 938.05 and 938.29(1)(a). To avoid overlap in both figures and prose, this
Orlando page leads on 316.193 DUI-specific fine bands, 938.07's DUI-only $135 court cost,
322.21(8) reinstatement fees, 322.2715(5) and 28.246(4). Fla. Stat. 938.29 is not used here.
