## What the columns mean

- **status** — `registered` means someone holds the name, from the
  Cloudflare snapshot where possible and Verisign RDAP otherwise.
- **ours** — whether *we* are that someone. Set only by the Cloudflare
  snapshot. `registered` with an empty `ours` needs a human.
- **expires_on / auto_renew** — straight off the registrar. An
  `auto_renew` of `NO` on a rented site is a revenue risk, not a detail.
- **site_written** — the copy exists and `template/build.py` passes it.
  Independent of whether the domain is bought.
- **phone** — the tracking number on the site. `PLACEHOLDER` means the
  build still warns and the site must not be published.
- **tenant** — the contractor renting it. Empty means no tenant, which
  changes what the site is allowed to claim (see `template/LOCKED.md`).
- **hosting** — where it is actually served from, once deployed.
