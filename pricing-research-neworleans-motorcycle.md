## Rules of professional conduct cited

**Louisiana Rules of Professional Conduct, Rule 1.5.** Source: Louisiana Attorney Disciplinary Board, Rules of Professional Conduct (PDF) — https://www.ladb.org/Material/Publication/ROPC/ROPC.pdf (retrieved 2026-08-23)

- **1.5(a)** — fee must be reasonable; eight factors; no percentage published.
- **1.5(c)** — contingent fee permitted except as prohibited by (d) or other law. Requires a **writing signed by the client**, with a copy or duplicate original delivered to the client at execution; must state the method of determination including percentages accruing on settlement, trial and appeal; the litigation and other expenses deducted from the recovery; **whether those expenses are deducted before or after the fee is calculated**; and clear notice of expenses the client owes whether or not the case is won. Closing written statement of outcome and remittance required.
- **1.5(d)(1)-(2)** — no fee in a domestic relations matter contingent on securing a divorce or on the amount of alimony/support or property settlement in lieu of those; no contingent fee for a criminal defendant. Cited on the page only as the boundary; neither applies to a crash claim.
- **1.8(i)(2)** — a lawyer may contract with a client for a reasonable contingent fee in a civil case (confirms permissibility).
