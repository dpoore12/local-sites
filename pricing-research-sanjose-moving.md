## IMPORTANT CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF

The task said California intrastate movers are "licensed by the CPUC under MAX 4." Household-goods carrier regulation moved
out of the CPUC: **Maximum Rate Tariff 4 is administered by the Bureau of Household Goods and Services (BHGS)** within the
California Department of Consumer Affairs. The edition in force is **effective January 1, 2025**:
https://bhgs.dca.ca.gov/forms_pubs/20240101_maxtariff.pdf (local copies `/home/user/workspace/ca_max4.pdf`, `ca_max4.txt`).
The "CAL-T" number is BHGS-issued. Nothing on the page attributes the tariff to the CPUC.

## What MAX 4 actually says

**Item 320 — maximum hourly rates, moves of 100 miles or less. Santa Clara County is in Territory A.**

| Crew | Straight time | Time and a half | Double time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unit + 1 person | **$142.01** | $181.84 | $228.01 |
| Unit + 2 persons | **$228.01** | $307.65 | $387.55 |
| Each additional person | **$81.80** | $119.31 | $156.98 |

**Item 36 — chargeable time = loading + unloading + DOUBLE the actual driving time** between origin and destination
(the "double drive time" rule).

**Item 330 — per-piece maximums** (5 pieces or fewer, 50 miles or less): first piece $98.50 / $183.12 / $255.79 by mileage band; each additional piece $33.52.

**Written agreement:** an "Agreement for Moving Services" showing a **Not to Exceed Price** is required before service begins,
may not be preprinted, may be raised only by bona fide written change order, and is not waivable.

**BHGS fees and coverage** (https://bhgs.dca.ca.gov/licensee/hhm_faqs.shtml): household mover permit filing **$500**;
transfer $150; quarterly fee $15 (including $5 to CHP); subhaul bond minimum $15,000; bodily injury $250k/$500k;
property damage $100k; **combined single limit not less than $600,000**; **cargo minimum $20,000 per shipment**;
gross-revenue fee ½ of 1%; late report penalty 25%.

2. **Three movers + truck, hourly — $159–290.** Low = Finch cash $159; high = Silicon Valley $290; Trek $160/$180 inside.
3. **Four movers + truck, hourly — $200–350.** Low = Trek cash $200; high = Silicon Valley $350; Finch $209/$219 inside.
4. **Studio move, minimum hours plus doubled driving time — $360–513.** ITEMIZED TOTAL. Low = Trek cash $120/hr × 3-hour minimum = **$360**. Mid check: Finch card $129 × 3 = $387, plus its posted 5% card processing fee = **$406.35**. High = FairPrice's own worked example, $171/hr × 3 hrs = **$513** with travel time already counted twice under Item 36.
5. **Two-bedroom apartment, inside San Jose — $400–1,500.** Low = Nor-Cal local-move floor $400 (Clutch's 4-hour 2-BR figure of $400–600 corroborates the bottom); high = Silicon Valley $1,500 and FairPrice $1,500.
6. **Three or four-bedroom house, local — $1,800–3,500.** Low = FairPrice 3-BR $1,800; high = Silicon Valley 4-BR $3,500. Row is labeled three-to-four bedroom precisely because the $3,500 figure is a 4-BR number.
7. **Packing added to the move — $300–1,500.** Low = Nor-Cal $300; high = Nor-Cal $1,500; Silicon Valley $400–1,200+ and Clutch $800–1,200 inside.
