## Arlington permits and inspections
- Kitchen and bath remodels, wall removals, garage conversions and changes to the existing framework of the home require a permit; city recommends a licensed professional contractor for electrical and plumbing work. [City of Arlington, "Do I Need A Permit? A DIY Guide", Dec 2024](https://www.arlingtontx.gov/News-Articles/2024/December/Do-I-Need-A-Permit-A-DIY-Guide)
- Permit categories include Electrical, Plumbing, Residential Building, Windows/Doors, Concrete/Foundation. Inspections must be requested before 7 a.m.; requests after 7 a.m. are scheduled the following business day. 24-hour inspection hotline 817-261-8817. Apply/pay/schedule at arlingtonpermits.com, or in person at the One-Start Center, City Hall 2nd floor, 101 W. Abram St. Permit fees are not quoted over the phone. [City of Arlington, "Do I Need a Permit?" PDF](https://www.arlingtontx.gov/files/assets/city/v/1/code-compliance/documents/get-involved-code-compliance/do-i-need-a-permit.pdf)
- Arlington adopted the 2021 IBC, IRC, IPC and IMC via Ordinances 22-055/22-057/22-058/22-059 on November 1, 2022. The current construction chapter ordinance 25-075 took effect September 16, 2025, with implementation and enforcement October 16, 2025 after the 30-day delay under Tex. Local Gov't Code 214.218. [Arlington Ordinance 25-075 PDF](https://www.arlingtontx.gov/files/assets/city/v/1/city-secretary/documents/city-code-of-ordinances/adopted-ordinances-not-yet-codified/construction-chapter-ord.-25-075.pdf)
- $325 minimum permit charge for additions, alterations, remodels and repairs; a residential building permit covers building, electrical, mechanical and plumbing fees for the project rather than billing each trade separately. Schedule adopted by Resolution 25-258, effective Oct 1, 2025. [Arlington Planning and Development Services fee schedule](https://www.dshs.texas.gov/sites/default/files/foodestablishments/pdf/Local%20Ordinance%20Registry/planning-and-development-services-fee-schedule_oct-2025.pdf)
- NOTE: the city fees_calculator page states the 35% non-refundable plan review fee in the context of commercial and multifamily projects, so I did NOT reuse the site.json framing of 35% as a general residential add-on in the service copy. [Arlington fees calculator](https://www.arlingtontx.gov/city_hall/departments/planning_development_services/permitting_and_inspections/fees_calculator)

## Texas plumbing law — who may move a drain
- Tex. Occ. Code 1301.002(7) defines "plumbing" to include "a fixture, appurtenance, appliance, or piping, including a drain or waste pipe, used to carry wastewater or sewage from or within a building" to a sewer service lateral. 1301.351(a): a person other than a responsible master plumber may not engage in plumbing unless properly licensed/registered and supervised by a licensee. 1301.051: a property owner is not required to be licensed to perform plumbing in the owner's own homestead. [TSBPE Plumbing License Law (plain view), June 2023](https://tsbpe.texas.gov/wp-content/uploads/documents/TSBPE_PlumbingLicenseLaw(PlainView)_June2023.pdf)
- Responsible Master Plumber: $225 initial license fee, $300 renewal, commercial general liability coverage of at least $300,000. [TSBPE RMP page](https://tsbpe.texas.gov/license-types/responsible-master-plumber/)

## Housing stock and neighborhoods
- Median year built for an Arlington single-family property is 1984; more than 65% of the housing stock was built before 1990 (2023 Tarrant Appraisal District data). [Arlington EDC single-family housing profile, July 2024](https://arlingtontxedc.com/assets/main/singlefamilyhousingprofile_final.pdf)
- Heart of Arlington Neighborhood Action Plan: typical house built 1959, about 1,530 sq ft; over 80% of residences built 1950–1969; almost 40% of houses have only one bathroom; 54% have a single-car garage or none; the majority of Arlington's older homes (1950–1969) are in the Central and East Sectors; oldest residential buildings are on Center Street and First Street. The 2007 Arlington Historic Resources Survey rated post-WWII subdivisions established 1945–1960; College Oaks, Elm Shadows, Glynn Oaks and Mill Creek Estates each received a "high" rating. Brasher-Keith Addition sits on Park Row Drive between South Center Street and Johnson Creek and is named as a candidate for a conservation district overlay. Elm Shadows and Mill Creek Estates both border Johnson Creek. [Heart of Arlington Neighborhoods Plan PDF](https://www.arlingtontx.gov/files/assets/city/v/2/strategic-initiatives/documents/neighborhood-plans/heart-of-arlington-neighborhoods-plan.pdf)
- South Center Street Historic District: Craftsman-inspired bungalows in the 500 and 600 blocks of South Center Street, described as the finest group of early 20th century bungalows remaining in Arlington. [Downtown Arlington](https://downtownarlington.org/post/visit-these-historic-downtown-places-during-historic-preservation-month) — NRHP reference 03000334, periods of significance 1900–1924, 1925–1949, 1950–1974, significant year 1916, style Bungalow/Craftsman. [NPGallery NRIS 03000334](https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/03000334)

## Soil, slab and tile
- Houston Black (Texas state soil, Blackland Prairies MLRA 86A/86B and eastern Grand Prairie MLRA 85): high shrink-swell potential, very slow permeability, cracks 1.3 to 10 cm (1/2 to 4 in) wide at 30 cm (12 in) depth in dry periods, open 90 to 150 cumulative days in most years. [USDA-NRCS Official Series Description, HOUSTON BLACK](https://soilseries.sc.egov.usda.gov/OSD_Docs/H/HOUSTON_BLACK.html)
- 97.9% of new single-family homes started in the West South Central division (Texas, Oklahoma, Arkansas, Louisiana) in 2024 were built on slabs. [NAHB analysis of Census Survey of Construction](https://www.nahb.org/blog/2025/08/foundation-type-by-region)
- TCNA Handbook EJ171: interior movement joints placed no more than 25 ft apart in both directions; interiors exposed to moisture or direct sunlight reduce to 8 to 12 ft. [Custom Building Products TB100 on the TCNA Handbook](https://www.custombuildingproducts.com/media/61023957/TB100-Changes-to-the-2017-TCNA-Handbook.pdf) and [TCNA placement FAQ](https://tcnatile.com/resource-center/faq/placement/) (perimeter gap at room edges, often hidden by base or shoe molding)

## Drain material and life expectancy
- InterNACHI estimated life expectancy: cast-iron waste pipe 60 years above ground, 50 to 60 years below ground; ABS and PVC waste pipe 50 to 80 years; cast-iron bathtub 100 years; exhaust fans 10 years; shower enclosure/module 50 years. [InterNACHI life expectancy chart](https://www.nachi.org/life-expectancy.htm)
- Cast iron gave way to plastic drain pipe through the 1970s; by the early 1980s essentially all residential drain lines were plastic. [Lakeview plumbing history overview](https://lakeviewpuller.com/2021/08/10/house-plumbing-systems-through-the-years/)
- Cast iron drain pipe installed 1950–1980 has a typical 50 to 75 year service life; repair-versus-replace turns on age, number and severity of failure points. [James Armstrong Plumbing, Mesquite TX](https://www.jamesarmstrongplumbing.com/drain-cleaning-hydro-jetting/cast-iron-drain-pipes-mesquite-tx/)

## Showers, drains, accessibility
- IPC 417.5.2: shower floors sloped 1/4 inch per foot toward the drain; TCNA shower receptor methods echo it; recessing the substrate is how a curbless entry gets the slope without raising the room. [TileLetter, curbless shower planning](https://www.tileletter.com/critical-planning-for-curbless-shower-success/)
- Grab bars, shower seats and bench seats must resist a single 250-pound concentrated load applied in any direction at any point (IBC 1607.7.2 / ICC A117.1). [UpCodes, loads on grab bars](https://up.codes/s/loads-on-handrails-guards-grab-bars-and-vehicle-barriers)

## Ventilation and climate
- IRC M1505.4.4 Table: bathrooms and toilet rooms require 50 cfm intermittent or 20 cfm continuous local exhaust. [UpCodes M1505.4.4](https://up.codes/s/local-exhaust-rates)
- IRC M1501.1: mechanical exhaust must discharge to the outdoors, not into an attic, soffit, ridge vent or crawl space. [Building Code Geek](https://buildingcodegeek.com/bathroom-exhaust-fan-code-requirements/)
- DFW climate normals: average relative humidity 82% at 6 a.m. and 56% at noon annually; May averages 87% at 6 a.m.; 20.2 days a year at or above 100°F; normal annual precipitation 37.01 inches. [NWS Fort Worth, DFW normals/means/extremes](https://www.weather.gov/fwd/dfw_records_normals)

## Lead and asbestos in older Arlington houses
- EPA RRP Rule: firms paid to disturb paint in housing built before 1978 must be EPA-certified; interior work disturbing more than six square feet of paint per room is covered; window replacement and demolition of painted surfaces are covered regardless of area. Plumbers, carpenters, electricians and general contractors are all covered trades. [EPA RRP for contractors](https://www.epa.gov/lead/renovation-repair-and-painting-program-contractors)
- Texas Asbestos Health Protection Rules (25 TAC 296) do not apply to a single private residence or an apartment building with no more than four dwelling units, except where NESHAP applies under 296.191(g)(4)(A)-(C). So a single-family bathroom demo is outside the state licensing scheme even when asbestos-containing material is present. [Texas DSHS TAHPR 2024 PDF](https://www.dshs.texas.gov/sites/default/files/asbestos/pdf/TAHPR%202024.pdf)
