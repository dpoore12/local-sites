## Before you pay

- **Cleanerin** asks only for a website and account details. Their staff build the listing from the site within a business day, so the listing shows whatever the site shows: city, phone, services, no street address.
- **MyLocalServices: try one first.** Step 2 asks for an address, and their page says the $2 charge is there because "free attracted false addresses and businesses that did not exist". A moderator checks every listing. So file one site (Pensacola) with the address left blank or city-only. If it's accepted, do the other 9; if it's rejected, the other $18 isn't worth it. Untick every optional extra at checkout (they renew yearly).
- Every description ends with the sites' own line: independently operated advertising, not itself a contractor or hauler. Leave license, insurance, hours and photos blank. The site photos are AI-made, so don't upload them.
