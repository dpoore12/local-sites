## The headline

**Six of Kyle's nine niches are dead at this population band.** Epoxy floors, popcorn ceiling removal, paver installation, gutter installation, septic pumping and spray foam returned 10–40 searches a month or no data at all, in every one of 20 cities. Whatever Kyle is ranking in those, it is not city-level search demand at 50k–200k.

**Three are alive: auto glass, junk removal, towing.** Every finalist below is one of those.

**Every commercial query carries a map pack** — 19 of 25 in Ahrefs, 8 of 8 on live pull. Small cities do not remove the map pack; they only weaken it. The ceiling from earlier today holds everywhere: organic #1 renders fourth on the page. **AI Overviews appear on 2 of 25.** The click survives on these terms.

## What each one actually looks like

**Towing, Kalamazoo MI (73k).** Organic #1 has 17 referring domains. Map pack slot 1 has 27 reviews; slot 2 is a 2.7-star operator with 890 reviews that Reddit calls out for price-gouging in storms. Facebook, Reddit and MapQuest all on page one. Ahrefs has no KD because the field is too thin to score. This is the one.

**Towing, Evansville IN (117k).** Map pack slot 1 is a 3.6-star operator. The Yelp result on page one is for Evansville, *Wisconsin*. MapQuest and Yahoo Local hold organic slots. A local site is in the pack with 9 referring domains. Organic floor 39.

**Junk removal, Cedar Rapids IA (137k).** A Facebook group post ranks organic #2. Only one local business site in organic at all. Pack entry at 23 referring domains. Highest volume of the junk markets (Ahrefs 200, DataForSEO 590).

**Junk removal, Asheville NC (94k).** Reddit is organic #1. Two local sites at 37 and 41. But College Hunks has 2,300 reviews in the pack — a franchise anchors slot 3.

**Junk removal, Evansville IN (117k).** Organic #1 is a one-man operator whose site says "I offer." Facebook page at #2. Floor 45.

**Windshield, Kalamazoo MI.** Pack slot 2 has 22 reviews. But Safelite, Glass Doctor and Gerber all on page one — three national chains. Organic floor 58. Harder than the pack suggests.

**Windshield, Fargo ND.** Glass Doctor holds two of three pack slots (458 and 299 reviews). Safelite organic #1. Floor 58. The chains own this one.

**Windshield, Springfield MO.** Highest volume (250 Ahrefs, 1,000 DFS) but Auto Glass Now has 1,200 reviews in the pack and Ahrefs KD is 33. Not a beginner market.

## The niche verdict

**Towing** is the best fit for this model by a wide margin. Urgent, high-intent, one call per job, no California contractor problem (it is not a construction trade), and the smallest link bars measured all day. It is also the least likely to attract national franchises — every towing SERP was local operators, Facebook pages and directories.

**Junk removal** is second. Good volume, soft organic fields, but 1-800-GOT-JUNK, College Hunks, LoadUp and Two Men and a Truck appear on nearly every SERP. You are competing with franchise location pages.

**Auto glass** is the weakest of the three. Safelite is on organic page one in every city tested, Glass Doctor in most. The pack can be soft (Kalamazoo slot 2 at 22 reviews) but the organic field is chain-heavy.

## Honest caveats

- Volumes are small. Ahrefs puts most of these at 90–200 searches a month. Kyle's economics (50–80 calls/month at position 4–5) assume a market that produces calls, and 100 searches a month at a 40% organic CTR is not 50 calls. These are $500/month sites, not $2,000/month sites.

- DataForSEO and Ahrefs disagree on volume by 2–20×. The doc uses Ahrefs. If Ahrefs is the one undercounting, the picture improves.

- Two sources disagreed on Pueblo, CO — DataForSEO's link data there was two years old. It was removed from the finalists. Re-pull before acting on it.

- Map pack review counts change monthly. The 22-review and 27-review slots are today's numbers.

- None of this changes the structural constraint: the map pack needs a real business. A towing site in Kalamazoo with 20 links takes organic #1, which renders fourth. The tenant takes the pack.
