## Accuracy handling

- **No fee cap asserted.** Virginia tests a fee against Rule 1.5(a) reasonableness; the page says a rate advertised as "the Virginia maximum" is a market convention, not law.
- **8.01-581.15 explicitly framed as a cap on total recoverable damages in a medical malpractice case, not a cap on a fee**, and the escalating figures are given with their effective-date structure.
- Note on the $52 filing figure: the Virginia Beach GDC page publishes $52 for filing civil papers; Va. Code 16.1-69.48:2 sets the statutory $36 component. The page presents both and explains that the local total includes statutory add-ons, rather than presenting them as competing numbers.
