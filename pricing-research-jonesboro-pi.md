## Bottom line on Arkansas

**Arkansas sets no percentage ceiling on an injury contingency.** Rule 1.5(a) of the
Arkansas Rules of Professional Conduct requires that a fee be *reasonable* and lists
eight factors; there is no schedule for negligence work. The only Arkansas percentage
is **25%**, and it belongs to **workers' compensation** (A.C.A. 11-9-715), where the
Workers' Compensation Commission must approve the fee. Arkansas's constitution also
forbids the legislature from capping injury or death recoveries (art. 5, § 32).

## Caps found that do NOT apply to a personal injury fee

- **A.C.A. 11-9-715 — the entire 25% structure, plus the $500 / $1,000 appeal
  ceilings, the $200 change-of-physician fee and the Commission-approval
  requirement — is workers' compensation only.** The page labels it that way and
  never presents it as a limit on a negligence contingency.
- No Arkansas statute caps a fee, or a damages award, in ordinary injury work; the
  constitutional provision above affirmatively blocks a legislated recovery limit.

## Discrepancy to disclose

The Craighead County Circuit Clerk's own page publishes **$167.50** to open a civil
case, while the county government's transparency fee page publishes **$165.00** for
the same line item (both citing A.C.A. § 21-6-403(b)(1); the county page also cites
§ 21-6-416). The statutory base in § 21-6-403(b)(1) is $150.00, with additional
statutory add-ons accounting for the difference. The page uses the clerk's $167.50
as the anchor, cites the $150.00 statutory base in the table, and the body states
that the two county-published totals differ by $2.50 and should be confirmed with
the clerk on the day of filing.
