# What Kyle's public videos add to our copy (26 Oct 2026)

Source: all 26 long-form, non-review videos on Kyle's YouTube channel (@KyleReviewsCourses, about 9 hours, 129k words of captions), plus one competitor testimonial video. Each was read in full by one of three readers. Transcripts are kept locally in `kyle-screen/kyle-videos/text/` and are not committed, because they are other people's content. Income figures are self-reported unless noted.

## The big surprise: the course isn't Kyle's

The $2,980 course is **Shiv's "Unemployable" program**, about $4,000 on a payment plan. Kyle is a successful student and a paid affiliate: Shiv says Kyle earned about $30k in one month from about 40 referrals. rentlocalsites.com is Kyle's funnel into Shiv's course. Most of the 26 videos are hosted by Shiv, interviewing students.

Shiv runs his own software:
- **Site Panda**, the site builder
- **Call Owl**, call tracking

A different Kyle (Kyle Ruoff) runs that software, and they sell ready-made sites. Shiv says they've built about 3,500 sites together.

## The Maps answer: through the client

Kyle never uses a fake address. **After a client signs, he works with that business owner to verify a Google Business listing in the owner's city**, built only to rank, with no branding. Kyle calls it optional but "the only tricky part", and says it permanently raises calls and rent. A storefront client simply gave his own address.

Nobody in 26 videos explains virtual offices, service-area settings or verification without a client. In a competitor's group, suspended listings are treated as normal.

The order is **site first, client second, Maps third.** Ranking Maps listings pull far more calls. St. Louis auto glass: map #1 plus organic #8 got 322 calls in 30 days. Organic alone still rents: organic #8 with no map listing got about 82 calls a month at $20 a call.

## New things we haven't copied

**Selling (the biggest gap)**
1. **Sell early, before ranking.** These all happen in the videos:
   - Hand a site's very first lead straight to a business owner. That's how Alexandria got her first client.
   - Pre-sign clients and deliver leads with Google Ads until SEO ranks. About a third of Pierre's revenue is ads.
   - Take a commission of about 10% of jobs closed while a site climbs, then switch to a flat fee once calls are steady. Greg says he made about $20k one summer this way.

   Kyle's own first deal was $6,000 wired upfront by an attorney he knew, before he had ranked anything.
2. **Who to call:**
   - Businesses already running Google Ads ("obviously paying for marketing").
   - Businesses listed in niche directories.

   Scrape a list and call down it. One student texts first: a blue iMessage bubble means a personal cell.
3. **Kyle's trial script:**
   - Offer a free 7-day "test drive". "All I need is a phone number and email."
   - Understate the volume: quote 40–50 calls when the site gets 70–80.
   - "We never talk with or bill the customer."
   - Warn up front that texts don't forward and some calls will be spam.
   - Text the owner after each lead: "how did the call from Rachel go?"
   - A trial lands within 1–3 calls.

   **Don't copy his opener:** "another company can no longer take these calls" is often made up.
4. **Price:**
   - Shiv's rule is $1 for every $10 the owner makes.
   - Real deals run $500–$1,500 a month. Weekly billing ($400–$500 a week) is common.
   - Minimum calls to sell: a steady 5 a month for high-ticket trades (concrete, roofing), 25–30 for low-ticket.
   - Underpricing to start is fine, then raise it.
   - Pitch against the owner's current ad spend: $25 Google Ads clicks, HomeAdvisor, Thumbtack.
5. **Getting paid:**
   - Take the card when the deal is agreed and charge half of month one immediately, then auto-bill.
   - No contracts for Kyle, but some students sign 3 or 12 months.
   - Any payment method is fine at first.
6. **Grow a client:** build sites for the surrounding towns and sell them to the same client. Introduce clients to each other, ask every "no" for a referral, and give the client a review QR code.

**Phones**
7. **Call whisper and email:** before each call a voice says "Call from [Company]", and the recording plus the caller's number are emailed after. Providers are CallRail or Shiv's Call Owl. Unrented sites go to voicemail, as ours do. The 30-day call count and the recordings are what the owner sees on the sales call.

**Content and links**
8. **The weekly loop:**
   - Publish at once and wait out 1–2 weeks of the "Google dance".
   - Then each week add 2–3 pages (a service page, an About page, a city page) **plus a few free or $10–25 links** copied from competitors via Ahrefs, Mangools or Majestic.
   - Check again in a week and repeat.

   We're adding pages. We're not adding links each round.
9. **Unrented sites earn their keep** as link donors in the student swap group (Neil has given about 100 and got about 100).
10. **Volume:**
    - Shiv: start with 10–20 sites, because about 30% never find a buyer, and judge them at 6 months.
    - Kyle at one point says skip research and build 200. That contradicts his weak-market advice.
    - Rank #3–6 still rents for $500–800.
11. **Cheap builds:** Weebly or Site Panda drag-and-drop, about $30 a month all in per site. Domains match service plus city exactly. Small sites work: a 5-page auto glass site gets about 70 calls a month.

**Not in any of the 26 videos:** citation or directory tools (BrightLocal, Whitespark), how many citations to build, word counts, schema. Citations come up once, in passing.

## What this means for us

1. **Dan's idea, call businesses and offer free leads until they pay, is exactly what they do,** and it's also how they get Maps. The client's address and Maps listing come with the deal. This is the missing step, not the $3k course.
2. **The course mostly buys the link-swap group, coaching and Shiv's software.** We already have everything the videos describe except the swap group and a sales habit.
3. **Add links to the weekly content loop:** a few free or cheap copycat links per site per week, not pages alone.
4. **Phones:** add a whisper ("Call from [site name]") and emailed recordings before the first client. That's a Telnyx change, not a purchase.
5. **Start selling now.** Their students' biggest regret is waiting for rankings before calling owners.
