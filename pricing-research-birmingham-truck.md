## Discrepancies and limits — flagged

1. **Filing-fee mismatch.** The AOC distribution chart shows the statutory base at **$206** (≤$50,000) and **$306** (>$50,000), while the Jefferson County clerk page publishes **$251 / $351**. The difference is local/technology add-ons layered on the statutory base. The page publishes the clerk's $251/$351 figures, which are what a filer actually pays in Birmingham, and does not present $206/$306 as the amount due.
2. **Currency of the clerk page.** The Jefferson County filing-fee page itself states its figures are current as of **June 21, 2012**. It is the only schedule the clerk publishes; figures should be re-verified with the Circuit Clerk before relying on them for a filing.
3. **eCFR blocked.** ecfr.gov returned an IP block; 49 C.F.R. 387.9 Table 1 was read on Cornell LII instead. Text matches the federal minimums.
4. **Ala. Code 6-5-410** (wrongful death, punitive damages only) could not be fetched and is **not used anywhere on the page**.

## Accuracy handling

- **No contingency cap asserted.** Alabama's control is Rule 1.5(a)'s "clearly excessive" standard; the page says explicitly that Alabama sets no ceiling for a tractor-trailer case and that a "state maximum" percentage is a practice, not a statute.
- **The 15 percent figure is labeled as workers' compensation** under Ala. Code 25-5-90(a), in its own section, with a statement that it does not govern a truck-accident contingency.
- 49 C.F.R. 387.9 minimums are presented as insurance floors bearing on which carrier is at the table, not as a measure of claim value.
