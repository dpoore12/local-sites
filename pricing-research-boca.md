## Context figures used in the body copy (already sourced in site.json local_facts)

- More than 4.75 miles of managed shoreline; Hurricane Wilma wind damage 2005-10-24 (NOAA)
- Sectional and rollup exterior doors are Florida product-approval categories (Palm Beach County)
- A City permit does not exempt an owner from HOA regulations (Boca Raton HOA affidavit)

## Not verified

- Palm Beach County's Wind Speed Maps page (https://discover.pbc.gov/pzb/Maps/Wind-Speeds.aspx)
  describes Figure 1609 and per-parcel GIS tools but does not print a single mph figure, so the
  170 mph anchor is taken from the City of Boca Raton affidavit, which states it directly.
- The city fee schedule PDF carries an effective date of October 1, 2018 with a 10/01/2019
  document date; it is the current schedule published on the city's document center, but a newer
  revision may exist behind the city's permit fee estimator, which was not retrieved.
- No design-pressure (psf) figure is published on the page because no city document retrieved
  states a specific psf value for a residential garage door opening.

### Not used, and why

- **Garage Doors USA (garagedoorsusafl.com)** posts a full price list but states "Prices shown are
  parts only; labor is billed separately," with labor $150-$400. A parts-only figure cannot set a
  range on rows that include labor, so it was excluded rather than totaled, because the page gives
  no per-job labor allocation.
- **Ultra Garage Doors** posts only "repairs from $70," a floor with no job attached.
- **The Door Doctor of South Florida** serves Boca Raton (https://www.thedoordoctor.net/service-areas/,
  read 2026-08-23) but its pricing page (https://www.thedoordoctor.net/pricing/, read 2026-08-23) now
  shows only "installation labor pricing starts at $300," explicitly labor-only, so it was excluded
  from the opener-replacement row.
