## IMPORTANT CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF

The task said North Carolina intrastate movers operate under "Maximum Rate Tariff No. 6 / household goods."
The tariff actually in force is **Maximum Rate Tariff No. 2**, issued **December 30, 2025**, effective **January 1, 2026**,
published by the North Carolina Utilities Commission at https://www.ncuc.gov/industries/documents/maxrate.pdf
(local copies: `/home/user/workspace/nc_mrt.pdf`, `nc_mrt.txt`). No Tariff No. 6 was found. All page figures use No. 2.

## What the NC maximum rate tariff actually says

Section II hourly rates apply to intrastate household-goods moves of **50 miles or less**.

**Rule 53 — maximum hourly rates, regular time (8 a.m.–5 p.m., Mon–Fri):**

| Crew | Max per hour |
| --- | --- |
| Vehicle + 2 persons | **$188.40** |
| Vehicle + 3 | $243.30 |
| Vehicle + 4 | $297.90 |
| Vehicle + 5 | $352.95 |
| Each additional person | $54.80 |
| Each additional vehicle | $52.50 |

**Overtime maximums:** $235.15 (2), $310.25 (3), **$385.15 (4)**, $460.10 (5); additional person $74.85; additional vehicle $65.75.

**Chargeable time:** runs from crew arrival at origin through completion at destination, rounded to the quarter hour;
travel between addresses chargeable at up to **one hour per 50 miles**.

**Item 18 — valuation:** Full Value Protection **$1.00 per $100 of declared value** (minimum valuation $5.00/lb, single truck);
$1.25 per $100 (minimum $6.00/lb) where more than one truck is used. Released value default **$0.60/lb**, which is not insurance.

**Item 10 — specialty maximums:** piano or organ, grand or 38 inches and over **$111.90**; smaller $81.65; laden freezer $93.30;
slate pool table **$128.25**. Flight carry inside, first flight $33.55, each additional $16.85; outside first flight $32.15.

**Item 20:** elevator **$3.10 per 100 lb**; stairs **$2.20 per 100 lb**.

Certificate requirement and consumer guide:
https://www.ncuc.gov/Industries/transportation/transportation.html and https://www.ncuc.gov/industries/transportation/movingguide.html

## Rows as published, and how low/high were set

1. **Truck + 2 movers, hourly — $130–190.** Low = Well Known $130; high = Dogwood $190; Romero $137 inside. All below the $188.40… note Dogwood's $190 is marginally above the regular-time tariff maximum, which is legal at overtime rates and is why the body frames posted rates as "roughly seventy to a hundred percent of the legal maximum."
2. **Truck + 3 movers, hourly — $172–235.** Low = Romero $137 + $35 = **$172**; high = Dogwood $235; Well Known $190 inside.
3. **Four movers, hourly — $260–325.** Low = Well Known $260; high = Dogwood $325 (includes two trucks, which the tariff prices separately).
4. **Labor only — $50–60 per mover per hour.** Low = Moving Muscle $50; high = UniMovers $60 ($120 ÷ 2 movers).
5. **Studio / 1-BR — $375–800.** Low = Well Known studio $375; high = Royalty studio/1-BR ceiling $800.
6. **Two-bedroom inside Wake County — $645–1,400.** ITEMIZED TOTAL. Low = Dogwood 3-hour minimum at $190/hr = $570, **plus the posted $75 per-truck Wake County travel fee = $645** (with the posted 3.5% card fee it would be $667.58). High = Royalty 2-BR ceiling $1,400; Well Known $850–1,100 inside.
7. **Three-bedroom in the Triangle — $1,200–2,400.** Low = Royalty $1,200; high = Royalty $2,400; Well Known $1,600–2,100 inside.
8. **Specialty piece — $100–600.** Low = Dogwood piano/gun safe $100; high = Royalty specialty ceiling $600. Tariff Item 10 maximums quoted in the note.

- Royalty labels its numbers planning ranges rather than a quote; cited as "posted planning ranges."
- Every row cites 2–3 distinct hostnames; verified programmatically.
