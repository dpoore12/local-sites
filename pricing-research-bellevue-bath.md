### Bellevue fee tables as published (all scanned PDFs, OCR'd locally)

**Building — Table 3-A** (View/951): $1–$500 = **$23.50**; $501–$2,000 = $23.50 + $3.05 per additional $100;
**$2,001–$25,000 = $69.25 for the first $2,000 plus $14.00 for each additional $1,000**;
**$25,001–$50,000 = $391.75 for the first $25,000 plus $10.10 for each additional $1,000**;
$50,001–$100,000 = $643.75 + $7.00 per $1,000. Inspections outside normal hours **$47.00/hr** (2-hour minimum);
reinspection $47.00/hr; additional plan review $47.00/hr.

**Plumbing — Table 3-D** (View/952): each fixture or trap **$9.80**; repair or alteration of drainage or vent piping
**$4.75 per fixture**; water piping / water-treating equipment alteration $4.75; building sewer $24.65;
gas piping 1–5 outlets $6.15, each additional $1.10; lawn sprinkler system $14.80; backflow device ≤2 in $12.30;
**reinspection $49.50**; permit issuance $23.50 and supplemental permit $7.25 per the 1997 UAC.

**Electrical — Table 3-B**: https://www.bellevue.net/DocumentCenter/View/955/Electrical-Permit-Fees-PDF
(issuance $23.50; receptacle/switch/lighting outlet, first 20, $1.10 each; residential appliance $4.75; reinspection $49.50).

permits = 1997 Uniform Administrative Code fees as amended; **penalty fee 4× the regular permit fee**;

late renewal $20/month; **Class C residential contractor unlimited $100/year**; Class D residential limited $75/year;
Class A $250; Class B $200; Class E roofing $50; special master plumber (one job) $150; SFD roofing permit $80; siding $25.

## Rows as published, and how low/high were set

1. **Tub → tiled walk-in shower — $9,000–12,000.** Low and high = Omaha Bath Remodel's posted conversion band; Pritch's completed Omaha project with a **$10,000 shower portion** corroborates the middle.
2. **Small bathroom, 40–50 sq ft — $8,000–20,000.** Low = Precision small-bath floor $8,000; high = Precision ceiling $20,000; Home Matters' small-bath tiers ($9,000–22,000 across grades) and Mad City's wet-area band inside.
3. **Hall bath, whole room replaced — $15,000–30,000.** Low = Omaha Remodeling Associates hall-bath floor $15,000; high = Pritch's stated $20,000–30,000 project band ceiling; Home Matters' $19,000–25,000 high-end average bath inside.
4. **Primary bath, walk-in shower + double vanity — $25,000–80,000.** Low = Omaha Remodeling Associates and Precision floor $25,000; high = Home Matters large-bath luxury ceiling **$80,000** (also its highest completed project).
5. **Finished projects with the price published — $20,000–55,000.** Low = Pritch's completed 6×10 Omaha bath **$20,000**; high = Home Matters' completed La Vista 120 sq ft bath **$55,000**; the Bellevue 42 sq ft project at $34,000 and the Papillion project at $43,000 sit inside.
6. **Rate per square foot — $150–300.** Low = both sources' floor $150; high = Precision's $300/sq ft standard-renovation ceiling; Omaha Remodeling Associates posts $150–250.

## Notes / caveats

- This is a **thin market**: three of the seven usable price sources present metro bands rather than an own-project rate card, and three of the well-known bath brands operating locally publish only promotions. Rows 1, 5 and 6 are the ones anchored in company-specific posted figures.
- The Precision Enterprises figures appear on a blog post whose slug is unrelated to bathrooms (`/blog/signs-you-need-a-storage-unit/`); the bathroom cost content is genuinely on that page and the domain is the company's own. Worth re-checking if the URL later changes.
- Bellevue's fee documents are scanned images; they were rendered with `pdftoppm` and OCR'd with tesseract to read the tables (local artifacts `/home/user/workspace/bellevue_fees.pdf`, `bnf_951.pdf`, `bnf_952.pdf`, `bnf_955.pdf`, page PNGs).
- Every row cites 2–3 distinct hostnames; verified programmatically. Both Home Matters URLs are the same hostname, so they never appear on the same row.
