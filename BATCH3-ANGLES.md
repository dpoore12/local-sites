# Batch 3 — Angle Assignments (the final 51)

Fifty-one sites are being written across three waves. Eight of them are car
accident lawyer sites, six are garage door repair, five are personal injury.
Without assigned anchors, eight writers would independently find the same state
statute of limitations and call it a local fact.

Find your domain below and dig where your anchor points. **The anchor tells you
where to dig, not what to conclude.** You still do your own research and you
still need working source URLs. If an anchor turns out to be unsourceable or
wrong, say so in your report and substitute something else genuinely local.

Read `WRITER-BRIEF.md` first. Everything in it applies, including the
domain-prefixed temp image filenames and the 15-consecutive-word rule.

## The one rule that matters most here

Thirty-two sites are already written. The build will reject you if you share
fifteen consecutive words with any of them. Under each trade below is a list of
what the already-written sites used. **Those angles are burned.** Do not
rediscover them and do not paraphrase them.

For the legal trades this is the trap: the state statute of limitations is the
first thing every writer finds, and in a state where a sibling site already used
it, you cannot. Go to the county instead — the court's own structure, its filing
location, its published caseload, a county-level crash or claim statistic from
the state DOT.
