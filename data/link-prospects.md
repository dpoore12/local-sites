# Local link prospects for 12 city service websites

Every page below was opened and checked on 22 August 2026. National directories (Yelp, Angi, Houzz, Justia, Avvo, HomeAdvisor, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, YellowPages, FindLaw), competitor firms and contractors, and any paid-link or guest-post marketplace were left out on purpose. Costs are only listed when the page itself publishes a number.

The five kinds used below:

1. Local news site / neighborhood blog
2. Neighborhood, HOA or community group site
3. Chamber of commerce directory / join page
4. City or county government or library resource page
5. Bar association, legal aid or local trade association
