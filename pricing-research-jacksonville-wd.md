## Bottom line

Florida is the exception in this portfolio: **Bar Rule 4-1.5(f)(4)(B) sets a genuine sliding contingency schedule.**
A fee exceeding it is **presumed, unless rebutted, to be a clearly excessive fee** — it is a rebuttable presumption
plus a court-approval escape hatch, not an absolute ceiling. The tiers below were read off the rule text and are
reproduced on the page without rounding.

## The exact tiers — Rule 4-1.5(f)(4)(B)(i)

Scope of (f)(4): personal injury, property damage, or death or loss of services resulting from personal injuries based
on tortious conduct, **including products liability**.

**(f)(4)(B)(i)a — before filing of an answer or the demand for appointment of arbitrators (or expiration of the time
to do either):**
1. **33 1/3%** of any recovery **up to $1 million**
2. **30%** of any portion of the recovery **between $1 million and $2 million**
3. **20%** of any portion **exceeding $2 million**

**(f)(4)(B)(i)b — after the answer or demand for appointment of arbitrators is filed, through entry of judgment:**
1. **40%** of any recovery **up to $1 million**
2. **30%** of any portion **between $1 million and $2 million**
3. **20%** of any portion **exceeding $2 million**

**(f)(4)(B)(i)c — where all defendants admit liability at the time of filing their answers and request a trial only on
damages:**
1. **33 1/3%** of any recovery **up to $1 million**
2. **20%** of any portion **between $1 million and $2 million**
3. **15%** of any portion **exceeding $2 million**

**(f)(4)(B)(i)d — an additional 5%** of any recovery after institution of any appellate proceeding is filed, or
postjudgment relief or action is required for recovery on the judgment.

The tiers are **cumulative layers**, not replacements — a $2.5M pre-answer recovery draws 33 1/3% of the first $1M plus
30% of the second $1M plus 20% of the remaining $500K. The page says so explicitly.

## Court-approval route to exceed the schedule — (f)(4)(B)(ii)

A client who cannot obtain the lawyer of the client's choice because of the limits, or who cannot obtain the contents of
the required Statement of Client's Rights, may **petition the court in which the matter would be filed, or the circuit
court in the circuit where the cause of action arose**, to approve the fee contract. The court authorizes it if it
determines the client has a complete understanding of the rights being waived and of the contract's terms. The petition
may be filed as a separate pre-suit proceeding, or with the complaint, or before service, and that aspect of the file
may be **sealed**. The petition must show service on the client. If the court **denies** authorization, a copy of the
petition and the order must be served on **The Florida Bar in Tallahassee**. Authorization does **not** bar a later
inquiry into whether the fee actually charged was clearly excessive.

## Other subdivisions of 4-1.5 cited

- **(f)(1)** — writing, method of determination, percentages for settlement/trial/appeal, costs and whether deducted
  before or after the fee, closing statement.
- **(f)(2)** — signed by the client and by each participating lawyer/firm, each assuming joint legal responsibility.
- **(f)(3)(A)-(B)** — no contingency in domestic relations matters; none for defending a criminal case.
- **(f)(4)(A)(i)-(ii)** — client must receive, read and sign the **Statement of Client's Rights**; the contract is
  cancellable by written notice **within 3 business days** of execution, with no fee owed for work performed in that
  period, though the lawyer may recover funds reasonably advanced.
- **(f)(4)(B)(iii)** — **medical liability claims only**: Fla. Const. art. I, §26 entitles a claimant to no less than
  **70% of the first $250,000** and **90% of amounts above that**, exclusive of reasonable and customary costs, unless
  the client executes the sworn waiver. **Labeled as medical liability only; not applied to this page's case type.**
- **(f)(4)(D)** — fee division: primary lawyer **no less than 75%**, secondary **no more than 25%**, absent court
  authorization for substantially equal participation; percentages applied after appellate counsel's fee comes off.
- **(f)(5)** — closing statement itemizing all costs and expenses and stating each lawyer's fee, signed by every
  participating lawyer and by the client, **retained for 6 years**.
- **(f)(6)** — structured settlements: the percentage is calculated on the **cost of the structured settlement or its
  present money value, whichever is less**.

## Guardianship interaction — Fla. Stat. 744.387

https://www.leg.state.fl.us/Statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&Search_String=&URL=0700-0799/0744/Sections/0744.387.html

- (1) The court may authorize a settlement it finds in the ward's best interest.
- (2) A **natural guardian may settle a minor's claim not exceeding $15,000 without a bond**; legal guardianship of the
  property is required where the net settlement exceeds that figure.
- (3)(a) After an action is commenced, **no settlement is effective unless approved by the court having jurisdiction**.
- (3)(b) Above $15,000 with no guardian appointed, the court shall require appointment of a guardian of the property.
