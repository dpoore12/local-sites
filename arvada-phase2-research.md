## Colorado statewide plumbing licensure (the differentiator)
- The State Plumbing Board "licenses, registers, and regulates plumbers, apprentices, and plumbing contractors" and does **not** regulate pipe fitters, drain cleaners, or gas pipe installers. Board has eight members appointed by the governor; the Examining Board of Plumbing was established 1917 and moved to DPO in 1970. https://dpo.colorado.gov/Plumbing
- License classes and scope, C.R.S. 12-155-103: master plumber (4), journeyman plumber (3), residential plumber (13) limited to "one-, two-, three-, and four-family dwellings, which dwellings must not extend more than two stories aboveground." Same section defines "plumbing" as items "located within the building or extending five feet from the building foundation, excluding any service line extending from the first joint to the property line." https://law.justia.com/codes/colorado/title-12/business-professions-and-occupations/article-155/section-12-155-103/
- Apprentice ratio, C.R.S. 12-155-124: "One licensed journeyworker plumber, master plumber, or residential plumber shall not supervise more than three plumbing apprentices at the same job site." Supervisor is responsible for the apprentice's work. https://codes.findlaw.com/co/title-12-professions-and-occupations/co-rev-st-sect-12-155-124/
- Board rule 3 CCR 720-1.2 defines direct supervision: supervising licensed plumber "physically present at the same physical addresses listed on the permits ... or no more than five minutes distance from the apprentice within the same construction or job site." Same rule cites 2021 IPC (412.3-412.8, 409.4) and 2021 IRC (P2708.4), states later editions are not included, requires relief valve discharge captured and conveyed to the sanitary drainage system or the exterior (IPC 504.6.1 / IRC P2803.6.2), and IRC P2605.2: thermal expansion tanks supported per manufacturer instructions and not supported by the connecting piping. https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/colorado/3-CCR-720-1.2

- Arvada Building Division plumbing inspectors do plan review and inspection, verify state licenses under C.R.S. 12-155-108, verify apprentice cards under 12-155-124, verify residential-license scope under 12-155-103(13), and "may file a complaint with the State Plumbing Board for any violation of C.R.S. Title 12, Article 155." https://www.arvadaco.gov/1242/Policy-on-Review-of-Plumbing-Licenses
- Current adopted codes, adopted March 24, 2026: 2024 IBC, IRC, IFGC, IMC, **2024 International Plumbing Code**, IPMC, 2024 IFC, IEBC, 2026 NEC, etc. Previous set (2018 codes incl. 2018 IPC) "in effect October 2020-March 2026." https://www.arvadaco.gov/165/Adopted-building-codes-and-design-criter
- "In the State of Colorado, water heater installations or replacements require a licensed plumbing contractor, permits, and inspections"; homeowner may do the work if they will occupy the home. Water heater permit type = Residential Plumbing; Backflow Device has its own permit type. Inspections weekdays 8am-4pm. https://www.arvadaco.gov/434/Residential-Mechanical-Plumbing
- Fee schedule: water heater flat permit $55.00 (Table 18-5); other plumbing permits by contract value under Table 18-1, minimum $34.00; **reinspection fee $125** (Table 18-3). https://www.arvadaco.gov/1263/Building-Fee-Schedule  (NOTE: existing pricing_body said reinspection $77 — current schedule says $125.)
- Water/sewer service permits: ROW permits only to holders of a valid active General Municipal Contractor's license with Arvada; Arvada-licensed plumbing contractors may permit private-property work only; homeowners may repair on private property but a permit is still required; Colorado 811 locate confirmation number required before approval; traffic control plan for ROW work, site-specific plan on arterials/collectors; street cut moratorium map with pre-construction meeting; services abandoned at the main using a repair clamp; 1-2 business days review. https://www.arvadaco.gov/660/Sewer-Water-Service-Installations-Repair
- Excavation notice, C.R.S. 9-1.5-103: notice to the notification association "at least two business days prior thereto not including the day of actual notice"; within eighteen inches horizontally of a marked facility, nondestructive methods. https://law.justia.com/codes/colorado/title-9/buildings-and-equipment/article-1-5/section-9-1-5-103/

## Arvada water/sewer boundary and reporting
- Sewer: "Maintenance and care of the sanitary sewer service lines from the home up to and including the connection to the City's main sewer line are the responsibility of the homeowner." City maintains over 450 miles of sewer pipe. Wastewater Division 720-898-7770 (after 3:30 pm/weekends 720-898-7070) checks city-owned lines, and **will review a homeowner's televised video inspection and give an unbiased second opinion** by appointment. https://www.arvadaco.gov/439/Sewer-Information
- Water: "the City owns the portion of the line from the drinking water main to the meter pit ... the homeowner owns the portion of the line from the meter pit to their home's foundation." Lead and pre-1960 galvanized service lines prohibited; free replacement program May 1, 2024 through December 31, 2026; fines possible beginning 2027; Water Quality 720-898-7800. https://www.arvadaco.gov/647/Service-Line-Inventory-Replacement-Progr
- "For water line or sewer line breaks, please call 720-898-7070." https://www.arvadaco.gov/898/Report-a-Problem-or-Ask-a-Question ; also https://www.arvadaco.gov/433/Water-Sewer-Stormwater-Systems
- Infrastructure age: "The City's water and sewer systems date to the early 20th century with current components installed starting in the 1950s"; 1960s-1980s funded components "are reaching the end of their useful life"; over 600 miles of water pipe and over 450 miles of wastewater pipe. https://www.arvadaco.gov/661/Utilities-Transparency
- Sources: Ralston Reservoir (Denver Water North System incl. Gross Reservoir) and Arvada Reservoir (Ralston Creek and Clear Creek); two treatment plants, Ralston primary, Arvada plant May-October; over six billion gallons treated. https://www.arvadaco.gov/459/Water-Quality

## Backflow / cross-connection
- Arvada: all backflow prevention assemblies must be inspected and tested annually; since a 2022 schedule change "All assemblies must now be tested by a certified tester by July 31st annually"; non-compliance fee schedule added beginning 2023; program phone 720-898-7793. https://www.arvadaco.gov/450/Backflow-Cross-Connection-Control-Progra
- CDPHE: Regulation 11 section 11.39 backflow rule updated with an effective date of October 15, 2023; HB25-1077 signed March 28, 2025 lets certified cross-connection control technicians inspect, test and repair devices. https://cdphe.colorado.gov/bpccc
- IPC 607.3 text: where a storage water heater is supplied with cold water passing through a check valve, pressure reducing valve or backflow preventer, a thermal expansion tank shall be connected downstream of all such devices. https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCadmin/0-0-0-224170 ; closed-system explanation (tankless with storage tank, dual check at meter) https://www.newcastlede.gov/DocumentCenter/View/17348/2009-IPC-6073-PDF
- Hose connections: sillcocks, hose bibbs and wall hydrants must be protected by an atmospheric or pressure-type vacuum breaker, or a permanently attached hose connection vacuum breaker (608.16.4.2, IPC-based state code text). https://up.codes/s/protection-by-a-vacuum-breaker

## Freeze physics and climate
- "Surprisingly, ice forming in a pipe does not typically cause a break where the ice blockage occurs." "It's not the radial expansion of ice against the wall of the pipe." Pressure rises downstream between the blockage and a closed faucet; upstream the water retreats toward its source; "Usually the pipe bursts where little or no ice has formed." Opening a faucet relieves the pressure. Freezing onset for uninsulated pipe in an unconditioned attic at 20 degrees F or below; a survey of 71 plumbers found bursts appearing when temperatures fell into the teens. https://www.iccsafe.org/wp-content/uploads/DIS-FreezeBurstPipe.pdf
- Underlying research: Jeffrey R. Gordon, 1996, Building Research Council, University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, "An Investigation into Freezing and Bursting Water Pipes in Residential Construction" — bursting is due to elevated water pressure in a confined volume, not ice pushing on pipe walls. https://www.ideals.illinois.edu/items/54859
- NWS Denver/Boulder: record low -29 on January 9, 1875 (lowest in Denver history); 30 occurrences of -20 or colder since 1872; -24 on December 22, 2022; -25 on February 1, 1951 and February 8, 1936. https://www.weather.gov/bou/lowtempextremes and https://www.weather.gov/bou/Climate_Record_January
- Normal lows (1991-2020): January 18-19 degrees, January mean 31.7; February normal lows 19 rising to 23 late month, February mean 32.7. https://www.weather.gov/bou/Climate_Record_January , https://www.weather.gov/bou/Climate_Record_February

## Landlord / habitability
- C.R.S. 38-12-505(1)(b)(IV): premises deemed uninhabitable if it substantially lacks "Running water at all times and hot water in an amount necessary for the tenant to perform all ordinary activities related to maintaining cleanliness and health"; (III) plumbing or gas facilities maintained in good working order. https://law.justia.com/codes/colorado/title-38/tenants-and-landlords/article-12/part-5/section-38-12-505/
- C.R.S. 38-12-503: landlord must contact the tenant not more than twenty-four hours after receiving notice; commence remedial action within twenty-four hours where the condition materially interferes with life, health, or safety, and seventy-two hours where the premises are uninhabitable. https://law.justia.com/codes/colorado/title-38/tenants-and-landlords/article-12/part-5/section-38-12-503/
