# Kyle's Model, Replicated — Then Beaten

**August 29, 2026.** Big-ticket parked. This is the obscure-service, small-city track, screened with live data.

---

## What I did

Kyle's stated formula: obscure services, cities under 100,000 people, avoid plumbing/HVAC/roofing in big metros, avoid personal injury law, build about 10 pages, get 10–30 links.

I ran that formula as a data screen:

1. **707 US cities** with populations between 37,000 and 100,000.
2. **50 obscure services** — Kyle's own named niches (junk removal, epoxy floors, popcorn ceiling removal, septic pumping, towing, auto glass, gutters) plus 43 adjacent ones with real ticket value and low franchise presence.
3. **35,350 service-plus-city combinations**, every one checked for real search volume and cost-per-click. 7,312 came back with measurable demand.
4. **546 survivors** with 70+ monthly searches and $5+ cost-per-click went to a live page-one pull.
5. **1,072 winning local businesses measured** — every one's ranking page downloaded for word count, every one's sitemap read for total page count.
6. **Duplicate city names filtered out.** This mattered: 173 of 364 measured markets had their data polluted by a same-name city elsewhere. "Dumpster rental Cleveland" for Cleveland, Tennessee returns Cleveland, Ohio companies and Ohio's search volume. Those are flagged and pushed down.

**191 clean, verified markets survived. All 70 in the first batch have a domain available.**

## How we beat him, not just match him

He builds 10 pages at roughly 415 words. Against a 9-page winner that's a coin flip. Here's the margin we take:

| | Kyle | Us |
|---|---|---|
| Pages per site | ~10 | **35** — 15 service pages, 15–20 neighboring-town pages |
| Words per page | ~415 | **600–900** — matches what actually wins here (759) |
| Per-city facts | generic | real landmarks, neighborhoods, housing stock, climate pulled per city |
| Design | template | the locked navy-and-gold system, already built and tested |
| Lead capture | phone | phone **plus** the form and lead desk already live |
| Build cost | manual | our build pipeline generates all 35 pages |

Against a 2-page, 229-word site holding number one, a 35-page site with real local content isn't a coin flip.

**And we already have the machine.** The 83 sites gave us the template, the build script, the hosting pipeline, the call routing, the form, and the lead database. Marginal cost of site 84 is a domain and a phone number.

## The one thing that changes strategy

Kyle avoids exactly what we built. Our 83 are garage door repair in Fort Worth, AC repair in Los Angeles, emergency plumbing in Atlanta, personal injury law in San Diego — where winners carry 286 pages. These 191 are dumpster rental in Gulfport and dryer vent cleaning in Ocala, where winners carry 49.

**That's not a reason to abandon the 83.** They're built, hosted, and indexed, and the ranking plan for them stands. But the next 70 should go here, because the same 35-page build that's a long shot in Fort Worth is a favorite in Hagerstown.
