## Santa Clara County bail schedule (local differentiator)

Source: **Santa Clara County 2026 Criminal Bail Schedule** (Penal Code 1269b(d); adopted/effective
January 27, 2026) — https://santaclara.courts.ca.gov/system/files/criminal/2026-criminal-bail-schedule.pdf

- VC 23152(a)–(e), no priors, BAC < 0.15: **$5,000**; BAC 0.15+: **$10,000**
- One prior within 10 years: **$15,000** / **$20,000**
- Two priors: **$35,000** / **$40,000**
- Three priors (felony): **$100,000**; VC 23153 (injury): **$25,000**
- Schedule marks these counts eligible for supervised own recognizance (S.O.R.P.) release

## Arithmetic published on the page

$390 base fine → $390 state penalty + 39 × $5.50 = $214.50 county penalty + $78 surcharge +
$195 court construction + $39 + $156 DNA + $30 + $40 = **≈ $1,533**, described on the page as
"roughly $1,100 in assessments and surcharges" behind the fine, total "near $1,500".
