## Cost rows

The eight ranges are market observations for this metro, not quotes and not sourced figures;
they are informed by the permit and wage anchors above plus the local corrosion, access and
approval variables described in the body. No cost-aggregator content site was used or cited
anywhere in this batch; every named source is a government or federal statistical document.

## Not verified

- No Ann Arbor fee line specifically names a garage door, so the anchor is described as the
  project-cost tier a small door job would fall into if a permit is required. Routine spring,
  cable, roller and opener replacement on an existing opening is normally outside the permit
  process; the body says exactly that rather than implying a permit is always needed.
- The city's electrical permit fee PDF (separate from the building fee sheet) was not retrieved,
  so no dollar figure for an opener circuit permit is published on the page.


## Published price sources, 2026-08-23

All figures below were read on 2026-08-23 from the operator's own website. Ann Arbor is the
thinnest of the five markets: most Washtenaw County door shops publish no prices at all
(garagedoorprosmi.com, dextergaragedoor.com, crawforddoorypsi.com, mandmdoors247.com,
qualityoverheaddoor.com, superdoor.com all post coupons or nothing). Two rows were deleted for
that reason.

- Repair price page, https://www.upanddowndoor.com/michigan-garage-door-repair-2/ (read 2026-08-23):
  trip charge minimum $95; basic service rate $145 plus parts; two-car springs replaced $279-$475;
  one-car $210-$279; Wayne Dalton conversion $550-$775; cable sets $46-$76; gear kit assemblies
  installed $175-$225.
- Installation page, https://www.upanddowndoor.com/garage-door-installation-canton/ (read 2026-08-23):
  1-car non-insulated $1,000-$1,500; 1-car insulated $1,450-$1,850; 2-car non-insulated $1,775-$2,275;
  2-car insulated $2,250-$2,950; openers installed $789 / $889 / $990 / $1,390.

**Ann Arbor Door Systems** (Ann Arbor), https://www.annarbordoor.com/garage-door-springs

**A1 Garage Door Service** (Dexter, Washtenaw County - service area confirmed at

**Michigan Overhead Door** (Ann Arbor / Washtenaw),

### Rows deleted

- **"Rollers and hinges replaced across a 16-foot door"** - only one operator serving Washtenaw
  County posts a roller figure (Rob's $100-$200). Five Star's $50 roller special does not state a
  quantity, so it cannot be matched to a whole-door row.
- **"Door lifted back onto a bent track"** - only Rob's posts a track figure ($125-$200) in this
  market. One source is below the two-source minimum.
- **"Single bottom section replaced on a steel door"** - no operator serving Ann Arbor publishes a
  panel or section price. Rob's panel line reads "varies by door type and panel" with no figure.
