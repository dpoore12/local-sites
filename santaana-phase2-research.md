# Santa Ana PI phase 2 — verified source notes (fetched 2026-08-25)

## Orange County Superior Court / procedure
- Central Justice Center, Santa Ana: handles traffic, criminal, unlimited civil, limited civil, small claims; serves Orange, Santa Ana, Tustin, Villa Park, and **all Orange County cities for limited civil, unlimited civil and small claims**; full-security screening. https://www.occourts.org/location/central-justice-center
- Limited civil = amount in dispute $35,000 or less; unlimited = over $35,000 or non-money; complex = CRC 3.400. https://www.occourts.org/divisions/civil
- Mandatory e-filing: CCP 1010.6, Cal. Rules of Court 2.253(b)(2), OCSC Local Rule 352, Admin Order 13/03. Attorneys must e-file in limited, unlimited and complex civil. Self-represented parties exempt. E-filed docs timely until **midnight** on due date (CCP 1010.6(d)(1)(D)); if same-day service required, file by **5 p.m.**; received on/after midnight = stamped next court day. Bench warrants, subpoenaed documents, bonds, undertakings may not be e-filed. https://www.occourts.org/online-services/efiling/efiling-civil
- Local Rule 301: **personal injury** actions are subject to civil case management. Rule 303: random assignment to one judicial officer for all purposes. Rule 308: all complex-civil papers e-filed; PDF; scans 200–400 dpi; >50 MB may be split; exemption by written ex parte application. Rule 315: meet and confer within **20 days** after service of responsive pleading; joint Meet and Confer Statement (L-0964) within **30 days**. Rule 316: Settlement Conference Statement lodged **5 court days** before MSC; settlement demand served at least 5 days before. https://www.occourts.org/system/files/civil/09div3.pdf
- Civil Case Time Schedule: proof of service of complaint due day **60** (CRC 3.110(b)); Case Management Conference / order deadline day **180**, 45 days' notice, CM-110 filed 15 calendar days before (CRC 3.722(b), 3.725(a)). https://www.occourts.org/system/files/civil/civilcasetimeschedule.pdf
- Self-help: CMC usually **120–180 days** from filing. https://www.occourts.org/self-help/self-help-civil/before-trial

## Government claims (dangerous condition of public property)
- Gov. Code 835 elements (dangerous condition, proximate cause, foreseeable risk, employee-created or notice under 835.2). https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=835.&lawCode=GOV
- Gov. Code 911.2(a): injury claim presented **not later than six months** after accrual; other causes one year. Gov. Code 910: claim over **$10,000** states no dollar amount, only whether it would be a limited civil case. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&division=3.6.&title=1.&part=3.&chapter=2.&article=1.
- Gov. Code 945.6: suit within **six months** after written rejection notice is delivered or mailed (913), or **two years** from accrual if no notice given. (verified by screenshot of leginfo GOV 945.6)
- County of Orange claim form: injury/personal property claims within **six (6) months from date of loss**; other losses within one year (Gov. Code 911.2); file with **Clerk of the Board of Supervisors, 400 W. Civic Center Drive, 6th Floor, Santa Ana**. https://ocsheriff.gov/sites/ocsd/files/2022-08/County%20Claim%20Form.pdf
- City of Santa Ana claims: original form with **wet signature**, delivered or mailed to City Clerk, **20 Civic Center Plaza, 8th Floor**; "Electronic service will not be accepted"; third-party administrator AdminSure sends confirmation within two weeks; filing after six months but within one year requires an **Application for Leave to Present a Late Claim**. https://www.santa-ana.org/claims/
- Streets & Highways Code 5610: owners of lots fronting a public street must maintain the sidewalk so it will not endanger persons or property. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=SHC&sectionNum=5610.

## Statutes of limitation and damages
- CCP 335.1: two years — assault, battery, or injury to or death of an individual caused by the wrongful act or neglect of another. (screenshot verified) https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CCP&sectionNum=335.1.
- CCP 340.5: three years after date of injury OR one year after plaintiff discovers/should have discovered the injury, whichever occurs first; three-year limit tolled only for proof of fraud, intentional concealment, or a non-therapeutic foreign body; minors within three years, under age 6 → three years or before 8th birthday, whichever is longer. (screenshot verified)
- CCP 364(a): no professional-negligence action may be commenced unless defendant given at least **90 days'** prior notice of intent; (b) no particular form; (d) notice served within 90 days of expiration extends the filing time by **90 days** from service. (screenshot verified)
- Civil Code 3333.2 (as amended by AB 35, 2022): (b) non-death actions — $350,000 caps against providers collectively, institutions collectively, and unaffiliated defendants; (c) wrongful death — $500,000 tiers; (g) effective for cases filed on/after Jan 1, 2023, then subdivision (b) amounts rise **$40,000 each January 1 for 10 years up to $750,000** and subdivision (c) amounts rise **$50,000 each January 1 up to $1,000,000**; the amount in effect at judgment/award/settlement applies; (h) 2% annual inflation adjustment beginning Jan 1, 2034. Arithmetic for 2026 (three January increases after 2023): **$470,000** per (b) tier; **$650,000** per (c) tier. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=3333.2. and AB 35 text https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billTextClient.xhtml?bill_id=202120220AB35
- BPC 6146: contingency limits in health-care professional negligence — 25% of recovery where all parties sign release before complaint/arbitration demand filed; 33% after filing; "recovered" = net after disbursements/costs; plaintiff's medical care costs and attorney office overhead are NOT deductible; motion for higher fee on good cause; periodic payments under CCP 667.7 valued on projected life expectancy. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=6146.
- Civil Code 1431.2 (Prop 51): noneconomic damages several only, in direct proportion to each defendant's fault; economic damages defined as objectively verifiable monetary losses. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1431.2.
- Civil Code 3333.4 (Prop 213): no noneconomic recovery where injured person was convicted DUI driver, was the owner of an uninsured vehicle in the accident, or was an operator who cannot establish financial responsibility; exception where the other motorist was convicted of DUI. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=3333.4.&lawCode=CIV
- Civil Code 1714: everyone responsible for injury occasioned by want of ordinary care in management of property or person, except so far as the injured person brought it on himself. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1714.
- Pure comparative negligence: Cal. Supreme Court, B.B. v. County of Los Angeles (S250734) — "In 1975, in Li … we abolished the contributory negligence defense and replaced it with 'a system of "pure" comparative negligence' that 'assess[es] liability in proportion to negligence.'" https://supreme.courts.ca.gov/sites/default/files/supremecourt/default/2022-08/S250734.pdf
- Veh. Code 16056(a)(2): for policies issued or renewed on/after Jan 1, 2025 — $30,000 per person / $60,000 per accident bodily injury, $15,000 property damage (previously $15,000/$30,000/$5,000). https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=VEH&sectionNum=16056.
- Comm. Code 2314: implied warranty of merchantability — goods must be fit for the ordinary purposes for which such goods are used, and adequately contained, packaged and labeled. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=COM&sectionNum=2314.

## Federal product record
- CPSC recall notices instruct consumers to stop using immediately; remedies published as full refund, free repair, free replacement; unit counts published per recall (e.g. about 46,200 power stations, about 21,040 off-road motorcycles, Aug 20 2026 notices). https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls
- NHTSA: requires manufacturers to recall vehicles/equipment with safety-related defects; remedy by repair, replacement, refund, or rarely repurchase; 17-character VIN on lower left windshield; VIN lookup shows open recalls; lookup excludes recalls more than 15 years old; owner complaints feed defect investigations. https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls

## Traffic safety
- OTS 2023 rankings, Santa Ana (Group A, average population 313,210, DVMT 2,845,860): total fatal & injury victims 1,972 → rank 4/15; pedestrians 157 → 5/15; pedestrians under 15: 17 → 3/15; bicyclists 115 → 4/15; speed-related fatal & injury collisions 319 → 3/15; nighttime 219 → 5/15; hit and run 229 → 5/15. https://www.ots.ca.gov/rankings/santa-ana-2023/
- OTS methodology: Group A = cities over 250,000 population; rank 1 is the worst in the group; Empirical Bayesian ranking using SWITRS crash data, DOF population, Caltrans DVMT. https://www.ots.ca.gov/media-and-research/crash-rankings/
- Santa Ana Vision Zero Action Plan (April 2024), collisions 2017–2021: 906 pedestrian collisions, 33 pedestrian fatalities, 119 pedestrian severe injuries (16% of pedestrian collisions were severe or fatal); 603 roadway users killed or seriously injured overall; pedestrian and bicyclist deaths/serious injuries follow primary and major arterials — 1st Street, 17th Street, Warner Avenue, Bristol Street, Fairview Street, Harbor Boulevard; city maintains over 400 centerline miles of streets. https://storage.googleapis.com/proudcity/santaanaca/2024/05/Santa-Ana-Vision-Zero-Action-Plan-April-2024.pdf
