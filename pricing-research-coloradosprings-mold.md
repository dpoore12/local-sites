# Pricing research — coloradospringsmoldremediationexperts.com (Colorado Springs, CO / mold remediation)

All figures retrieved 2026-08-23. Every URL below was fetched in this session. American spelling enforced throughout ("mold", never the British form, which the build rejects).

## Anchors

1. **EPA, Mold Cleanup in Your Home** — <https://www.epa.gov/mold/mold-cleanup-your-home>
   What it actually says: "If the moldy area is less than about 10 square feet (less than roughly a 3 ft. by 3 ft. patch), in most cases, you can handle the job yourself." It also directs that the water problem be fixed first, that mold be scrubbed off hard surfaces with detergent and water and dried completely, and that absorbent or porous materials such as ceiling tile and carpet may have to be thrown away. It does **not** set a legal limit — it is guidance about DIY feasibility.
2. **EPA, Mold Remediation in Schools and Commercial Buildings, chapter 3** — <https://www.epa.gov/mold/mold-remediation-schools-and-commercial-buildings-guide-chapter-3>
   Recommends **limited containment** for areas of roughly 10 to 100 square feet of contamination and **full containment** for areas greater than 100 square feet, or where an occupied space is adjacent and dust dispersion is likely. This is the threshold that changes the cost structure (poly enclosure, negative air, HEPA exhaust, respirators, decontamination chamber).
3. **Colorado HB25-1202, Mold Awareness and Registration Act** — <https://leg.colorado.gov/bills/hb25-1202>
   Bill status: **Lost**. Colorado therefore has no state license or registration for mold remediation contractors. Credentials on a van are private-body certifications, not state authorization.
4. **Colorado seller's property disclosure, form SPD19-6-23** — <https://dre.colorado.gov/sites/dre/files/documents/2023-08-07%20Seller_s%20Property%20Disclosure-Residential%20(fillable).pdf>
   Mandatory January 2024. Section on structural conditions / environmental conditions asks the seller about moisture and water problems and about the presence of mold or other biological hazards; the form warns of liability for failing to disclose a known adverse material fact.

Also fetched, cited only as prose: Colorado Springs Utilities residential rate sheet (<https://www.csu.org/hubfs/Document%20Library/ResidentialRateSheet.pdf?hsLang=en>) — water service charge $0.8512 per meter per day inside city, commodity $0.0578 per cubic foot for the first 999 cf; used only to support "billed at the residential rates Colorado Springs Utilities publishes." PPRBD fee schedule (<https://www.pprbd.org/Information/FeeSchedule>) fetched but no mold or remediation line exists in it, so no permit anchor was used on this site.

### Arithmetic (per-unit totaled to a stated quantity)

- **Lab sample row.** Mold Removal Express posts $150–$300 **per sample location**. The row's stated quantity is three locations (two rooms plus one outdoor control): 3 × $150 = **$450**, 3 × $300 = **$900**. The row high of 900 is that product; the row low of 300 is The Premier Clean's posted range floor for air-and-surface sampling. Basis string states the assumed three sample locations.
- No inspection fee at any cited company is stated to be credited toward remediation, so nothing was netted out. (Contrast: the Fort Worth foundation company excluded below does credit its fee — that concept appears in body prose on the Austin foundation site, not here.)

### Job-matching discipline

Testing prices were kept strictly out of remediation rows and vice versa. Pages that post **only** testing (Air Test Co $380 no-lab / $680 with 3 interior + 1 control / $90 per extra sample; Elevation Environmental mold clearance from $450, air clearance from $750; Rex Environmental, no prices) were not used to bound a remediation row. Water-damage-restoration prices (MyVoda $1,200–$5,000; Absolute's water rows; The Premier Clean's water blog $6,000+ mold figure) were likewise not used as mold remediation bounds.

### Excluded sources

- `coloradospringsrestorationservice.com` — templated multi-city doorway pattern; excluded despite posting a "Mold Remediation $2,500–$6,000" crawl-space line.
- `moldremovalexpress.com/colorado-springs-mold-removal/` and `/mold-removal-cost/` — no dollar figures posted at all ("Send photos. Get a real number.").
- Signature Inspecting's $250 "moisture inspection" was not counted as a mold inspection because the page does not describe it as one.

### Market note (report-worthy)

**This is the weakest data market of the five.** Most Colorado Springs operators publish mold **testing** prices and refuse to publish **remediation** prices, so the remediation rows rest on four companies that do post ranges: The Premier Clean, Absolute Water Damage and Mitigation, Elyon Restoration, and Restoration King. Elyon serves the Front Range including Colorado Springs but is Denver-centered; it was used only where a second local source already anchored the row.
