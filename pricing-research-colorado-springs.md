### Arithmetic for itemized totals

- **Emergency callout charge, $49-349:** Clean Water Co posts a $49 service call/diagnostic; Absolute Excavating posts an emergency or after-hours premium of $150-300 added to the call -> $49 + $300 = **$349** at the top. Row job wording was changed to "Emergency callout charge before any repair work begins" so it describes the charge these two pages actually publish, not a whole repair.

### Row-by-row mapping

1. Emergency callout charge $49-349 - itemized above.
2. Burst or frozen pipe $314-1,695 - PlumbSmart average Colorado Springs repair from $314; WireNut posted pipe repair to $1,695 (its in-wall/under-slab adder of up to $1,210 would push higher; not added, so the row stays inside what one company posts).
