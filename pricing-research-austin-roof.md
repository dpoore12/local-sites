# Pricing research — austinroofinspectionpros.com (Austin, TX / roof inspection)

All figures retrieved 2026-08-23 unless noted. Every URL below was fetched in this session.

## Anchors

1. **TREC standards of practice, roof covering — 22 TAC §535.228(c)**
   <https://www.trec.texas.gov/online-sops>
   Verbatim: inspector shall "inspect the roof covering materials from the surface of the roof"; not required to inspect from roof level if unsafe; not required to determine remaining life expectancy, number of layers, or identify latent hail damage. §535.228(d) covers roof structures and attics. Full SOP = 22 TAC §§535.227–535.233.
2. **TREC fee schedule** (effective Dec 15, 2025)
   <https://www.trec.texas.gov/agency-information/fee-schedule>
   Apprentice Inspector application $60 / renewal $30; Real Estate Inspector $100 / $50; Professional Inspector $120 / $60.
3. **City of Austin, work exempt from building permits**
   <https://www.austintexas.gov/development-services/work-exempt-building-permits>
   Item 12: "Asphalt shingles that replace existing asphalt shingles, unless the property is located in the Wildland-Urban Interface area and 50% or more of the roofing is being replaced." Item 13: same treatment for any roof covering not adversely affecting roof structure.

**Checked and deliberately not used:** TDI designated catastrophe areas (<https://www.tdi.texas.gov/wind/maps/index.html>) — windstorm inspection requirements are coastal; Austin/Travis County is not a designated catastrophe area, so no TDI anchor belongs on this page. Clay-soil citation for this site is the Travis County AgriLife page (<https://travis-tx.tamu.edu/about-2/horticulture/soils-and-composting-for-austin/the-real-dirt-on-austin-area-soils/>, Blackland Prairie clays "shrink and swell when exposed to moisture"), used as prose only; the foundation site cites USDA soil series instead so the two Austin sites do not share a soil source.

**BLS:** no wage anchor used. `bls.gov/oes/current/oes_12420.htm` returns 403 to scripts; series ID for Austin-Round Rock roofers would be `OEUM001242000000047218108` (area 0012420, occ 47-2181, datatype 08). Not retrieved — earlier batches hit the keyless API daily threshold. Recorded here as unverified; no wage figure appears on the page.

### Arithmetic / job-matching notes

- The 2,000 sq ft home-inspection row is a **stated quantity** row: all three sources price by square-footage tier, and the tier containing 2,000 sq ft was used from each ($395 / $400 / $475). No multiplication involved.
- Free "storm inspections" advertised by RISE ("$0–$500" overall; leak diagnosis at no charge) were **not** used as a low bound; a free sales visit is not the inspection product the row describes.
- Home-inspection add-ons are priced two ways (bundled vs standalone). The row uses the bundled figure as low and the standalone as high, and the note says so.
- Noble PI (<https://noble-pi.com/blogs/home-inspection-cost-austin>: most $375–$675, average $510 under 2,500 sq ft, mold add-on $295, sewer $295) and Careful Home Inspections (<https://carefulhomeinspections.com/home-inspection-pricing/>: $325–$595 by tier, +$100 for 20-year-old homes, +$125 pier and beam, $175 re-inspection) were read but not cited; three sources already covered each row and a re-inspection row could not reach two hostnames.
