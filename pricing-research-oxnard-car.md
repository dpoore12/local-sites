## Headline finding

Same California rule set as the Modesto page, but every court cost figure here is taken
from the **Ventura County Superior Court's own published amounts** rather than the
Judicial Council schedule, and different line items are used (answer per defendant,
motion after first appearance, appeal, small claims). California has no percentage
ceiling for an auto injury contingent fee; the numeric ceilings in Bus. & Prof. Code 6146
reach only professional negligence claims against health care providers.
