## Headline finding

Pennsylvania sets **no numeric percentage cap** on a contingent fee in an auto injury
claim. Pa. R.P.C. 1.5(a) forbids an "illegal or clearly excessive fee" and lists eight
factors; Rule 1.5(c) governs the writing. The only numeric counsel-fee ceiling found in
Pennsylvania law is the **20%** limit in Section 442 of the Workers' Compensation Act.
The MCARE Act (Act 13 of 2002) was read for a fee ceiling and **contains none** — its
Section 509 addresses periodic payment of future damages after "the proportionate share
of counsel fees and costs," not a percentage limit.
