## Match-the-figure notes

- i30's figures are flat package prices, not "starting at," so they are used as both lows and highs where the package matches the row.
- Texas Pro's $28,000–45,000 mid-range is a rip-and-replace, so it supports row 4, not row 2's refresh band.
- "Often starts around" figures (OnPoint) are used only as lows, never as highs.
- Silicon-style per-hour trade labor ($45–200/hr, Texas Pro) and tile at $8–20/sq ft are quoted only in prose context, not as a row.
- Every row cites 2–4 distinct hostnames; verified programmatically.
