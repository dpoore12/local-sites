# Pricing research — charlotteguttercleaningpros.com (Charlotte, NC / gutter cleaning)

All figures retrieved 2026-08-23. Every URL below was fetched in this session.

## Anchors

1. **City of Charlotte storm water fees** — <https://www.charlottenc.gov/Services/Stormwater/Fees>
   Residential monthly fee tiers by impervious area, effective July 1, 2026: under 2,000 sq ft $10.32; 2,000–2,999 $14.78; 3,000–4,999 $21.43; 5,000+ $35.48. Tier 2 ($14.78) used as the headline value.
2. **N.C. General Statutes 87-1** — <https://www.ncleg.net/enactedlegislation/statutes/html/bysection/chapter_87/gs_87-1.html>
   A "general contractor" is one who undertakes to construct/alter/repair any building where "the cost of the undertaking is forty thousand dollars ($40,000) or more," and must be licensed. **This is the actual threshold** — gutter cleaning and ordinary gutter repair/replacement sit below it; the licensing question in this trade is therefore an insurance question, not a license question.
3. **NCLBGC classifications and limitations** — <https://nclbgc.org/classifications-and-limitations/>
   Limited license: $750,000 per project; Intermediate: $1,500,000; Unlimited: no restriction.

Also fetched, not used: Charlotte Water rates (<https://www.charlottenc.gov/water/Pay-your-Bill/Rates-Fees>, sewer $6.88/Ccf) and the city storm-water explainer (<https://www.charlottenc.gov/City-News/Here%E2%80%99s-How-Storm-Water-Fees-Make-a-Difference>).

### Arithmetic (per-unit rows totaled to a stated quantity)

- **Guards, 150 linear feet assumed** (basis string says "per 150 linear feet installed"):
  - Gleaming Gutters: $9–$14/LF installed → the page itself states $1,350–$2,100 for ~150 ft. $9 × 150 = **$1,350** = row low. (<https://www.gleaminggutters.com/gutter-guards>)
  - Keyway Construction: micro-mesh aluminum frame $10–$14/LF → page states $1,500–$2,100 at 150 LF; screen $3–$5/LF → $450–$750 at 150 LF (<https://www.keywayconstructionclt.com/gutter-guard-cost-charlotte-nc/>)
  - Best Roofing Now: micro-mesh $10–$18/ft → page states $1,500–$2,700 at 150 ft; multi-story adds $1–$5/ft (<https://www.bestroofingnow.com/gutter-guard-cost>)
  - Clean Pro Charlotte: surgical-grade 304 stainless micro-mesh quoted at **$22.50 per linear foot** → 22.50 × 150 = **$3,375** = row high.
- **Best Choice Roofing** replacement: $12.00/LF × 150 LF = $1,800, inside the 1,000–4,000 band; the band's endpoints come from Best Roofing Now (low) and Vision Roofing (high) as posted totals, so no multiplication was needed for the bounds.
- **Squeegee Pros packed-debris surcharge** ($50–$100) and its $79 blockage-guarantee service fee are stated in the body prose as a surcharge, not stacked into a cleaning row.
