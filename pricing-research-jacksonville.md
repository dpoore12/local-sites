### Arithmetic for itemized totals

- **Slab leak row, $5,450 high:** 904 Plumbers prices detection and repair as separate line items - detection $150-450 plus repair $1,500-5,000+ -> $450 + $5,000 = **$5,450** for a located and repaired slab leak. The low, $550, is Rolland Reash's posted starting price for a slab leak repair.

### Emergency pricing in this market

Only The Greatful Plumber states that emergency calls cost more, and it publishes no surcharge figure. 904 Plumbers prices a same-day emergency water heater swap at the same $1,100-2,600 as a scheduled one. Roto-Rooter Jacksonville advertises no extra charge for nights and weekends. So no after-hours multiplier was applied to any Jacksonville row.
