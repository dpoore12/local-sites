# Citation and directory targets

Built 7 Sep 2026 from the actual referring domains of `stanleywoodard.com`, the
weakest site on page one for "jonesboro personal injury lawyer" (67 referring
domains, ranks 9th). Our Jonesboro site has 3. The gap to page one in that market
is roughly 70 referring domains, not the 488 portfolio median - small markets have
a far lower bar.

The 67 sort into three groups, and the split is the finding.

## Group A - general business directories: GETTABLE

Take a business name, a phone and a service area. No bar licence, and a street
address is optional or hidden on most.

## Group B - attorney directories: BLOCKED FOR US

This is the important one. These require a licensed attorney and a bar number:

superlawyers.com · nacdl.org · thenationaltriallawyers.org · lawinfo.com ·
lawyerslisted.com · bestattorneysofamerica.com · attorneyhelp.org ·
lawfirmlookup.com · toplawdog.com · attorney4injury.com · top10accidentlawyers.com ·
top100lawyers.com · top100criminaldefenseattorneys.com · juridipedia.com ·
lawresolution.com · 101attorney.com · localpersonalinjury.net ·
distinguishedjusticeadvocates.com · getpracticehelp.com · injury-attorney-lawyer.com

**We are not a law firm and cannot list on any of them.** That is roughly 20 of the
67 - the single largest gettable block for a real competitor, and it is closed to
us. It is only open through a signed attorney tenant listing their own firm.

This is why the 40 legal sites are structurally harder than the 43 home-services
sites, and it is not a content problem.

## Group C - junk that arrived on its own: DO NOT PURSUE

Roughly 30 domains with spam scores of 50-80: fashionclothingnews.com,
uncledspizza.com, betwinnermirror.com, quero.party, screenshots.wiki,
australianwebdirectory.pro, wheretobuybest.link and similar. Scrapers and link
farms nobody built deliberately. They inflate a competitor's count and are not
worth chasing or paying for.

## What this actually means

Of the 67 domains behind a page-one Jonesboro ranking:

- ~20 need a bar licence we do not have
- ~30 are junk that accumulates without effort
- **~17 are genuinely gettable by us today**

So the honest ceiling on direct citation work is roughly 15-20 referring domains
per site, against a target near 70. Directory submission is worth doing - going
from 3 to 20 is real - but it does not close the gap on its own for a legal site.

The rest has to come from a tenant (who brings their own bar-verified listings) or
from the slower accumulation that any live site picks up over time.

## Cannot be automated

Every one of these requires account creation, and several use CAPTCHAs. The
research and the submission data can be prepared per site; the submitting is
manual.
