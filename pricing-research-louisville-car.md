Mode: `fees`, fee_kind `contingency`. Retrieval date for every item: **2026-08-23**.

## Headline finding

Kentucky sets **no numeric percentage ceiling** on a contingent fee in a motor vehicle
injury claim. SCR 3.130(1.5)(a) bars an "unreasonable fee or an unreasonable amount for
expenses" and lists eight factors. Kentucky's one hard numeric fee schedule is in
workers' compensation, KRS 342.320, which is tiered and carries an **$18,000** ceiling.
