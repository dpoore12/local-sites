## Headline finding

California caps contingent fees **only** in claims against health care providers for
professional negligence (Bus. & Prof. Code 6146). For an ordinary motor vehicle injury
claim there is no percentage ceiling: the limit is Rule 1.5(a) of the California Rules of
Professional Conduct ("unconscionable or illegal fee") plus the contract requirements of
Bus. & Prof. Code 6147, which requires the contract to state that the rate **is not set
by law and is negotiable** whenever section 6146 does not apply.
