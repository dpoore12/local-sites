# Boca Raton garage door — verified research notes (fetched 2026-08-25)

## Florida / Palm Beach County code

- **Wind-borne debris region definition + county-wide applicability.** Palm Beach County
  Planning, Zoning & Building wind speed map page: "WIND-BORNE DEBRIS REGION: Areas within
  hurricane-prone regions located: 1. Within 1 mile (1.61 km) of the coastal mean high water
  line where the ultimate design wind speed Vult is 130 mph (48 m/s) or greater; or 2. In areas
  where the ultimate design wind speed Vult is 140 mph (53m/s) or greater. Linear interpolation
  between contours may not be utilized... All of Unincorporated Palm Beach County is within the
  Wind Borne Debris Region."
  https://discover.pbc.gov/pzb/Maps/Wind-Speeds.aspx
- **Adopted code edition.** "PBC Amendments to the Florida Building Code, 8th Edition (2023) -
  12/31/2023" — https://discover.pbc.gov/pzb/building/pages/building-code.aspx
  Florida Building Code home: "The Effective Date for the Florida Building Code, 8th Edition
  (2023), is December 31, 2023." https://www.floridabuilding.org/
- **PBC amendment 110.3.13 / 110.3 item 10 (impact-resistant coverings inspection).** Building
  official shall inspect to determine "The system indicated on the plans was installed" and "The
  system is installed in accordance with the manufacturer's installation instructions and the
  product approval."
  https://discover.pbcgov.org/pzb/building/BuildingCodes/PBC-Amendments-to-the-Florida-Building-Code-8th-Edition-2023.pdf
- **PBC amendment 105.15 opening protection** — $50,000 improvement cost / $750,000 insured or
  just value trigger for site-built single-family detached in the wind-borne debris region. Same PDF.
- **Garage doors in FBC.** FBC Building 8th Ed. Section 1609.1.2.3: "Garage door glazed opening
  protection for wind-borne debris shall meet the requirements of an approved impact-resisting
  standard or ANSI/DASMA 115." Section 1609.7 + Table 1609.7(1) gives garage door design
  pressures; at Vult 170 mph a 16 ft x 7 ft door (roof angle >10 deg) is +26.4 / -29.4 psf.
  Table 1609.7(2) exposure/height adjustment: Exposure C at 15 ft mean roof height = 1.21;
  Exposure D at 15 ft = 1.47. Table values include the 0.6 load reduction factor. Minimum
  positive and negative wind load 10 psf.
  https://up.codes/viewer/florida/fl-building-code-2023/chapter/16/structural-design

## Product approval / NOA

- **Palm Beach County product approval page.** Implementation date of the Florida Product
  Approval System was October 1, 2003. Rule 61G20-3 F.A.C. Category 1 of the eight covered
  categories is "EXTERIOR DOORS (rollup, sectional, sliding, swinging, automatic; pre-engineered
  roof access hatches, or other)". Local approval per FS 553.8425, Rule 61G20-3, PPM PBO-118.
  https://discover.pbc.gov/pzb/building/pages/product-approval.aspx
- **Statewide search.** floridabuilding.org Product or Application Search: search by FL# or
  manufacturer; "the code version is the 2023 Florida Building Code which became effective
  December 31, 2023." Categories include Exterior Doors and Impact Protective Systems. Search
  fields include HVHZ approved yes/no, impact resistance yes/no, design pressure. Approval
  statuses: Approved, Validated, Pending FBC Approval, Revoked, Suspended, Denied.
  https://www.floridabuilding.org/pr/pr_app_srch.aspx
- **Miami-Dade Product Control Search.** Search by File Number (e.g. 01-0712.04) or advanced
  parameters: File Classification, Applicant, Category, Subcategory, Material, Impact Rating,
  Maximum Design Pressure (+), Maximum Design Pressure (-).
  https://www.miamidade.gov/building/pc-search_app.asp
- **FS 553.842 Product evaluation and approval** — commission adopts rules for a statewide
  product evaluation and approval system; long-term approvals "valid until the requirements of
  the code on which the approval is based change, the product changes in a manner affecting its
  performance as required by the code, or the approval is revoked."
  https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0500-0599/0553/Sections/0553.842.html

## City of Boca Raton

- **170 mph / Exposure C.** Over-the-counter windows, doors and shutters affidavit, single-family
  residence: "Applicant has read and understands Memorandum dated, January 1st, 2018 that the
  wind velocity used for single family residence is 170 mph and that all buildings or structures
  shall be considered Exposure C unless Exposure D applies." Also: "complete and legible Product
  Approval or Notice of Acceptance (NOA) of all products installed are to be on site for
  inspection purposes, failure to provide Product Approval or Notice of Acceptance (NOA) may
  result in a failed inspection with re-inspection fees assessed." Documents required at
  application include a "Recorded and Certified copy of Notice of Commencement (for projects
  greater than $5,000)". Water infiltration testing per FBC 1709.5.1 and FBC R609.3 exceptions.
  https://www.myboca.us/DocumentCenter/View/22225/Single-Family-Windows-Doors-Shutters-Affidavit-PDF
- **Permit exemption code, § 19-67.** Building permits not required for "Alteration, replacement
  or repair work to a 1- and 2-family dwelling performed by a qualified owner-builder or properly
  licensed contractor having value of less than $1,000.00 within any 12-month period," provided
  the work does not adversely affect, among other things, "Building wind load protection
  including roofing, windows, doors and shutters" or "Structural components".
  http://bocaraton.elaws.us/code/coor_ch19_artiii_div1_sec19-67
- **§ 19-96 Opening protection.** Improvement to a site built detached 1- and 2-family dwelling
  requiring a permit with estimated cost of $50,000.00 or more, in the wind borne debris region,
  insured value $750,000.00 or more (or just valuation $750,000.00 or more) must provide opening
  protections as required for new construction. Ord. No. 5455, 5-22-18.
  http://bocaraton.elaws.us/code/coor_ch19_artiii_div1_sec19-96
- **Shoreline.** "the City's over 4.75 miles of shoreline" — https://www.myboca.us/364/Coastal-Management
- **Fees (from site.json, city user fee schedule):** $100 minimum alteration/repair permit fee
  covering first $500 of valuation then 1.75 percent of remainder; $99 garage door permit for
  storm damage repair within one year of a named storm (retrofitting excluded); $79 reinspection.
  https://myboca.us/DocumentCenter/View/22579/User-Fees-Schedule-Building-Permit-Section-PDF

## Openers — 16 CFR 1211

- "This standard applies to all residential garage door operators manufactured on or after
  January 1, 1993 for sale in the United States."
  https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2015-title16-vol2/pdf/CFR-2015-title16-vol2-part1211.pdf
- 1211.6(a): inherent primary entrapment protection required; 1211.6(b): plus constant-pressure
  control, external secondary device, or inherent secondary device. 1211.6(c): mechanical switch
  or relay in an entrapment protection circuit "shall withstand 100,000 cycles of operation".
  1211.6(d): on switch/relay malfunction the operator must move the door to and stay within
  1 foot (305 mm) of the uppermost position. https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/1211.6
- 1211.7(b)(1)(i): other than the first 1 foot of travel from full upmost position, the operator
  "shall initiate reversal of the door within 2 seconds of contact with the obstruction," then
  return the door to and stop at the full upmost position. 1211.7(b)(2): test with the operator
  "exerting a 25-lbf (111.21-N) pull or its rated pull, whichever is greater."
  1211.7(b)(3)(i): floor test object "shall be 1 inch (25.4 mm) high."
  1211.7(b)(1)(iii): door cannot be moved downward until the operator reverses the door a minimum
  of 2 inches (50.8 mm). https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/1211.7

## DASMA

- ANSI/DASMA 102-2004 §6.1: "Residential and commercial door systems shall be designed to operate
  at a minimum of 10,000 cycles when they are properly selected, installed, operated, and
  maintained." §2.2 Cycle: "One cycle of a door is complete when it is moved from the closed
  position to the fully open position and returned to the closed position." §6.2 cycle life
  testing per ANSI/DASMA 109. §9.3 torsion springs shall not balance sectional doors with an
  index ratio of less than 6 to 1 (mean coil diameter six times wire diameter). §9.3.1.1 tubular
  torsion shaft deflection limit 1.5 degrees per foot; §9.3.1.2 solid shaft 2 degrees per foot;
  linear deflection limit one tenth inch (2.5 mm) per foot.
  https://law.resource.org/pub/us/cfr/regulations.gov.docket.15/dasma.102.2004.html
- DASMA TDS 190 "Factors Affecting Spring Cycle Life": "Springs should be kept dry to prevent
  surface rust development." "Rust reduces the effective area of spring wire, which in turn
  reduces its overall strength." "Rust can also cause corrosion pits from which fatigue cracks
  can accelerate and reduce cycle life." Also: applying a spring to the wrong door, wrong number
  of turns, nicks from dropping/throwing a spring. Spring cycle life may not coincide with door
  cycle life; ANSI/DASMA 109 tests door cycle life.
  https://www.dasma.com/wp-content/uploads/pubs/TechDataSheets/CommercialResidential/TDS190.pdf
- DASMA TDS 155v (ASCE 7-16 wind load guide): tables 100–200 mph; "The wind loads in this TDS
  have been adjusted using an allowable stress design load factor of 0.6 as per ASCE 7-16
  Section 2.4.1"; "Basic Wind Speeds above are three second peak-gust values"; "Garage doors
  evaluated as Components and Cladding"; "Installation details vary. Consult manufacturer's
  instructions." ANSI/DASMA 108 used to determine structural load performance.
  https://www.dasma.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/TDS155v-ASCE-7-16.pdf

## Neighborhoods and housing stock

- **Pearl City** established 1915 "for Black residents south of what is today Glades Road between
  Dixie and Federal Highway"; "Boca Raton's oldest community"; likely named for the Hawaiian
  Pearl pineapple. https://bocahistory.org/virtual-exhibition/pearl-city/
  City designated sites list: "Pearl City Historic District ca. 1915".
  https://bocahistory.org/designated-historic-sites/
- **Old Floresta.** "In 1925, the Mizner Development Corporation purchased a tract of land in Boca
  Raton from five Chicago businessmen." "A total of twenty-nine residences, based on ten different
  house plans designed by Addison Mizner, had been constructed in the subdivision by the end of
  1927." Aiken House at 801 Hibiscus Street constructed 1926, Mediterranean Revival; a one-story
  garage and guest house built 1927 sits west of the main residence.
  https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/c4c78b3b-f2ed-42b4-8f36-0589fa245d9f
- **Camino Gardens.** "Camino Gardens arose on the site of a former Africa theme park, Africa
  U.S.A. The park closed in 1961 and by 1962 the new development was underway featuring houses
  designed by local architect Howard McCall."
  https://bocahistory.org/virtual-exhibition/those-were-the-days/
- **Boca Raton Riviera.** "In 1945, present-day Riviera was platted in four separate stages."
  Units A, B & D owned by Boca Raton Riviera, Inc.; Unit C by Abraham and Amelia Garzo. "A few
  homes still date from the late 40's and early 50's."
  https://rivieracivicassociation.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/History-of-Boca-Raton-4-11-2024.pdf
- **Growth / build era.** Arvida founded 1956 after Davis bought the Boca Raton Hotel & Club for
  $22,500,000 and about 1,500 acres. City population 992 in 1950, 6,961 in 1960, roughly 28,500
  by 1972. https://bocahistory.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/052-Vol.-XXVII-October-2025.pdf
- **Owner-occupied housing unit rate, 2019-2023: 65.8%** — Census QuickFacts, Boca Raton city.
  https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/bocaratoncityflorida/PST040224
- **Hurricane Wilma**, October 24, 2005, widespread wind damage in Boca Raton.
  https://www.wpc.ncep.noaa.gov/tropical/rain/wilma2005.html
