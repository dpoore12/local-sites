## Notes and limits

- Santa Barbara's court publishes the statewide schedule rather than a local one; the
  amounts above were read off the copy hosted on the court's own domain. The 2026
  statewide schedule (retrieved same day) carries the identical $435 / $370 / $225 / $150
  / $60 / $500 / $1,000 figures.
- The court's filing-fees index page (https://www.santabarbara.courts.ca.gov/forms-filing/filing-fees,
  retrieved 2026-08-23) lists the schedules but prints no amounts, so it is not used as a
  figure source.
- No local courthouse-construction surcharge applies in Santa Barbara County; the
  statewide schedule flags surcharges only for Riverside, San Bernardino and San Francisco.
- California Rule 1.5 sets no percentage cap for a crash claim. § 6146's 25%/33% ceilings
  are medical malpractice only and are labeled that way on the page.
