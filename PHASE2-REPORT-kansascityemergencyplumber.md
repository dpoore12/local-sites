## Verified facts used, with the URL each came from

Consent decree / combined sewers
- Original Clean Water Act consent decree signed by a federal judge September 27, 2010; originally a 25-year, $4.7 billion program; Third Amended Consent Decree signed March 3, 2021, extending final compliance from 2035 to 2040 and lowering cost through 2035 to $2.3 billion — https://www.kcsmartsewer.us/approach/third-amended-consent-decree
- Program began in 2010 as the "Overflow Control Program," renamed KC Smart Sewer in 2017; cracked and broken combined-sewer pipes 12 inches in diameter and smaller are being rehabilitated — https://www.kcsmartsewer.us/our-approach
- EPA/DOJ: approximately 1,300 illegal overflows since 2002 including CSOs, SSOs and private property backups; about 6.5 billion gallons of untreated sewage discharged annually; decree targets ~88% capture of typical wet weather flows in the combined system — https://www.epa.gov/enforcement/kansas-city-missouri-clean-water-act-settlement
- Combined sewers serve an area south of the Missouri River of about 56 square miles; six wastewater treatment plants; combined sewers carry sanitary flow plus stormwater during rain — https://www.kcwater.us/about-us/wastewater/
- 98 wet weather overflow control projects; nearly 2,800 miles of sewer pipe (not used on page but verified) — https://www.kcsmartsewer.us/about
- City law prohibits connecting rain gutters, downspouts, basement and driveway sump pumps and yard area drains to the separate sanitary sewer — https://www.kcsmartsewer.us/initiatives/keep-out-the-rain-program

Ownership boundary and lateral procedure
- "Customers are responsible for maintenance and repair of the private residential sewer lateral itself from the point of its connection to the sewer main to the building plumbing." Plumber calls KC Water Investigations and Inspections to verify blockage location; CCTV push-camera documentation must be presented to KC Water; director may authorize repair of a defective portion of a private line in the City right-of-way or easement, conditions including 100% blockage or a "no relief" situation — https://www.kcwater.us/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Repair-and-Maintenance-of-Residential-Service-Laterals.pdf
- KC Water maintains sanitary sewers under streets or in back yards; customer maintains the private lateral; report a backup so the main can be checked, and if the backup persists contact a plumber; cleaning with a three-inch blade suggested every six months where trees or large shrubs are near the lateral — https://www.kcwater.us/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Whats-on-Tap-May-June-2020.pdf
- "The customer is responsible for the pipe that runs from the home or business to the point of connection to the city sewer main." — http://www.kcwater.us/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Whats-on-Tap-Summer-Fall-Sep-Oct-2022_web.pdf

Permits and licensing
- Repair permits required where an existing service is altered, extended, repaired or renewed from the main to one foot inside the property line; work done without a permit has the fee tripled; permits expire after 180 days; backflow permits required where a sewer service line must have an approved backflow prevention assembly for connection to pressurized or surcharged mains; utilities must be notified at least 48 hours before excavation under Missouri law — http://www.kcwater.us/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/2022-Rules-and-Regulations-for-Sewer-Service-Lines-12-22-2021.pdf
- Permits needed before most plumbing work; owner-occupant of a single-family dwelling may pull permits for work performed personally, others must be licensed contractors; two-family, multifamily and commercial permits only to licensed contractors — https://www.kcmo.gov/city-hall/departments/city-planning-development/electrical-plumbing-and-mechanical-permits
- Plumbing permit not required for: repairs to only the working parts of a faucet or valve; clearance of stoppages; repair or replacement of defective fixtures or valves where no piping is altered or extended; replacement and repair of lavatory and sink traps — https://www.kcmo.gov/city-hall/departments/city-planning-development/building-permit-exempt-work
- Licensing path: business license, then a trade Certificate of Qualification (Journeyman Plumber / Master Plumber) applied for by the qualified individual, then the business contractor license, enforced under Article XII of the Kansas City Building and Rehabilitation Code — https://www.kcmo.gov/city-hall/departments/city-planning-development/contractor-licensing
- "The city shall be at no expense in the construction or repairing" of private sewers — https://library.municode.com/mo/kansas_city/codes/code_of_ordinances/379014?nodeId=COORKAMIVOII_CH60SESEDI_ARTIISEDRCOCO_DIV1CO_S60-25PRSE

Landlord-tenant
