## Correction to the brief: the subdivision is 1.5(c)(4)(ii), not 1.5(d)

The task brief said "Colo. RPC 1.5(d)". **That is not where the domestic relations prohibition lives in Colorado.** The operative subdivision, read directly from the rule, is **Colo. RPC 1.5(c)(4)**:

> "(4) No contingent fee agreement may be made (i) for representing a defendant in a criminal case, (ii) in a domestic relations matter, where payment is contingent on the securing of a divorce or upon the amount of maintenance or child support, or property settlement in lieu of such amounts, or (iii) in connection with any case or proceeding where a contingency method of a determination of attorney fees is otherwise prohibited by law."

So the page cites **1.5(c)(4)(ii)**. Sources:
- https://www.cobar.org/For-Members/Opinions-Rules-Statutes/Rules-of-Professional-Conduct/Rule-15-Fees (retrieved 2026-08-23) — used as the on-page source URL
- https://www.cobar.org/Portals/COBAR/Repository/RPC/1.5.pdf?ver=k62_esHYlvulDgrx0dWT_A== (retrieved 2026-08-23) — full rule and comments PDF

## How the narrowness of the prohibition was handled

The page states the two hooks explicitly and nothing broader: the bar reaches (1) payment contingent on **securing the divorce** and (2) payment scaled to the **amount** of maintenance, child support or property settlement in lieu of those. It says in terms that this is not a rule that no part of a family fee may ever depend on how a case ends, and it does not claim that hourly billing is legally required.

The carve-out is quoted in substance from **Comment [6]**: the prohibition "does not preclude a contract for a contingent fee for legal representation in connection with the recovery of post-judgment balances due under support, maintenance or other financial orders because such contracts do not implicate the same policy concerns." That appears as its own fee_row (contingent fee allowed for collecting past-due balances).

## Other fee-rule provisions cited

- **1.5(b)** — basis or rate of the fee, the expenses the client is responsible for, **and the scope of the representation**, communicated **in writing** before or within a reasonable time after commencing; changes also in writing.
- **1.5(f)** — fees are not earned until a benefit is conferred or a service performed; advances of unearned fees are the client's property, held in trust.
- **1.5(g)** — **nonrefundable fees and nonrefundable retainers are prohibited**, as are agreements unreasonably restricting a refund of unearned or unreasonable fees.
- **1.5(h)** — flat fee terms must be in writing (description of services, amount and timing, what is earned on specified events, and what is earned if the representation ends early). Referenced in the body prose.
