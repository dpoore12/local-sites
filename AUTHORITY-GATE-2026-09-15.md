# local-sites authority gate — 2026-09-15

**Question asked:** for our best-shot sites, how many referring domains and what Domain Rating do the sites actually ranking top-10 for the money queries have, and does that number make each site winnable or dead?

**Short answer:** the referring-domain *count* is a junk metric in these niches and should not be used as the gate. Both our sites and the competitors are buried under the same PBN/scraper spam networks. The honest gate is **Domain Rating (which discounts the spam) plus the number of *claimable* local citations**, and by that gate the real local competitors have almost no authority — DR 0 to 9 in towing and mobile-mechanic, DR up to ~39 in the harder verticals. Five of the seven sites are winnable. The lever is not backlinks; it is a live Google Business Profile with reviews plus ~15-20 real citations.

Confidence: **reasoned**, off live Ahrefs SERP + backlink data pulled today. Not mechanical — a SERP is a snapshot of a live, opaque system and can shift.

---

## Read this before the tables — three findings that change what the number means

**1. The refdomain count is polluted for everyone.** I pulled the actual referring-domain list for the #3 site on "towing kalamazoo" (grahamstowing.com, shown by Ahrefs as DR 7 with 208 refdomains). Almost every one of those 208 is a spam link farm: `seo-boosting.shop`, `link-baron-*.store`, `outrank-hq-*`, `rankboostly.shop`, `worldclasspbnlinks.biz`, numeric `*.xyz`, expired-domain networks. Strip the junk and grahamstowing has roughly **15-25 genuine referring domains**, and nearly all of them are *free directory listings anyone can claim*: BBB, D&B, YellowPages, Superpages, DexKnows, Porch, Owler, Hoovers, plus a few towing directories (gotruckgo, towlot, towmile) and a handful of local-radio mentions. DR 7 is the truth; 208 is noise. Wherever you see a low DR next to a big refdomain count in the tables below, read the refdomain count as mostly spam.

**2. Our own sites are already carrying the same spam — verified, not assumed.** All seven priority domains are DR 0. But they are *not* blank:

| our site | DR | referring domains | quality |
|---|---|---|---|
| kalamazootowingpros.com | 0 | ~180 | 100% PBN/scraper spam, 0 legitimate |
| savannahmobilemechanicpros.com | 0 | ~149 | 100% PBN/scraper spam, 0 legitimate |
| (other 5 priority sites) | 0 | not enumerated | same portfolio-wide pattern expected |

This is almost certainly ambient junk that every newly-registered domain attracts from scraper link-networks, not something you did. Two consequences: (a) **do not buy any "SEO backlink" or Fiverr ranking package** — that is exactly this garbage and it does nothing; (b) per Google's own guidance (google-search-essentials skill) these are ignored, not penalised, so a disavow file is not urgent. Our *legitimate* refdomain count is effectively **0**, and that is the number that matters.

**3. The number can't see the real gate.** In every SERP below, the entrenched positions are held by a Google local pack, Yelp/Angi/directories, and — in the winnable niches — Facebook groups and Reddit threads. The local businesses that hold the organic slots rank on **local relevance signals a backlink tool cannot measure**: a claimed Google Business Profile, review count, NAP-consistent citations, a real address in the city, and business age. A site with DR 0 (aztecsmobilemechanic.com, evansvilletowingandrecovery.com, arroyo-plumbing.com) ranks on page 1 in several of these SERPs. That is the proof that authority is not the binding constraint. Trust and local presence are.

## The refdomain target, stated honestly

The literal answer to "what refdomain number unlocks page 1" is **not a big number** — it is roughly **15-20 legitimate referring domains** for the winnable five, and the composition matters far more than the count:

- **Non-negotiable, and not a refdomain at all:** a claimed and populated Google Business Profile with real reviews. This is what the DR-0 competitors have that we don't, and it is what unlocks the local pack. No amount of links substitutes for it.
- **The free citation set anyone can claim (≈10-12 domains):** Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Maps, BBB, D&B, YellowPages, Superpages, DexKnows, MapQuest, Yelp, Porch, plus niche directories per vertical (towing: gotruckgo, towlot, towmile, ifindtowtruck; mobile mechanic: the auto-repair aggregators already ranking).
- **A handful of genuinely local links (3-5):** chamber of commerce, one local-news or local-radio mention, a supplier or partner page. grahamstowing's real edge is exactly this — a few WBCK/WKFR/K1025 radio mentions on top of the free citations.

Hit that ~15-20 set of *real* citations and you match or exceed the legitimate profile of every DR 0-9 competitor in the winnable five. You will still lose without the GBP.

## What I could not verify

- Whether Google is currently penalising the inbound PBN spam on our domains. Ahrefs shows DR 0 and Google's stated policy is to ignore it, but I cannot see inside Google. If any site later shows a manual action in Search Console, revisit a disavow then, not now.
- The freshness ceiling: this is one SERP snapshot on one day from one location. Local SERPs vary by the searcher's exact location and shift over weeks. Treat the verdicts as a 90-day read, not permanent.
- Survivorship: every ranking competitor is a site that worked. The identical build that failed and never ranked is invisible to this analysis.
