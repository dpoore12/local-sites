## The constitutional differentiator

- **Ark. Const. art. 5, § 32** (as amended by Amendment 26): the General Assembly may prescribe
  compensation amounts for employee injury/death, "Provided, that otherwise no law shall be enacted
  limiting the amount to be recovered for injuries resulting in death or for injuries to persons or
  property; and in case of death from such injuries the right of action shall survive."
  https://law.justia.com/constitution/arkansas/article-5/section-32/
- **Ark. Const. art. 5, § 20**: "The State of Arkansas shall never be made defendant in any of her courts."
  https://law.justia.com/constitution/arkansas/article-5/section-20/
- **Amendment 80, § 3**: "The Supreme Court shall prescribe the rules of pleading, practice and procedure
  for all courts; provided these rules shall not abridge, enlarge or modify any substantive right and
  shall preserve the right of trial by jury as declared in this Constitution."
  https://law.justia.com/constitution/arkansas/amendments/amendment-80/
- **Johnson v. Rockwell Automation, Inc. (Ark. 2009)** — Act 649 of 2003 (Civil Justice Reform Act):
  § 16-55-202 (nonparty fault) held unconstitutional (procedural; separation of powers art. 4 § 2 +
  Amendment 80 § 3); § 16-55-212(b) (medical-cost evidence limited to amounts actually paid) held
  unconstitutional as a rule of evidence.
  https://law.justia.com/cases/arkansas/supreme-court/2009/40302.html
- **Bayer CropScience LP v. Schafer (Ark. 2011)**: affirmed that Ark. Code Ann. § 16-55-208, the punitive
  damages limit, is unconstitutional. Compensatory $5,975,605; punitive $42,000,000 (rice farmers, LLRice).
  https://law.justia.com/cases/arkansas/supreme-court/2011/10-1246-0.html
- **Broussard v. St. Edward Mercy Health System, Inc., 2012 Ark. 14**: the words "provided only by a
  medical care provider of the same specialty as the defendant" in § 16-114-206(a)(1)–(2) violate
  separation of powers, Amendment 80 and the courts' inherent authority; language severable, rest of
  the malpractice act unaffected.
  https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Broussard_v._St._Edward_Mercy_Health_System,_Inc./Opinion_of_the_Court
- **2018 Issue 1 (SJR 8)** — Arkansas Supreme Court opinion No. CV-18-749 (Oct. 18, 2018) affirmed removal
  from the ballot: "We conclude that Issue No. 1 does not comply with article 19, section 22 of the
  Arkansas Constitution"; sections not "reasonably germane"; writ of mandamus barred counting, canvassing
  or certifying votes. Ballot title would have capped a contingency fee at 33 1/3% of net recovery,
  capped punitive damages at the greater of $500,000 or 3x compensatory, and capped non-economic damages
  at $500,000 per claimant ($500,000 aggregate for wrongful-death beneficiaries), with General Assembly
  power over Supreme Court rules.
  https://wehco.media.clients.ellingtoncms.com/news/documents/2018/10/18/issue1.pdf
- **Act 28 of 2025 (HB1204, 95th General Assembly), approved February 11, 2025** amended
  **Ark. Code § 16-64-120** to add (b): "Recovery of damages under subsection (a) of this section for past
  necessary medical care, past necessary medical treatment, or past necessary medical services received
  includes only those costs actually paid by or on behalf of the plaintiff or that remain unpaid and for
  which the plaintiff or any third party is legally responsible."
  https://arkleg.state.ar.us/Home/FTPDocument?path=/ACTS/2025R/Public/ACT28.pdf
  Bill text: https://arkleg.state.ar.us/Home/FTPDocument?path=/Bills/2025R/Public/HB1204.pdf
  Commentary confirming act number/date and effect on the collateral source rule:
  https://ualr.edu/socialchange/2026/04/19/the-abrogation-of-the-collateral-source-rule-in-arkansas/
  NOTE: this is the same paid-not-billed rule the court struck in 2009 as an evidence rule; the 2025
  version was written into the damages-recovery statute instead. No decided constitutional challenge found.
- **Collateral source rule, Montgomery Ward & Co. v. Anderson, 334 Ark. (Oct. 22, 1998)**: gratuitous or
  discounted medical services are a collateral source; four exceptions from Evans v. Wilson (rebutting
  premature return to work, attribution to another cause, impeaching testimony that plaintiff paid the
  bills, showing plaintiff kept working). UAMS bill $24,512.45, fifty per cent discount excluded.
  https://law.justia.com/cases/arkansas/supreme-court/1998/97-1456.html

## Fault, deadlines

- **§ 16-64-122** Fault: compare claimant's fault with the fault of the party or parties from whom
  recovery is sought; lesser degree = recover diminished in proportion; "equal to or greater in degree"
  = no recovery. Fault defined to include act, omission, conduct, risk assumed, breach of warranty.
  https://codes.findlaw.com/ar/title-16-practice-procedure-and-courts/ar-code-sect-16-64-122/
- **§ 16-56-105** three-year list, incl. (3) "All actions founded on any contract or liability, expressed
  or implied." https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/title-16/subtitle-5/chapter-56/subchapter-1/section-16-56-105/
  Arkansas Bar Association Statute of Limitations Handbook, "K. NEGLIGENCE ... three (3) years ...
  A.C.A. § 16-56-105": https://higherlogicdownload.s3.amazonaws.com/ARKBAR/1a292642-40fe-477f-b013-f50e48c3c706/UploadedImages/2017_Statute_of_Limitations_Handbook-Eighth_Edition_Final.pdf
- **§ 16-114-203** medical injury: two years; accrual is "the date of the wrongful act complained of and
  no other time"; foreign object one year from discovery or when it should have been discovered,
  whichever is earlier; child 9 or younger — later of 11th birthday or two years, with an outer 19th-birthday
  provision. https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/title-16/subtitle-7/chapter-114/subchapter-2/section-16-114-203/
- **§ 16-116-203** product liability: "commenced within three (3) years after the date on which the death,
  injury, or damage complained of occurs."
  https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/title-16/subtitle-7/chapter-116/subchapter-2/section-16-116-203/
- **§ 16-62-102** wrongful death: three years after death; one year after a nonsuit; beneficiaries incl.
  spouse, children, parents, siblings, in loco parentis; mental anguish "will include grief normally
  associated with the loss of a loved one"; recovery not subject to the decedent's debts.
  https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/title-16/subtitle-5/chapter-62/section-16-62-102/
  Handbook notes Hertlein: where death is caused by medical injury, § 16-114-203 controls.

## Premises / product / medical elements

- **Kroger Co. v. Smith (Ark. App. 2005)**: owner owes invitees ordinary care to keep premises reasonably
  safe; owner is not an insurer; duty to warn covers "hidden dangers, traps, snares, pitfalls and the
  like"; no duty to warn where the condition is known or obvious.
  https://law.justia.com/cases/arkansas/court-of-appeals/2005/ca05-320.html
- **Brookshire's Grocery Co. v. Pierce (Ark. App. 2000)**: plaintiff must show either (1) the substance
  was there through defendant's negligence or (2) it "had been on the premises for such a length of time
  that the defendant knew or reasonably should have known of its presence."
  https://law.justia.com/cases/arkansas/court-of-appeals/2000/ca00-164.html
- **§ 16-116-101** supplier liability: supplier engaged in the business, product supplied in a defective
  condition rendering it unreasonably dangerous, defect a proximate cause; applies without any contractual
  relation; real estate generally not a product, but a good affixed to or installed in real estate is.
  https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/title-16/subtitle-7/chapter-116/subchapter-1/section-16-116-101/
- **§ 16-114-201** definitions: "medical injury" = any adverse consequences arising out of professional
  services, incl. failure to diagnose, want of informed consent, premature abandonment, failure to maintain
  equipment; "medical care provider" list incl. hospital, nursing home, pharmacist, clinic.
  https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/title-16/subtitle-7/chapter-114/subchapter-2/section-16-114-201/
- **§ 16-114-206(a)** burden of proof by expert testimony (locality standard) — the "same specialty"
  words struck by Broussard. **§ 16-114-207(2)**: no witness whose compensation is in any way dependent
  on the outcome may give expert testimony.
  https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/title-16/subtitle-7/chapter-114/subchapter-2/section-16-114-206/
  https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/title-16/subtitle-7/chapter-114/subchapter-2/section-16-114-207/

## Insurance

- **§ 27-19-605(a)**: not less than $25,000 for bodily injury or death of one person, $50,000 for two or
  more persons in one accident, $25,000 for property damage.
  https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/title-27/subtitle-2/chapter-19/subchapter-6/section-27-19-605/
- **§ 23-89-403**: uninsured motorist coverage not less than the § 27-19-605 limits must be provided
  unless a named insured rejects it in writing; where higher liability limits are bought the insurer must
  have available and the agent must offer UM up to those liability limits; written rejection of increased
  limits required for new business on and after January 1, 2000.
  https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/title-23/subtitle-3/chapter-89/subchapter-4/section-23-89-403/
- **§ 23-89-209**: the insured must have the opportunity, rejectable in writing, to buy underinsured
  motorist coverage; UIM at least equal to the § 27-19-605 bodily injury limits; UIM is unavailable unless
  UM was elected; UIM not reduced by the tortfeasor's coverage except above the insured's damages; 30-day
  payment window after notice of a tentative settlement.
  https://codes.findlaw.com/ar/title-23-public-utilities-and-regulated-industries/ar-code-sect-23-89-209/

## State-entity fork (Arkansas State University)

- **§ 19-10-204(a)**: Claims Commission has "exclusive jurisdiction over all claims against the State of
  Arkansas and its several agencies, departments, and institutions," and none over municipalities,
  counties, school districts or other political subdivisions; (b)(2)(A) jurisdiction only over claims
  barred by sovereign immunity. https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/2018/title-19/chapter-10/subchapter-2/section-19-10-204/
- **§ 19-10-209**: a claim must be filed with the Director "within the period allowed by law for the
  commencement of an action for the enforcement of the same type of claim or action."
  https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/title-19/chapter-10/subchapter-2/section-19-10-209/
- **§ 19-10-215**: the commission "shall not pay an award in excess of fifteen thousand dollars ($15,000)";
  above that it refers the claim to the General Assembly for approval and appropriation.
  https://law.justia.com/codes/arkansas/title-19/chapter-10/subchapter-2/section-19-10-215/
- Commission FAQ: no filing fee, no attorney required, no process server needed; agency has 30 days from
  receipt to answer; contested awards held 40 days for reconsideration or appeal under § 19-10-211.
  https://arclaimscommission.arkansas.gov/faq/
- **Bd. of Trustees of Univ. of Ark. v. Andrews, 2018 Ark. 12 (Jan. 18, 2018)**: legislative waiver of
  sovereign immunity in § 11-4-218(e) "is repugnant to article 5, section 20"; reversed and dismissed.
  https://law.justia.com/cases/arkansas/supreme-court/2018/cv-17-168.html

## Local texture

  and nine courthouses. The six counties are Clay, Craighead, Crittenden, Greene, Mississippi, and
  Poinsett." "Three counties have two county seats: Craighead County with county seats in Jonesboro and
  Lake City." Eastern District of Craighead County: "all civil and criminal cases shall be assigned to the
  judge in Division 10." Western District: "All civil cases filed in this District shall be equally
  assigned to the judges in Divisions 1, 2, and 9."
