## Texas statutes (Justia)
- CPRC 71.004: (a) action is for the exclusive benefit of the surviving spouse, children and parents; (b) one or more may bring it for the benefit of all; (c) if none of those individuals has begun the action within three calendar months after the death, the executor or administrator "shall bring and prosecute the action unless requested not to by all those individuals." https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/civil-practice-and-remedies-code/title-4/chapter-71/subchapter-a/section-71-004/
- CPRC 71.003(a): subchapter applies only if the injured individual would have been entitled to bring an action had the individual lived. https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/civil-practice-and-remedies-code/title-4/chapter-71/subchapter-a/section-71-003/
- CPRC 71.021: personal injury action does not abate on death; survives to and in favor of the heirs, legal representatives and estate. https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/civil-practice-and-remedies-code/title-4/chapter-71/subchapter-b/section-71-021/
- CPRC 71.009: exemplary as well as actual damages recoverable where death caused by wilful act or omission or gross negligence. https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/civil-practice-and-remedies-code/title-4/chapter-71/subchapter-a/section-71-009/
- CPRC 71.010: (a) jury may award damages proportionate to the injury from the death; (b) damages divided in shares as found by the jury among those entitled and alive at that time. https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/civil-practice-and-remedies-code/title-4/chapter-71/subchapter-a/section-71-010/
- CPRC 16.003(b): two years, accruing on the death of the injured person. https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/civil-practice-and-remedies-code/title-2/subtitle-b/chapter-16/subchapter-a/section-16-003/ (404 on Justia during run; text confirmed via 16.003 Justia subtitle-b path listing and 16.062 page context — used cautiously)
- CPRC 16.001: legal disability = under 18 or unsound mind; time of disability excluded; no tacking; later disability does not suspend. https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/civil-practice-and-remedies-code/title-2/subtitle-b/chapter-16/subchapter-a/section-16-001/
- CPRC 16.062: death of a person against whom or in whose favor there may be a cause of action suspends limitations for 12 months; if an executor or administrator qualifies first, limitations begins to run at qualification. https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/civil-practice-and-remedies-code/title-2/subtitle-b/chapter-16/subchapter-d/section-16-062/
- CPRC 41.008(b): exemplary damages capped at the greater of (1) two times economic damages plus noneconomic damages found by the jury not to exceed $750,000, or (2) $200,000. https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/civil-practice-and-remedies-code/title-2/subtitle-c/chapter-41/section-41-008/
- Texas Constitution art. XVI, sec. 26: every person, corporation or company committing homicide through wilful act, omission or gross neglect is responsible in exemplary damages to the surviving husband, widow, heirs of his or her body, without regard to any criminal proceeding. https://law.justia.com/constitution/texas/sections/cn001600-002600.html
- CPRC 101.101: (a) notice not later than six months after the incident, reasonably describing the damage or injury, time and place, and incident; (b) city charter/ordinance notice periods permitted by law are ratified; (c) notice requirements do not apply where the unit has actual notice of death, injury or property damage. https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/civil-practice-and-remedies-code/title-5/chapter-101/subchapter-d/section-101-101/
- CPRC 101.023: state $250,000 per person / $500,000 per occurrence; unit of local government other than a municipality $100,000 / $300,000; municipality $250,000 / $500,000. https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/civil-practice-and-remedies-code/title-5/chapter-101/subchapter-b/section-101-023/
- CPRC 101.021: waiver limited to motor-driven vehicle/equipment operation and condition or use of tangible personal or real property. https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/civil-practice-and-remedies-code/title-5/chapter-101/subchapter-b/section-101-021/
- CPRC 101.056: chapter does not apply to failure to perform an act not required by law, or to a discretionary decision/non-decision. https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/civil-practice-and-remedies-code/title-5/chapter-101/subchapter-c/section-101-056/
- CPRC 101.106: (a)/(b) filing against the unit or against an employee is an irrevocable election that immediately and forever bars the other; (f) 30-day amendment mechanic. https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/civil-practice-and-remedies-code/title-5/chapter-101/subchapter-d/section-101-106/
- CPRC 101.102(a),(b): suit in state court in the county where the cause of action arises; pleadings must name the governmental unit. https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/civil-practice-and-remedies-code/title-5/chapter-101/subchapter-d/section-101-102/
- Labor Code 408.182: (a)-(c) spouse/children/grandchildren; (d) if none, equal shares to surviving dependents who are parents, stepparents, siblings or grandparents; (d-1) non-dependent eligible parents, one payment per household, total not more than 104 weeks; (d-2) eligible-parent claim due by the first anniversary of the death. https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/labor-code/title-5/subtitle-a/chapter-408/subchapter-j/section-408-182/

## Exemplary cap in death cases — brief's premise NOT verified
- Tex. Health Enterprises v. Geisler (Tex. App. 1999): court "decline[d] to hold the constitutional provision that provides for punitive damages in wrongful death actions renders a statute, which limits punitive damages in survival actions, unconstitutional," and cited Horizon/CMS Healthcare v. Auld as "approving use of former section 41.007 cap in wrongful death actions." https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/tx-court-of-appeals/1092508.html
- Diamond Shamrock Refining v. Hall (Tex. 2005): the court of appeals had held 41.008 applied and was not unconstitutional as applied; the Supreme Court did not reach the argument, deciding on absence of clear and convincing evidence of gross negligence. https://law.justia.com/cases/texas/supreme-court/2005/2000547.html
- Supreme Court of Texas, No. 24-0045, opinion delivered May 22, 2026: 41.008 caps are computed defendant by defendant, using the economic damages attributable to that defendant's percentage of responsibility. https://www.txcourts.gov/media/1462759/240045.pdf

## City of Austin / Travis County
- Austin City Charter art. XII, sec. 3: written notice, verified by affidavit, to the city council or city manager within forty-five (45) days; must state when, where and how, apparent extent of injury, amount of damages, claimant's residence, and names/addresses of witnesses; failure "shall exonerate, excuse and exempt the city from any liability whatsoever." No act of an officer or employee waives it. https://services.austintexas.gov/edims/document.cfm?id=292113
- City of Austin claims page: submit the letter within 45 days of the incident; Law Department Claims, P.O. Box 1546, Austin TX 78767-1546. https://www.austintexas.gov/services/file-claim
- Austin traffic deaths 2025: 99 fatalities, 301 serious injuries; fatalities up 2% vs 2024; serious injuries down 28%; pedestrian deaths 39 (2024) to 30 (2025); "approximately 65%-75% of Austin's traffic fatalities occur on roads owned by the state." https://www.austintexas.gov/transportation-public-works/mobility-matters/severe-crashes-show-downward-trend-2025
- Vision Zero project performance (Oct 2, 2025): fatalities on city-owned roads relatively flat while fatalities increased on state and other agencies' roadways; 29 major intersection projects -38.1%; 47 protected intersections -42.1%; 138 protected left turns -51.3%. https://www.austintexas.gov/sites/default/files/files/TPW/VisionZero/VZ-Analytics-Austin-Safety-Project-Performance-10.02.25.pdf
- TxDOT 2024 county table: 139 fatal crashes, 155 fatalities in Travis County. https://www.txdot.gov/content/dam/docs/division/trf/crash-records/2024/13.pdf (from site.json local_facts)
- Travis County Medical Examiner FAQ: authority is Code of Criminal Procedure art. 49.25; categories include deaths less than 24 hours after admission, unattended, unnatural, suspicious, suicide, children under six; examination generally takes 12 to 24 hours; autopsy report is public information under the Public Information Act unless an exception applies; office at 7723 Springdale Road. https://www.traviscountytx.gov/medical-examiner/faq
- ME fee schedule (new fees effective January 1, 2025): autopsy report $25 (everyone except JPs, interagency entities, Travis County legal next of kin); investigation of death report $5; certification $50; digital images $30 per CD. https://www.traviscountytx.gov/medical-examiner/fee-schedule
- ME records request: mail/e-mail/in-person, Attn: Record Request, 7723 Springdale Road, Austin 78724; autopsy report request form asks for case number, decedent name, date of death, requester and relationship. https://www.traviscountytx.gov/medical-examiner/records-request and https://www.traviscountytx.gov/images/medical_examiner/docs/request-autopsy-report-form.pdf
- Travis County has two statutory probate courts, Probate Court No. 1 and Probate Court No. 2; the County Clerk's probate division handles filing procedures, court costs, citation issuance and letters. https://www.traviscountytx.gov/probate
- Central Health is Travis County's taxpayer-funded hospital district, created by voters in 2004; CommUnityCare is its clinic network. https://www.centralhealth.net/about/
- DWC death benefits: DWC Form-042 must be submitted to DWC or the carrier within one year of the death; benefits are 75% of average weekly wage; burial benefit request up to $10,000. https://www.tdi.texas.gov/wc/employee/deathben.html and https://www.tdi.texas.gov/forms/dwc/dwc042benclm.pdf
