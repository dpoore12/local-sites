## Limitations (the most misunderstood point)

- General rule: injury to the person = **one year** after the cause of action accrued. [KRS 413.140(1)(a)](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=58050)
- Motor vehicle tort: "An action for tort liability not abolished by KRS 304.39-060 may be commenced not later than **two (2) years** after the injury, or the death, or the **date of issuance of the last basic or added reparation payment** made by any reparation obligor, **whichever later occurs**." A replacement payment does not extend the date beyond the original payment date; "replacement payment" = same amount reissued because the original was lost, stolen or not delivered; the obligor must tell the claimant or the claimant's attorney on written request whether a payment was a replacement. [KRS 304.39-230(6)](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=45816)
- BRB claims: 2 years from loss + knowledge, or 4 years from the accident, whichever earlier; if benefits were paid, 2 years from the last payment. KRS 304.39-230(1)

**Brief accuracy note:** the brief's phrasing ("one-year limit ... runs two years from the last PIP payment") compresses two separate statutes. Verified position: KRS 413.140(1)(a) supplies the one-year general injury period, and KRS 304.39-230(6) supplies two years from injury, death, or the date the last reparation payment issued, whichever is later, for a motor vehicle tort claim not abolished by KRS 304.39-060.

## Fault, coverage, minimums

- Allocation of fault: jury answers interrogatories on damages disregarding contributory fault and on the percentage of total fault allocated to each claimant, defendant, third-party defendant and released person; court states each party's equitable share by percentage; a release reduces the claim by the released person's equitable share. No percentage bars recovery. [KRS 411.182](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=17782)
- Minimum liability: split limits **$25,000** per person / **$50,000** per accident bodily injury plus **$25,000** property damage, or a single limit of **$60,000**; plus basic reparation benefits. [KRS 304.39-110](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=46758)
- UM: must be in every liability policy on a vehicle registered or principally garaged in Kentucky, in the KRS 304.39-110 limits, unless a named insured rejects **in writing**; rejection valid for all insureds; after rejection, coverage need not be in a renewal unless a named insured requests it in writing. "Uninsured motor vehicle" includes an insured vehicle whose liability carrier is insolvent (insolvency within one year of the accident), one insured below the KRS 304.39-110 limits, and one where the liability insurer denies coverage. [KRS 304.20-020](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=45815)
- UIM: "Every insurer shall make available, **upon request**" — underinsured motorist = a party with liability coverage less than a judgment recovered against them. Settlement that would not fully satisfy the claim requires **written notice by certified or registered mail** to every UIM carrier; carrier has **30 days** to consent or preserve subrogation; consent or no response lets the claimant release the liability carrier without prejudice; refusal obliges the UIM carrier to pay the amount of the written liability offer within 30 days. [KRS 304.39-320](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=54466)
- Assigned claims plan available where BRB insurance is inapplicable, cannot be identified, is inadequate through the obligor's insolvency, or a claim was rejected for a reason other than non-entitlement; an owner who was required to carry security and did not cannot use the plan. [KRS 304.39-160](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=30045)
- BRB payment timing: payable monthly as loss accrues; overdue if unpaid 30 days after reasonable proof (or 15 days after a permitted accumulation period of up to 31 days); a part of a claim totaling **$100** or more can be overdue by itself; overdue payments bear **12%** interest, **18%** if delay was without reasonable foundation; provider must submit a charge within **180 days**. [KRS 304.39-210](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=57856)
- Unfair claims settlement practices, 17 listed items including failing to act reasonably promptly on communications, refusing to pay without a reasonable investigation, failing to affirm or deny coverage within a reasonable time after proof of loss, not attempting in good faith to effectuate prompt fair and equitable settlement where liability is reasonably clear, compelling insureds to litigate by offering substantially less than amounts ultimately recovered, and failing to give a reasonable explanation of the basis for a denial or compromise offer. [KRS 304.12-230](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=17037)

## Crash reporting and reports

- Immediate notification to a law enforcement officer having jurisdiction after a crash with fatal or nonfatal personal injury or damage rendering a vehicle inoperable; if the operator fails or cannot, the duty falls on the owner or any occupant; officer investigates and files a written report. [KRS 189.635(3)](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=54793)
- Property damage over **$500** with no officer investigation: driver files a written report with the department within **10 days**. KRS 189.635(5)(a)
- Officers report through the **E-CRASH** system in the Kentucky Open Portal Solution; the originating agency stays responsible for providing the report to authorized parties; copies cost **$5** on paper and **$10** through the KSP website. [502 KAR 15:010](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/kar/titles/502/015/010/)
- KSP asks people to allow **10 days** for an officer's report to appear; civilian collision reports are retained **up to two years** and released to an involved party by open records request. [KSP Civilian Collision Reports](https://kentuckystatepolice.ky.gov/civilian-collision-reports/)

## Federal trucking rules (49 CFR, 2025 edition via govinfo)

- Accident register kept **3 years** after each accident, listing date, city or town and state, driver name, number of injuries, number of fatalities, and whether hazardous materials other than spilled fuel were released, plus copies of all accident reports required by states or insurers; carriers must make all records and information pertaining to an accident available to authorized representatives. [49 CFR 390.15](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title49-vol5/xml/CFR-2025-title49-vol5-sec390-15.xml)
- Post-accident testing: alcohol and controlled substances testing as soon as practicable after a fatality, or where the driver is cited within **8 hours** (alcohol) or **32 hours** (controlled substances) and the crash caused bodily injury with immediate treatment away from the scene or disabling damage requiring tow-away; a record of explanation required if the alcohol test is not given within **2 hours**; attempts cease at **8 hours** for alcohol and **32 hours** for drugs; law enforcement breath, blood or urine results can satisfy the rule if the employer obtains them; a driver must remain readily available or may be deemed to have refused. [49 CFR 382.303](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title49-vol5/xml/CFR-2025-title49-vol5-sec382-303.xml)
- Records of duty status and supporting documents retained **not less than 6 months** from receipt; the driver keeps the previous **7 consecutive days** in the vehicle; the driver submits the record to the carrier within **13 days** of the 24-hour period. [49 CFR 395.8](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title49-vol5/xml/CFR-2025-title49-vol5-sec395-8.xml)

## Fee rules

- No percentage ceiling on an injury contingent fee. SCR 3.130(1.5)(a) bars an unreasonable fee and an unreasonable amount for expenses and lists eight factors; (c) requires a writing signed by the client stating the method, the percentages at settlement, trial and appeal, which expenses are deducted and whether before or after the fee, a clear notice of expenses owed whether or not the client prevails, and a written closing statement of the outcome and remittance; (d) bars a result-based fee for a criminal defendant and in divorce, alimony, support or property matters apart from liquidated arrearages. [Ky. SCR 3.130(1.5)](https://www.kycourts.gov/Courts/Supreme-Court/Supreme%20Court%20Orders/200905.pdf)
- Workers' compensation is Kentucky's one numeric schedule: 20% of the first $25,000, 15% of the next $25,000, 10% of the remainder, maximum **$18,000**, ALJ approval required, approval motion within 30 days of finality. [KRS 342.320](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=47622)
