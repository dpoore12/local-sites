## Accuracy handling

- **No contingency cap stated.** California uses an unconscionability standard for ordinary injury work; the page says this explicitly and calls a "California limit" on a general injury claim a custom, not a statute.
- **§6146 labeled.** The 25%/33% figures appear only under a heading identifying them as belonging to medical professional negligence, with an explicit statement that they do not apply to a highway, pedestrian or premises claim.
- Subdivisions were read on the primary sources listed above, not copied from the brief or another site.
