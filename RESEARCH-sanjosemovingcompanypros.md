## Regulator (brief's correction confirmed)
- Household movers were regulated by the CPUC until SB 19 (Hill, Ch. 421, Stats. 2017), signed October 2, 2017. Jurisdiction transferred to BEARHFTI on July 1, 2018; SB 1483 renamed it the Bureau of Household Goods and Services (BHGS), Department of Consumer Affairs, effective January 1, 2019.
  - https://bhgs.dca.ca.gov/about_us/index.shtml
  - https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/industries-and-topics/transportation/transfer-of-authority-of-household-goods
- BPC 19237(a)(1): intrastate work requires a bureau permit. 19237(a)(2): moves into or out of California require valid FMCSA operating authority. 19237(b): a mover operating in violation cannot enforce a security interest or sue the consignor/consignee. Operative date July 1, 2018 (BPC 19294).
  - https://bhgs.dca.ca.gov/laws/hhm_law_book_eff_1_1_26.pdf
- License types: "General Household Mover" (in-state, or in-state plus into/out of CA) vs "Interstate Mover" (only into/out of CA). Permit filing fee $500; transfer $150; quarterly report fee $15 (incl. $5 CHP safety fee); liability combined single limit not less than $600,000; cargo $20,000 per shipment; 25% penalty for late quarterly report.
  - https://bhgs.dca.ca.gov/licensee/hhm_faqs.shtml
- Penalties: BPC 19279.1 citation up to $5,000 per violation for operating without a valid permit (16 CCR 2943 range $250–$5,000); 19279 up to $500; 19279.3 advertising without a permit is a misdemeanor, fine up to $2,500; 19280 falsifying permit status up to $2,500 per day; 19277(b) up to $10,000 and/or one year for each violation of 19237(a).
  - https://bhgs.dca.ca.gov/laws/hhm_law_book_eff_1_1_26.pdf
  - https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/california/Ca-Professional-and-Vocational-Regulations-Code-SS-2943
- Enforcement: DCA/BHGS release 7/9/2026 — Bay Area and Southern California sweep produced 30 business citations totaling $110,230, including two federal citations at $39,615 each; run with FMCSA, which supplies tips, licensing information and federal citation authority.
  - https://www.dca.ca.gov/newsroom/2026/bhgs/20260709_bhgs_targets_unlic_movers.pdf
- Earlier sweep: two-day statewide operation, 40 household movers inspected across 8 counties (12/23/2025).
  - https://www.dca.ca.gov/newsroom/2025/bhgs/20251223_statewide_unlicensed_movers.pdf
- Consumer rules: documents must be supplied when hired 3+ days ahead; estimates only in writing after visual inspection; verbal/internet estimates are illegal and may be unenforceable; a Change Order cannot be used because a mover underestimated; loss/damage claims in writing within 9 months of delivery.
  - https://bhgs.dca.ca.gov/consumers/movers.shtml

## Maximum Rate Tariff 4 (effective 1/1/2025) — https://bhgs.dca.ca.gov/forms_pubs/20240101_maxtariff.pdf
- Item 320, shipments 100 miles or less, Territory A straight time: $142.01 (vehicle + 1), $228.01 (vehicle + 2), $81.80 each additional person. Time-and-a-half: $181.84 / $307.65 / $119.31. Double time: $228.01 / $387.55 / $156.98.
- Item 36: billable time = loading + unloading + double the driving time origin to destination. Exception 1: multiple trips — double drive time on the first trip, actual thereafter. Exception 2: two or more shipments on one truck — 25 minutes total driving time each. Fractional hours: 0–7 min omitted; 7–22 = 1/4; 22–37 = 1/2; 37–52 = 3/4; 52–60 = full hour. Start/end times recorded to the minute.
- Item 128: Agreement for Moving Services delivered no less than three days before the day of the move; Not to Exceed Price and mover's final signature added no later than the day of the move but before any service; must contain mover's CAL-T number; para 5 — mover must relinquish the shipment on payment of the Not to Exceed Price plus post-agreement change orders; overcollection refunded within 10 days.
- Item 130: the Not to Exceed amount shall not be preprinted, must be just and reasonable and set in good faith on the specific circumstances.
- Item 120: Change Order for Services, signed by both before performance; itemizes added articles and services.
- Item 104: charges may be collected before possession is relinquished; credit card counts as cash payment; mover must unload upon payment of the Not to Exceed Price plus valid change orders whether or not it believes more is owed; freight bills presented within 7 calendar days after delivery; lien extinguished on that payment; no lien attaches to food, medicine, medical devices, items to treat or assist a person with a disability, or items used for the care of a minor child.
- Item 465: a mover cannot refuse to release goods once the Not to Exceed amount plus agreed additions is paid; an unlicensed mover has no right to withhold goods for any reason.
- Item 88: CAL-T number required on stationery, shipping documents and related forms (para 3) and in printed advertising including classified listings (para 7).
- Item 136: rates are based on a declared value of $0.60 per pound per article; default maximum liability $20,000 of actual cash value unless another value is declared in the shipper's own handwriting; Actual Cash Value protection $1.09 per $100 of declared value; Full Value protection $2.14 per $100; with a $250 deductible $0.65 per $100; with $500 deductible $0.37 per $100; storage-in-transit valuation $0.16 (ACV) and $0.37 (full value) per $100; no deductible applies to documented loss or non-delivery.
- Item 470 booklet: basic 60-cent protection example — a 20-pound article yields $12.00.
- Item 340 (Territory A): dish-pack/barrel container $55.55, packing $50.01, unpacking $20.92; carton under 3 cu ft $13.72 / $12.03; 3 cu ft $20.69 / $18.20; 4.5 cu ft $24.53 / $22.06; 6 cu ft $28.05 / $25.28; 6.5 cu ft $32.02 / $28.78; mirror/painting/glass-top corrugated container $48.32 / $43.53; mattress carton 39x80 $28.34; mattress cover $14.64. Hourly packing/unpacking per person: $91.12 straight time, $134.28 time-and-a-half, $177.02 double time. Note 6: container charge includes dividers, paper, tape and labels, no separate materials charge. Note 8: no charge for wardrobes on shipments moving at Item 320 hourly rates. Overtime multipliers on per-container rates: 1.47 and 1.94.
- Items 108/116: cubic feet multiplied by seven pounds per cubic foot to approximate weight on weight-rated shipments.

## FMCSA — https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/sites/fmcsa.dot.gov/files/2023-10/FMCSA_R%26R_Handbook_Web_v1.pdf
- Claim window: 9 months from delivery (from the date the shipment should have been delivered if the whole shipment is lost); mover has 30 days to acknowledge and 120 days to give a disposition, with possible 60-day extensions on written notice.
- Bill of lading must include names, telephone numbers, addresses and USDOT numbers of participating motor carriers when known; broker vs mover distinction; carrier search at http://li-public.fmcsa.dot.gov and protectyourmove.gov.
- Binding estimate: delivery on payment of 100% of the estimate plus additional-service and impracticable-operations charges not exceeding 15% of all other charges due at delivery. Non-binding: no more than 110% may be required at delivery, remaining charges billed after 30 days. Failure to relinquish after that payment is holding the shipment hostage in violation of federal law.
- Impracticable operations: unforeseen destination conditions including elevators, stairs, or required parking permits may be billed after 30 days from delivery.

## Could not verify / brief corrections
- No primary source found for an "MTR" permit number format. Maximum Rate Tariff 4 Item 88 and the BHGS consumer booklet specify the Bureau-issued CAL-T ("T") number only. site.json also says CAL-T, so the copy uses CAL-T.
- Maximum Rate Tariff 4 sets no universal minimum charge or minimum number of hours. Item 128 and Item 132 only require any minimum hours, weights or materials to be stated on the paperwork; the Item 470 booklet says some movers may impose minimums. Copy says so rather than naming a state minimum.
- No City of San José page found stating an elevator-reservation or certificate-of-insurance requirement; those are private property rules, so the copy treats them as building-imposed and anchors the cost consequence in the FMCSA impracticable-operations rule.
- Santa Clara County's Territory A classification under Item 210 was taken from site.json (which is the authority) rather than re-verified line by line in the tariff.
