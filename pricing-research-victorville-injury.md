## Notes and limits

- San Bernardino is one of only three California counties (with Riverside and
  San Francisco) whose first-paper fees carry a local courthouse-construction surcharge,
  which is why the limited civil totals here are $240 and $380 rather than the statewide
  $225 and $370. Confirmed both on the county court's own schedule and in the appendix of
  the statewide schedule effective Jan. 1, 2026
  (https://courts.ca.gov/system/files/file/statewide-civil-fee-schedule-eff-01012026.pdf).
- The unlimited civil first-paper total stays $435 in San Bernardino: the schedule shows
  the $35 surcharge offset in that tier.
- California Rule 1.5 sets no percentage cap for auto injury work. § 6146's 25%/33%
  ceilings are medical malpractice only and are labeled that way.
