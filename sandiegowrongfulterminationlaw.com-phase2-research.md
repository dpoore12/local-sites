## Agency identity and the administrative clock
- DFEH renamed to the Civil Rights Department (CRD) effective July 1, 2022 by SB 189 (Ch. 48, Stats. 2022): https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/deptnamechange/ and https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2022/11/2022-Legislative-Summary.pdf
- CRD complaint process: "In employment cases, you must submit an intake form to CRD within three years of the date you were last harmed." Non-employment: one year. Appeal of closure: 10 calendar days. https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/complaintprocess/
- Gov. Code 12960(b): filing a complaint means filing an intake form, and the verified complaint relates back to it. 12960(e)(5): three years for Article 1 (12940 et seq.) violations. 12960(e)(6)(A): up to 90 extra days for late-discovered facts. 12960(f)(1)(B): tolling of the civil deadline until one year after CRD closes without suing. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=12960.&lawCode=GOV
- AB 9 (2019) extended the FEHA administrative window from one year to three: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2019/10/10/governor-newsom-signs-worker-protection-bills-addressing-sexual-harassment-wages-and-health-protections/ and bill text https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=201920200AB9
- Gov. Code 12965(c)(1)(A): if CRD does not sue within 150 days, it notifies that a right-to-sue notice issues on request; if not requested, it issues on completion of investigation and not later than one year after filing. 12965(c)(1)(C): the notice states the civil action must be brought within one year from the date of the notice. 12965(a)(5)(A): two years for group/class complaints. 12965(e)/(f): tolling where the charge is deferred between EEOC and CRD. 12965(c)(6): prevailing-party fees, costs, expert fees; prevailing defendant only on frivolous/unreasonable/groundless finding. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=12965.&lawCode=GOV
- EEOC: 180 days baseline, 300 days where a state agency enforces a parallel law; federal-sector 45 days to contact an EEO counselor. https://www.eeoc.gov/time-limits-filing-charge
- EEOC: "Once you receive a Notice of Right to Sue, you must file your lawsuit within 90 days." https://www.eeoc.gov/filing-lawsuit
- CRD/EEOC work sharing and dual filing; "You must file a complaint with CRD even if you wish to file a case directly in court"; immediate right-to-sue available on request. CFRA/PDL details, 5-employee threshold. https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/employment/

## At-will and the public policy tort
- Labor Code 2922: "An employment, having no specified term, may be terminated at the will of either party on notice to the other. Employment for a specified term means an employment for a period greater than one month." https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&division=3.&title=&part=&chapter=2.&article=4.
- Tameny v. Atlantic Richfield Co. (1980): employer's authority "does not include the right to demand that the employee commit a criminal act to further its interests"; discharged employee "may maintain a tort action for wrongful discharge"; also cites Labor Code 2856 (obedience except where impossible or unlawful). https://law.justia.com/cases/california/supreme-court/3d/27/167.html

## Whistleblower and wage retaliation
- Labor Code 1102.5(a),(b): protection applies where the employee has reasonable cause to believe the information discloses a violation, "regardless of whether disclosing the information is part of the employee's job duties." (f)(1): civil penalty up to $10,000 per employee per violation, awarded to the employee. (j): court may award fees to a successful plaintiff. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=1102.5
- Labor Code 1102.6: once a protected activity is shown by a preponderance to be a contributing factor, the employer bears the burden of showing by clear and convincing evidence the action would have occurred for legitimate, independent reasons. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=1102.6
- Labor Code 98.6(b)(1): rebuttable presumption in the employee's favor if the prohibited action occurs within 90 days of the protected activity; reinstatement and reimbursement of lost wages and work benefits. (b)(3): civil penalty up to $10,000 per employee per violation. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=98.6

## PAGA
- LWDA/DIR PAGA portal: $75 filing fee for a new PAGA claim notice and for an employer response; court complaint filed with LWDA within 10 days of filing suit; judgment/order within 10 days of entry; portal submission is not service of process. https://www.dir.ca.gov/Private-Attorneys-General-Act/Private-Attorneys-General-Act.html
- The commonly cited 65-day LWDA review window appears only in secondary sources here, so it was written around.

## Evidence, records, final pay
- Labor Code 1198.5(b)(1): personnel records available not later than 30 calendar days from a written request, extendable to 35 by written agreement; (d) one request per year for former employees; (c)(1) records kept 3 years after termination; (k) $750 penalty; (n) inspection right ceases while a lawsuit relating to a personnel matter is pending in the trial court; (p) 50-request monthly limit per representative. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=1198.5
- DLSE: discharge means all wages including accrued vacation immediately (LC 201); quit with 72 hours' notice, paid at quitting; without notice, within 72 hours (LC 202); payroll records within 21 calendar days, $750 penalty. https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/faq_paydays.htm
- DLSE waiting time (LC 203): daily rate times days unpaid, maximum 30 calendar days, weekends and holidays counted; payment or commencement of a court action stops accrual; a DLSE claim does not; good faith dispute prevents the penalty; worked example $2,500/month = $576.92/week = $115.38/day, 30 days = $3,461.54. https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/FAQ_WaitingTimePenalty.htm

## FEHA substance
- Gov. Code 12940(a) categories; 12940(m)(1) reasonable accommodation and undue hardship per 12926(u); 12940(m)(2) retaliation for requesting accommodation "regardless of whether the request was granted"; 12940(n) "timely, good faith, interactive process"; 12945.2(a) CFRA 12 months service, 1,250 hours, 12 workweeks; 12945.2(b)(4)(A) five or more employees; 12945.2(o),(p) concurrency with FMLA and the pregnancy-disability exception; 12950.1(a)(1) and 12952(a) five-employee thresholds. https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&division=3.&title=2.&part=2.8.&chapter=6.&article=1.
- CRD: PDL up to four months, four months equals 17 1/3 weeks; CFRA counted separately from PDL. https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/employment/ and https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2023/01/CFRA-and-Pregnancy-Leave_ENG.pdf
- FMLA: 50 or more employees for at least 20 workweeks; worksite with 50 employees within 75 miles; 1,250 hours; 12 months of employment; 12 workweeks (26 for military caregiver). https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fmla/faq

## Severance and releases
- Gov. Code 12964.5(b)(1)(A): unlawful for a separation agreement to include a provision prohibiting disclosure of information about unlawful acts in the workplace. (b)(4): employer must notify the employee of the right to consult an attorney and give at least five business days. https://law.justia.com/codes/california/code-gov/title-2/division-3/part-2-8/chapter-7/article-1/section-12964-5/
- 29 U.S.C. 626(f)(1)(F): at least 21 days to consider, 45 days for a group exit incentive program; (G) 7-day revocation; (H) job titles and ages disclosure for group programs; (f)(3) burden on the party asserting the waiver; (f)(4) no waiver may interfere with filing an EEOC charge. https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/29/626
- Civil Code 1542: a general release does not extend to unknown or unsuspected claims that would have materially affected the settlement. https://law.justia.com/codes/california/code-civ/division-3/part-1/title-4/chapter-6/section-1542/
